Sacks, picks & 'a bag of tricks!' - NFL best plays

The NFL's brightest stars lit up week 10 of the 2026 season with some astonishing plays on Sunday.

The likes of Brock Purdy, Christian McCaffrey, Patrick Mahomes, Jahmyr Gibbs and Ja'Marr Chase showcased their skills with some explosive action to help their teams to victory.

The San Francisco 49ers produced a historic display in Dallas while there were also big wins for the Kansas City Chiefs, Detroit Lions and Cincinnati Bengals.

It means the 49ers and Buffalo Bills continue to share the league's best record (8-1), while five teams moved to 7-2, including the in-form Jacksonville Jaguars.

Christian McCaffrey scored his final touchdown just as the San Francisco 49ers' general manager John Lynch made his way to the sideline in the fourth quarter

The San Francisco 49ers and Dallas Cowboys have a fierce rivalry stretching back to the 1990s and they delivered another classic with a shootout in Texas.

Brock Purdy showed why he is among the leading contenders for the Most Valuable Player award as the 49ers won 46-40, racking up 584 yards of offence - the most Dallas have ever allowed at home.

San Francisco had three touchdowns of more than 65 yards, while Purdy threw for a career-high 389 yards and three touchdowns. The quarterback even blocked for one of the 49ers' other three scores, a 70-yard run by Deebo Samuel on a reverse trick play.

The highlights were two Purdy passes to Christian McCaffrey. The first saw Purdy show remarkable poise as he scrambled out of the pocket before finding McCaffrey for a 74-yard score, while the second was a perfectly-executed play over the middle that saw McCaffrey sprint in untouched from 66 yards.

Dual-threat running back McCaffrey finished with three scores and 223 total yards, including a career-high 161 receiving yards.

Purdy's MVP rival Josh Allen had a relatively quiet game as the Buffalo Bills won 31-12 at the New York Jets, while the Jacksonville Jaguars won 40-23 at the Tennessee Titans.

Including the end of last season, the Jaguars have won 15 of their past 17 regular-season games and must be considered Super Bowl contenders, especially after bringing in three key defensive players before the trade deadline on Tuesday.

Joe Burrow became the new MVP favourite last week and continued his superb form as the Cincinnati Bengals romped to a 38-18 win in their divisional match-up against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

New Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino was among the crowd as Burrow was involved in four touchdowns for the fifth straight game, tying an NFL record.

The Bengals quarterback had 300 passing yards before half-time and finished with 409. Most of them were to Ja'Marr Chase, who claimed 231 yards from just 11 catches for the 7-2 Bengals.

Their divisional rivals, the Cleveland Browns, pulled off another comeback as they recovered from 17-0 down to beat the Houston Texans 27-24.

The Browns have won their opening four home games for the first time since 1963 and are now 6-4.

Quarterback Deshaun Watson led the Browns to a game-winning drive in the fourth quarter of all six of those wins and he said this one was "definitely special" as it came against his former team.

Amon-Ra St Brown met Bayern Munich forward Jamal Musiala at the Allianz Arena and waved the German flag before and after Sunday's game

Munich staged the penultimate international game of the season and just playing in Germany was a "dream come true" for the Detroit Lions' wide receiver Amon-Ra St Brown.

The 27-year-old's mother is German, and he and his two brothers speak the language fluently and have been regular visitors to their mother's homeland.

St Brown, who arrived wearing lederhosen and waved the German flag as he ran on to the field, scored two touchdowns as the Lions charged to a 35-10 win over the New England Patriots.

Yet St Brown was outshone by team-mate Jahmyr Gibbs, who had 99 receiving yards, 153 rushing yards and two touchdowns, taking him to 62 overall - a new NFL record in a player's first four seasons.

The Patriots lost Drake Maye to an ankle injury when he was sacked on the game's opening drive by Aidan Hutchinson, who injured his knee as he celebrated, and the Lions fear it is a significant injury.

Detroit, the Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears are now all 7-2 in the ultra-competitive NFC North, with the Vikings bouncing back after suffering back-to-back defeats.

Kirk Cousins returned to Minnesota before the trade deadline on Tuesday and the veteran quarterback led them to a 30-10 win at their other divisional rival, the 6-4 Green Bay Packers, with their defence claiming eight sacks - the most in a single game this season.

The Las Vegas Raiders allowed Cousins to rejoin Minnesota as they felt Fernando Mendoza, this year's first overall draft pick, was ready to become their starting quarterback.

Mendoza's first start came against the Seattle Seahawks, the Super Bowl champions, and he managed to run in a 10-yard touchdown on the Raiders' opening drive.

But the Raiders' offence then struggled and Mendoza was sacked six times as the Seahawks won 21-13.

"It's good to get punched in the face because now it's a great opportunity to grow and learn," said Mendoza.

Seattle quarterback Drew Lock made two big touchdown passes on the run, one to Cooper Kupp and the other a 71-yard score by Jaxon Smith-Njigba.

The Kansas City Chiefs' run game has caught the eye so far this season, in particular new signing Kenneth Walker, but he was out injured on Sunday as Patrick Mahomes reminded everyone he has one of the best arms in the business.

For the first time since 2022, the three-time Super Bowl winner threw for five touchdowns, plus 348 yards, as Kansas City won 35-13 at the Atlanta Falcons.

Running back Emmett Johnson stepped up in the absence of Walker to claim 101 rushing yards, 70 receiving yards and two touchdowns.

Mahomes made explosive plays to Cyrus Allen and Andrew Armstrong for their first NFL scores, while fellow wide receiver Josh Downs scored on his debut after the Chiefs traded for him on deadline day.

That took Mahomes to 332 career touchdown passes, including the play-offs, putting the 31-year-old top of the all-time list for the most in a player's first 10 seasons.

The Chiefs are 7-2 and will need their receiving corps to ease the burden on Walker during a tough five-game stretch after next week.
