According to new developments, Harrison Glynne Jones was described as a "cheeky chappy, full of character"

A six-year-old boy who died when he was struck by a van outside of his home has been named as Harrison Glynne Jones.

In a tribute on a fund-raising page launched to support his family, he was described as a "cheeky chappy, full of character with a personality that could light up a room", and who had been "loved more deeply than words could ever express".

Officers were called to reports of an incident involving a white Ford Transit Tipper and a child on Mill Road in Llanfairfechan, Conwy county, after 17:00 BST on Thursday, where Harrison was pronounced dead at the scene.

North Wales Police said a 39-year-old man had been arrested and later released under investigation.

"Our family's world was changed forever when Kayleigh and Daryl's beautiful six-year-old son Harrison was tragically hit by a vehicle outside of his own home and sadly passed away at the scene in front of his family," Jones said.

"Harrison's mum and dad are facing the unimaginable task of saying goodbye to their precious little boy, while trying to somehow navigate life without him so we have set up this fundraiser to help Harrison's parents through the difficult weeks and months ahead."
