Former three-weight world champion Jorge Linares remains in the custody of US immigration authorities in Texas and is facing deportation.

The 41-year-old Venezuelan was detained on Sunday when US Border Force and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers arrested him at El Paso International Airport.

"On October 26, 2023, he entered the country with permission to stay for a temporary period, but in defiance of our nation's laws, he overstayed his welcome. He will remain in ICE custody pending removal proceedings," said an ICE spokesperson.

"To be clear, a pending application or work authorisation does not confer legal status in the United States."

Linares is based in Miami and has fought more than 10 times in America.

The immigration law firm Wilner & O'Reilly are representing Linares and they denied their client was in the US illegally.

"Mr Linares is authorised to be in the United States and he has cooperated fully with immigration authorities," said a Wilner & O'Reilly statement.

"We are in communication with ICE and are working to resolve this matter as quickly as possible. Immigration matters follow established procedures, and those procedures take time.

"We are confident in Mr. Linares's position and ask his fans, the press and the public for patience in the meantime."

Ex-world champion Linares detained by ICE in Texas

Linares posted a message on social media on Sunday saying that "ICE has me at the Texas border crossing. This is crazy".

He won 47 of his 56 bouts and claimed world titles at featherweight, super-featherweight and lightweight in a distinguished career which started in 2002.

He retired after being beaten by Britain's Jack Catterall in a light-welterweight bout in October 2023 in his last fight.
