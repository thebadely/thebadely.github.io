Let's dive into the details: Isiah Noel-Williams has been remanded in custody

League Two footballer Isiah Noel-Williams has been charged with rape.

Noel-Williams, who plays for League Two side Barnet, has been charged with three counts of rape and one count of sexual assault by penetration, Hertfordshire Police said.

The 23-year-old, of High Oaks, St Albans, was remanded in custody following a hearing at Hatfield Magistrates' Court on Saturday. 

In a statement, the club said it was "co-operating fully with authorities" and would determine further action "in due course".

Noel-Williams has also been charged with one count of engaging in sexual communication with a child, and one count of supplying a controlled (Class B) drug, police added.

In a statement, the London club said: "Barnet FC can confirm that first-team player Isiah Noel-Williams has been charged and remanded in custody as part of a police investigation by Hertfordshire Constabulary.

"The club is co-operating fully with the authorities and will determine any further action in due course.

"As this is now a matter for the courts, the club will not be making any further comment."

Noel-Williams, who previously played for Hemel Hempstead Town, Kettering Town, Matlock Town, Harrow Borough and Cheshunt, joined Barnet in June.
