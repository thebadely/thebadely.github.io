Let's dive into the details: Dafne Keen plays Greek goddess Artemis in season three, while Walker Scobell has starred as Percy Jackson since 2023

Percy Jackson is no ordinary teenager - and neither is the actor tasked with bringing the character to life. 

As the son of Greek god Poseidon, Percy has demigod status in the hit Disney series and while Walker Scobell doesn't have his powers, that hasn't made the actor's career any less special. 

The TV series, based on the novels by Rick Riordan, sets figures from Greek mythology and their children in the modern day and returns on Friday for its third instalment - The Titan's Curse.

Scobell was 14 when he made his debut as Percy in 2023. Now 17, the US actor says growing up in the spotlight has been an adventure in itself.

"There's an upside for each downside," he tells BBC Newsbeat.

"It's a lot easier to get a table at restaurants now and you get to make people's days who are huge Percy Jackson fans just by talking to them for a few minutes. And I think that's a special part of it."

But it's not the only part. 

Earlier this year, Scobell posted on Instagram that he would not be attending his school prom because of death threats sent to girls who live near him.

His co-star Aryan Simhadri, who plays Percy's best friend Grover Underwood, has also mentioned uncomfortable meetings with fans.

In an interview with Entertainment Weekly, external, he spoke about how "about 40 drunk college girls" recognised him and one tried to put her hand in his back pocket. 

Since then, he says "fame hasn't been too bad, I've been able to have a normal life because of my parents". 

"They really make sure when I'm not filming, I'm grounded and I'm planning for my future instead of being enamoured with the aspects of fame."

Aryan Simhadri has previously spoken about an uncomfortable interaction with drunk fans

The new series is the first time The Titan's Curse has been adapted on screen.

An earlier film adaptation starring Logan Lerman only reached The Sea of Monsters, the second novel in the five-part series.

So the cast say they're in uncharted territory for the project - which sees Percy and Grover trying to rescue Annabeth from Kronos's forces.

"We can make the season in our own weird way because there's nothing you can compare it to, only the book," says Scobell. 

"So it's the first time we're seeing any of this stuff."

Fans have been speculating online what to expect, especially after a shock diversion from the novel at the end of the last series. 

"I think both book readers and non-book readers are going to be excited and appreciate the tone shift," Simhadri says. 

"It changes how serious the world is, it's really the start of a new show, not to be dramatic."

It's caused a split in opinion from the fanbase, with one fan noting "they are going to stick to the books", but another saying "this is a twist they can get down with".

Leah Sava Jeffries, who plays Annabeth, says Greek mythology offers "stories for everyone"

The first series was hugely successful with the opening episode of it reaching 13.3 million viewers, according to entertainment data company Luminate.

That dipped to just over 11 million for the second series and the cast is hoping a fresh take on the story will keep fans interested.

"I personally really like seeing people theorise about what's going to make it," says Simhadri.

"People who have read the books like talking about how they think we're going to adapt it, I love reading those."

It's been a big year for Greek mythology adaptations, with Christopher Nolan's blockbuster The Odyssey getting huge box office numbers and critical acclaim. 

Leah Sava Jeffries, who plays Athena's daughter Annabeth Chase, says its popularity could be down to how people connect the ancient stories to their own lives.

"There's stories for everyone," she says.

"You can have a family where the dad is obsessed with The Odyssey but the children are too young, so they can just watch Percy Jackson. 

"I feel like there's probably a cooking show about Greek mythology at that point, I mean like there's so many connections no matter what you like."

Percy Jackson is released on Disney+ and Hulu on Friday 20 November.
