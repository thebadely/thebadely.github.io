Manchester United have signed Sweden winger Monica Jusu Bah from BK Hacken for just under Â£400,000.

The 23-year-old joins on a four-year deal and becomes United's third signing before Thursday's Women's Super League transfer window deadline.

Jusu Bah was under contract until 2028 at Swedish club BK Hacken and United sources say they faced competition for her signature.

"It's an unbelievable feeling to join Manchester United," she said.

"There is a real togetherness in the group and you can feel the excitement from everyone for a big season ahead.

"The opportunity to work with Eva Olid is perfect. Everybody knows the quality of her coaching and her ability to support players to reach their highest level.

"I'm ready to keep pushing myself, help the team achieve our ambitions together and create special memories at this incredible club."

She has played in Sweden for her entire career, breaking through at Umea before moving to BK Hacken in 2023.

In May, Jusu Bah helped BK Hacken win the inaugural Women's Europa Cup.

She has made 11 appearances for Sweden and played in all six of their Women's World Cup qualifying matches this year.

It is understood United hope to add at least one more player before next week's deadline. 

United have signed defender Andrea Medina from Atletico Madrid and goalkeeper Janina Leitzig from Leicester City.
