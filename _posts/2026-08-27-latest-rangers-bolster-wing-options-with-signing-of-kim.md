Rangers have bolstered their wide options with the arrival of Kim Min-su from Girona for an undisclosed fee, with manager Derek McInnes saying the South Korean adds "another exciting dimension to the squad".

The 20-year-old has signed a four-year contract, with the option of a further year, becoming Rangers' 12th signing of the summer.

Kim was loaned to second-tier Andorra last term and leaves Girona after their relegation from La Liga. He scored 19 goals in 84 appearances for Girona B and played eight times for their senior team. At Andorra, he netted six times in 40 games and provided four assists.

And he admits "it was a surprise" to hear of Rangers' interest.
