Here’s what you need to know: Phil Parkinson was appointed Wrexham manager in July 2021 when the club was in the National League. 

Wrexham manager Phil Parkinson admitted the 6-0 thrashing by West Ham United was among the lowest points of his time at the club.

The visitors were outclassed by a rampant West Ham at London Stadium on Friday.

It was Wrexham's heaviest league defeat in 22 years, since losing by the same margin at Bournemouth in 2004.

Parkinson said everyone had to take ownership of the performance, including himself.

"We're going to make sure we have that accountability as individuals and as staff," Parkinson said.

"We'll respond and we've got to put the ingredients back in place very quickly to put in a better performance than we did.

"[This] is the first night where we looked like we had a soft centre when it really mattered most and we've got to rediscover that.

"Everyone thinks it's been a complete bed of roses since I've been here [but] we've had other days that have been really, really tough.

"We've had setbacks and each season I've been here we've had periods where it's not been going well and from the outside the team can look a little lost.

"And then we've rediscovered what we're all about as a side.

"We've got to find a way back and give ourselves a chance. We need to show more character and resilience."
