Here’s what you need to know: Island Global Research said the survey was completed by more than 3,000 people across Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man

There has been "a major increase" in the overall cost of living in the past 12 months in Guernsey, Jersey and the Isle of Man, a survey has found.

Island Global Research, a market research and consultancy company, found more than 69% of respondents in Jersey, 68% in Guernsey and 70% in the Isle of Man said they had noticed a cost increase, external.

The survey, which was completed by over 1,000 people in each island, saw 57% of respondents describe themselves as "less than comfortable" with about one in 10 going without essentials like food and heating.

The survey also found 65% said they now spent less on non essentials and 53% said they are shopping around more because of the price rises.

In Jersey, 10% of respondents reported they could not afford the increased costs and often have to go with out essentials, while in Guernsey it was 11%.

The survey also showed that two thirds of people in Jersey said they had found it difficult to meet living costs in the last 12 months, an increase of 17 percentage points compared to 2024.

Guernsey also saw an increase with 58% reporting they found it difficult to meet living costs.

The survey also found about one in four received support to help finance their everyday living expenses in the last six months.

On the Isle of Man, 36% said they cut back on groceries and 30% received support to help finance everyday living expenses over a six-month period.
