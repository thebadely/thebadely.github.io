Chicago Bears deliver statement victory against Seahawks

The Chicago Bears travelled to Seattle and delivered an impressive 34-17 victory over the Seahawks.

D'Andre Swift rushed for three touchdowns as the Bears backed up their previous victory over the New England Patriots - the Super Bowl runners-up.

Swift, 27, scored all three of his touchdowns in the second half, with the Bears' defence, led by newly signed defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, expertly shutting down the hosts.

It was a third successive win for Chicago, who improve to 6-2 for the season.

Lamar Jackson sustained his latest injury when his hand struck an opponent after throwing the ball

Two former MVPs went head-to-head as Lamar Jackson returned from three games out with an ankle injury to face Josh Allen's Buffalo Bills.

But that match-up was ruined within moments as Jackson injured his right thumb on the game's first snap, and his back-up Tyler Huntley was then ruled out in the second quarter with a toe injury.

That meant they had to finish the game with undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano at quarterback and Buffalo ran out comfortable 36-10 winners.

The Ravens have lost their past three games and now have a 4-4 record, and Jackson could miss the rest of the season as he is set to undergo surgery.

This is the latest injury blow for Jackson. Although he is one of the most gifted players of his generation, the 29-year-old seems destined not to reach the Super Bowl during his career and the Ravens could miss out on the play-offs for a second straight year.

Huntley could also be out for weeks so the Ravens will have to act fast to bring in an experienced QB or Fagnano will have to start against the Jacksonville Jaguars on Thursday.

NFL's Campbell taken to hospital with neck injury

Minnesota put their 6-0 record on the line at their NFC North rivals the Detroit Lions, who won 19-15 to halt the Vikings' unbeaten start.

Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 153 yards and a touchdown as Detroit (5-2) prevailed in one of several key divisional match-ups.

Lions linebacker Jack Campbell was taken to hospital to be treated for a neck injury suffered while tackling tight end TJ Hockenson in the fourth quarter.

The Denver Broncos (6-2) replaced the Kansas City Chiefs (5-2) at the top of the AFC West after coming from 11 points down in the final quarter to win 28-24.

Patrick Mahomes threw for three touchdowns and 321 yards but that was not enough for the Chiefs as the Broncos' defence made a goal-line stand at the death to clinch victory.

The Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) are in charge of the AFC South after romping to a 42-7 win over the Indianapolis Colts, who look set for a rebuild.

After firing their general manager and trading two key players in the past two weeks, the Colts are reportedly in talks over trading star running back Jonathan Taylor.

Meanwhile, the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) beat the Cleveland Browns 26-7 to go top of the AFC North.

Another of Pittsburgh's divisional rivals, the Cincinnati Bengals, made hard work of it before beating the Tennessee Titans 43-36 in overtime.

Despite being intercepted three times by Tennessee's Amani Hooker, Burrow became the first player in 75 years - and only the second ever after Norm van Brocklin's 554 passing yards in a 1951 game - to record more than 450 passing yards, five touchdown passes and a rushing touchdown in a single game. 

After a mixed start to the season, the former first overall draft pick has also become the first quarterback in NFL history to register a 70% completion percentage plus touchdown passes in three consecutive games.

Burrow and his high-powered offence are in ominous form as they seek a Super Bowl return and host Pittsburgh in two weeks.

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers ran the ball 56 times while Atlanta had 28 carries for 99 yards

While Cincinnati's offence relies on its passing game and receivers, many of the NFL's running backs grabbed the spotlight on Sunday.

For the first time since 2003, at least six players had 140 rushing yards on the same day, including Bucky Irving (170) and Kenneth Gainwell (140) as the Tampa Bay Buccaneers beat the Atlanta Falcons 16-0.

Torrential rain in Florida made handling conditions difficult so Tampa Bay quarterback Baker Mayfield attempted just three passes, completing none of them.

Instead he spent most of the day handing the ball to Irving or Gainwell as the Bucs racked up a team-record 324 rushing yards.

Tyler Allgeier was the running back who starred for the Arizona Cardinals, rushing for 147 yards at the Dallas Cowboys.

But it was eccentric back-up quarterback Gardner Minshew who helped turn the game in Arizona's favour after replacing an ineffective Jacoby Brissett with the Cardinals trailing 17-3 late in the third quarter.

After being a late draft pick by Jacksonville in 2019, Minshew has spent most of his career as a back-up and in the off-season Arizona became his sixth team in eight years.

He made a thrilling debut, though, becoming the first quarterback this season to throw three touchdown passes in a fourth quarter.

That tied the game at the death, and although recent hero Chad Ryland failed to kick the extra point, he redeemed himself in overtime with a field goal to win 33-30.

It is only the second time Dallas have ever lost when leading by 10 points inside the final three minutes and leaves them 3-5.

It also heaps pressure on head coach Brian Schottenheimer, with outspoken team owner Jerry Jones saying: "It's an indictment of the whole show".
