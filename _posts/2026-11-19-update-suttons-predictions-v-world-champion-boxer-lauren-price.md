Here’s what you need to know: Manchester United start life under new boss Mauricio Pochettino on Sunday but will they get their first Premier League win since August when they face Liverpool at Anfield?

"My worry for United is that they have one of the worst back fours in the Premier League," said BBC Sport football expert Chris Sutton.

"Their defensive record is something Pochettino will try to address but, whoever he picks, they are just not very good."

Sutton is making predictions for all 380 Premier League games this season, against AI, BBC Sport readers anad a variety of guests.

For week 11, he takes on boxer Lauren Price, who is an Arsenal fan.

She will defend her unified welterweight titles against Michaela Kotaskova on Friday, and has a shot at becoming Wales' first undisputed world champion after the vacant WBO welterweight title was added to the fight.

Price returns to home soil at the Utilita Arena in Cardiff for a fight that will be shown live on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer, from 21:00 GMT.

Price, the first Welsh boxer to be an amateur world champion and win Olympic gold, said: "When I turned pro it was my dream to become 'undisputed'. To achieve that and make history again for Wales on Friday, and in front of my own fans too, would be a dream come true."

Before turning to boxing, Price was a world kickboxing champion and international footballer - she captained the Wales Under-19 side, and played for the senior team as well as Cardiff City.

"My football days have helped me massively to get where I am in the ring today," the 32-year-old told BBC Sport.

"I honestly believe my footwork, and how fast my feet are, comes from when I played football.

"I am really proud of everything I achieved in football, especially playing for my country. It is the same now in boxing - to get in the ring and represent Wales is always an honour."

Price said the support of her grandparents helped her become successful at so many sports  - and she followed her grandad, Derek, by becoming an Arsenal fan too.

"When I was a kid I was absolutely besotted with football and Arsenal were THE team when I was growing up," she explained.

"It was just the way they played under Arsene Wenger, and how they passed the ball - it was incredible. I loved Robin van Persie but Thierry Henry was my icon, my hero."

Chris Sutton and Lauren Price were speaking to BBC Sport's Chris Bevan.
