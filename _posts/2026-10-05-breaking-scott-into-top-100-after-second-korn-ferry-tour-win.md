Sandy Scott has moved into the world's top 100 at 87th after winning for the second week in a row on the Korn Ferry Tour.

The Scot, 28, had been placed 127th in the men's rankings and joins compatriot Robert MacIntyre, who is 19th, as one of the world's best 100.

Scott won the Compliance Solutions Championship in a play-off with American William Sides in Oklahoma on Sunday, seven days on from securing the Nationwide Children's Hospital Championship in Ohio.

He is top of the second-tier Korn Ferry Tour points list and has obtained PGA Tour membership for 2027.

Meanwhile, another Scot, Michael Stewart, has climbed 497 places in the rankings to 483rd after finishing joint fourth at the Alfred Dunhill Links Championship in St Andrews.
