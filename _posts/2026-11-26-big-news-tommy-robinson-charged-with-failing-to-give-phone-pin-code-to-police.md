Let's dive into the details: Far-right activist Tommy Robinson has been charged with failing to provide his phone's pin code to police at Heathrow Airport. 

The alleged incident happened when Robinson, whose real name is Stephen Yaxley-Lennon, was detained after returning to the UK from Russia via Turkey on 13 June this year.

The 43-year-old is due to appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court on 14 December, the Metropolitan Police said.

Posting on X after being charged, Robinson, describing himself as a journalist, said he "refused the Pin because it held private messages from grooming gang survivors".

He described the charge as "pure state persecution".

Robinson, from Bedfordshire, was detained under the Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019 in June and has been charged under the same act.

Prior to his arrival in the UK Robinson posted a video on X showing him meeting Elon Musk's father in Moscow.

Robinson is currently awaiting sentencing after being convicted of harassing two Daily Mail journalists.

A jury at Southwark Crown Court found him guilty of two counts of harassment causing fear of violence.

The case concerned Robinson's response to Daily Mail and Mail Online articles from August 2024, showing him on holiday in Cyprus while riots took place in England following the Southport murders.

Robinson was granted bail ahead of his sentencing, scheduled for 16 December.

Robinson had denied the charges, saying he had "panicked" and had been "scared" when the Daily Mail published a story accusing him of "stoking race riots from his sunbed".
