Lauren Price has a shot at becoming Wales' first undisputed world champion against Michaela Kotaskova on Friday after the vacant WBO welterweight title was added to the fight.

The 32-year-old will go up against Czech Republic's Kotaskova in Cardiff in a fight that will be shown live on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer.

Price will defending her IBF, WBA and WBC belts at Utilita Arena and will now have the chance to add a fourth.

Mikaela Mayer relinquished the WBO title ahead of her rematch with Chantelle Cameron in December.

Olympic champion Price successfully defended her belts and moved to a 10-0 record by beating mandatory challenger Stephanie Pineiro on points in April.

If Price, who also holds the IBO and Ring Magazine belts, wins then she will become the first Welsh boxer to hold all four major world titles simultaneously.

Joe Calzaghe beat Mikkel Kessler for the WBO, WBA and WBC super-middleweight titles in 2007 but had relinquished the IBF title.

Price would be the fourth British female fighter to achieve undisputed status after Savannah Marshall, Chantelle Cameron and Ellie Scotney.

American Mayer held the WBO welterweight title but will instead go up against Cameron for her unified WBO, WBA, and WBC super-welterweight belts.

"As much as I looked forward to unifying for undisputed, it's dragged out too long and the Cameron rematch is now my priority," she posted on social media.

"No need to be holding up the welterweight division in the meantime."

She faces a tough test against 34-year-old Kotaskova, who is the current WBF title holder and has suffered just one defeat in her 15-fight professional career.

Price headlines a bill featuring plenty of Welsh fighters with Rhys Edwards going for the WBA continental featherweight title against Scotland's Lee McGregor while Willy Gilheaney faces Argentina's Alberto Ignacio Palmetta for the WBA continental gold welterweight belt.
