Arsenal dominated Manchester City as they beat Enzo Maresca's side 3-0 in the Community Shield in the Principality Stadium.

Riccardo Calafiori opened the scoring inside 30 seconds for the Premier League champions before Kai Havertz added a second with a close range header.

Arsenal captain Martin Odegaard then wrapped up the win with a clever finish just three minutes after the break.

Here's how the players rated as the first piece of silverware of the season was lifted.
