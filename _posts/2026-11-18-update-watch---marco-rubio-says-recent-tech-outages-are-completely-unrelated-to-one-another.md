Recent tech outages across the US are "completely unrelated" to one another, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio has said.

Microsoft outlook went down for nearly an entire day, a tech glitch briefly grounded all flights in the US and major American banks reported system issues all in the space of several days.

The secretary said there is "zero evidence" of a link between the recent cyber incidents.

Rubio spoke to media in the Philippines' capital Manila, where he is joining US President Donald Trump on a major foreign event attending the Asean and Apec summits, where he is expected to meet with the leaders of China and Russia.
