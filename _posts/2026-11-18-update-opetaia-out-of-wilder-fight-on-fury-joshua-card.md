Australian Jai Opetaia has withdrawn from his heavyweight fight with Deontay Wilder on the undercard of Tyson Fury's bout with Anthony Joshua.

Unbeaten cruiserweight champion Jai Opetaia was set to step up to heavyweight for the first time to face American Wilder at Cardiff's Principality Stadium on 11 December.

Opetaia, 31, did not give a reason for his withdrawal, but it is understood he aggravated a hand injury in training.

"Apologies to everyone and especially [Wilder] and his team for withdrawing from the bout," he wrote on Instagram.

"Fighter to fighter, I know the feeling and it's frustrating. I've always prided myself on fighting anyone, any place."

Opetaia criticised "keyboard warriors" who suggested he was scared off by Wilder's powerful right hand.

Opetaia, who has won all 31 of his professional bouts, reiterated his ambition to become undisputed cruiserweight champion.

Unified champion David Benavidez has expressed an interest in facing Opetaia in 2027.

Opetaia's withdrawal leaves a significant gap on the undercard of one of the biggest all-British heavyweight fights in history.

It remains unclear whether former world champion Wilder, 41, will still feature on the bill, but several British heavyweights have expressed an interest in replacing Opetaia.

Derek Chisora has offered to face Wilder in a rematch, having lost a split decision in London in April.

Richard Riakporhe, Olympic bronze medallist Frazer Clarke and Johnny Fisher have said they would be prepared to fight.

The long-awaited showdown between Fury and Joshua is understood to be for the WBA 'super' heavyweight title, with ring walks expected to take place no earlier than 01:00 GMT on 12 December.
