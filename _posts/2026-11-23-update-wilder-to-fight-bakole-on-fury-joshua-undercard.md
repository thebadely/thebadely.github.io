According to new developments, Former world champion Deontay Wilder (left) has 45 wins, four defeats and one draw, while Martin Bakole has 21 wins, two defeats and one draw

Heavyweight Martin Bakole will face former world champion Deontay Wilder on the undercard of Tyson Fury's fight with Anthony Joshua in Cardiff on 11 December.

The 33-year-old Congolese fighter replaces Australian Jai Opetaia, who withdrew after aggravating a hand injury in training.

Scotland-based Bakole had been scheduled to fight unbeaten Nigerian Solomon Adebayo on the bill, but will instead face 41-year-old Wilder in the co-main event.

Bakole suffered a second-round knockout defeat by Joseph Parker in February 2025, having accepted the fight on just two days' notice.

He then fought to a majority draw with Nigerian Efe Ajagba in May 2025 and has not competed since.

Former WBC heavyweight champion Wilder, who has 43 knockouts in 45 victories, is looking to build on his split-decision win over Britain's Derek Chisora in April.

Unbeaten cruiserweight Opetaia, 31, was set to make his heavyweight debut against Wilder but pulled out of the contest last week.

Several British heavyweights, including Chisora, Richard Riakporhe, Frazer Clarke and Johnny Fisher had expressed an interest in facing Wilder following Opetaia's withdrawal.

Fury v Joshua - boxing's epic unfinished story

Fury and Joshua's long-awaited showdown has been more than a decade in the making.

After years of failed negotiations, the fight was finally agreed with the financial backing of Saudi boxing powerbroker Turki Alalshikh and will be streamed globally on Netflix.

They are expected to contest the WBA 'super' heavyweight title at Cardiff's Principality Stadium, with ringwalks planned for no earlier than 01:00 GMT on Saturday, 12 December.

But despite the magnitude of the main event, the undercard has so far failed to match the anticipation surrounding one of the biggest fights in British boxing history.

Wilder remains a recognisable name among British fans, largely because of his memorable trilogy with Fury, but a contest against Bakole is unlikely to capture the imagination of the wider public.

Boxing records website BoxRec currently lists only Fury-Joshua on the event, although Netflix has announced further bouts in a social media post.

Welsh heavyweight Moses Jolly (12-0-1, 8 KOs) will face Brazil's Igor Macedo, who has won 15 of his 16 professional fights, with 14 victories by knockout.

Swansea-fighter Jolly, 30, is unbeaten and last fought in August, when he drew with Italian Guido Vianello at London's O2 Arena.

Unbeaten 21-year-old American prospect Da'Mazion Vanhouter (14-0, 11 KOs) will face Belgium's Michael Essomba (11-2, 10 KOs) in another heavyweight contest.

Boxing great Manny Pacquiao and Cardiff's former world champion Joe Cordina had both been linked with appearances on the undercard, although Pacquiao is no longer expected to feature.

It remains unclear whether any further bouts will be added to the event.
