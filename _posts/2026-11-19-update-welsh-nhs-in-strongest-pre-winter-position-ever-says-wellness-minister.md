Wales' health minister has insisted that the Welsh NHS is in its strongest position heading into winter than ever before.

Mabon ap Gwynfor was speaking after the latest NHS performance figures showed that more than 11,500 people waited 12 hours or more in emergency departments from arrival until admission, transfer or discharge in one month.

In September, there were 4,900 patient pathways waiting more than two years for planned treatment - a fall of 160 compared to the previous month.

But the overall number of people waiting for NHS treatment in Wales has risen for the sixth month in a row. In September, there were 721,400 waiting for planned treatment, compared to 714,200 in August.
