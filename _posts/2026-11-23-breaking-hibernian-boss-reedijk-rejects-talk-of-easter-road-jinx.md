Let's dive into the details: Hibs have yet to win at home in the league this season and lost 4-0 to Dundee United on Saturday

Manager Marink Reedijk has rejected suggestions Easter Road is jinxed despite Hibernian being without a home win in the Scottish Premiership for more than seven months.

Hibs have lost nine of their last 10 home league games, with the 3-0 victory against Kilmarnock on 4 April under former manager David Gray the last success in the Premiership in front of their own fans. 

Reedijk - in charge for seven matches, three of which have been at home - believes a focus on "brilliant basics" can end that dismal run on Tuesday night against Dundee, who conceded eight at home to Heart of Midlothian at the weekend.

"We need to be ourselves, need to show what we're capable of, show what we've  worked at, who we are," said the Dutchman. 

"On Saturday we didn't represent ourselves in that way. Therefore if we start to do these things well in a consistent manner, I don't see that anything is jinxed whatsoever. It's up to us."

The wretched sequence stretches back to the end of last season, when Hearts, Celtic and Motherwell won after the Premiership split and continued this season with the Steelmen and their Edinburgh rivals triumphing again at Easter Road.  

Victories for Kilmarnock, Falkirk, Celtic and the mauling at the hands of Dundee United have been punctuated only by a draw against similarly out-of-form Aberdeen.

"The basics we've been doing well in the other matches, we didn't do them on Saturday. That was for me, I wouldn't say a shock but it was surprising," Reedijk added.

"Saturday didn't represent who we are and didn't represent the week's training we had. It is important you are consistent in the things you do. 

"I keep repeating the word consistency and the basic things that make us good: being intense, being brave in trying to go forward, being on the front foot, being proactive and sticking together.  

"For me the key is to do things consistently and to be happy to do the brilliant basics over and over and over again."

The Dens Park club might be ideal opponents for Hibs to be facing - the last Dundee manager to win in Leith was Ivano Bonetti more than a quarter of a century ago.
