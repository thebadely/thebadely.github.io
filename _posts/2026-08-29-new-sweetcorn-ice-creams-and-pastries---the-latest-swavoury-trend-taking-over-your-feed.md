"Sweetcorn belongs in desserts!"

That's according to Crystelle Pereira, a cook and former Great British Bake Off finalist known for her wacky flavour combinations.

While sweetcorn is already a staple in South East Asian and Latin American puddings, it has largely been relegated to sandwich fillings and pizza toppings in the UK. But that could all be changing.

In recent weeks, the vegetable has been popping up all over social media in sweet treat form - as an ingredient in everything from cookies and ice cream to cheesecake.

Food trend forecasters say sweetcorn is starting to appear in "some exciting and unexpected guises" on the UK food scene.

Cook and former Great British Bake Off star Crystelle Pereira says "sweetcorn belongs in desserts" 

It is all part of a wider food trend involving the combination of sweet and savoury flavours we wouldn't traditionally put together, sometimes called the "swavoury" movement, Shokofeh Hejazi of food trend agency thefoodpeople says.

Other examples include combinations like purple sweet potato ice cream, cheesecake with carrot compote, or chocolate bars loaded with salty potato chips. 

Both sweetcorn's flavour profile and ability to be blended into a creamy texture makes it a great fit for the trend, says Hejazi.

Other UK bakeries and dessert shops have been featuring sweetcorn baked goods on their menus too.
