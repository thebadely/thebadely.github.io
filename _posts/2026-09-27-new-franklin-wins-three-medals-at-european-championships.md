Olympic medallist Mallory Franklin completed her return to action by winning canoe gold and kayak cross silver at the Paddle Europe Slalom Championships in Ivrea.

The 32-year-old delivered a flawless, penalty-free run of 100.47 seconds to beat France's Doriane Delassus by 0.03 seconds and claim her third career European C1 title.

Victory marked Franklin's first individual medal since the birth of her daughter, having opened her week in Italy with a bronze medal in the team event.

"It's a really nice way to end the year. It's been a tough one coming back and getting back on the start line," said Franklin.

"I've been building throughout the year and it's just a nice way to finish it."

Franklin went on to complete a full set of medals on the final day, battling through the kayak cross knockout rounds under the floodlights to secure silver behind the Czech Republic's Tereza Kneblova.

Great Britain enjoyed double success in the kayak cross as two-time Olympic medallist Kimberley Woods took individual gold, winning the morning time trial in 48.02 seconds ahead of Poland's Klaudia Zwolinska.

And there was bronze in the men's K1 team event for Ben Haylett, Sam Leaver and Joe Clarke, to go with the one won by Woods, Franklin and Ellis Miller in the women's C1.

In the men's events, Clarke and Sam Leaver missed out on the podium in the kayak final, finishing fifth and sixth respectively behind Czech winner Jakub Krejci.
