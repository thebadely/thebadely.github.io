The United States began their bid to win an 11th consecutive Presidents Cup by opening up a 3-2 lead over the International team at the end of Thursday's fourballs matches at Medinah Country Club in Illinois.

Justin Thomas and rookie Jackson Koivun put the first point on the board for Brandt Snedeker's American team with a resounding 4&3 win over Canada's Corey Conners and Colombia's Nico Echavarria. 

World number one Scottie Scheffler, who was partnered with his long-time friend Sam Burns, took the 17th hole and held on to close out their victory over Min Woo Lee and Sungjae Im, who were left to rue several missed putts down the stretch. 

And two-time major champions Wyndham Clark and Collin Morikawa ensured the US would take an advantage into Friday's five foursome matches. 

Clark and Morikawa led from the opening hole against Australia's Adam Scott and reigning Open champion Ryan Fox before completing their 3&2 success with two holes to spare. 

"A session win is a session win; we're going to take it any way we can get it," said  Snedeker.

However, Geoff Ogilvy's International side will head into the alternate shot format on Friday with optimism of remaining in contention to claim only their second ever triumph in the biennial contest, some 28 years after their first in 1998.

Japanese duo Hideki Matsuyama and debutant Ryo Hisatsune recorded a 3&1 success over Cameron Young and Xander Schauffele, while Kim Si-woo and Tom Kim always looked comfortable in their 3&2 win over Chris Gotterup and Patrick Cantlay.

The five foursome matches scheduled to take place on Friday will be followed by two sessions on Saturday, split between four four-ball matches in the morning and four foursomes in the afternoon.
