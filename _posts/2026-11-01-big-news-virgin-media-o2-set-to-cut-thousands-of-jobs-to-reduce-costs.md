Here’s what you need to know: Thousands of Virgin Media O2 staff are facing redundancy as the telecoms firm attempts to save money in the face of billions of pounds of debt and tougher competition.

The broadband provider has not finalised the number of roles to be cut but it is likely be a sizeable chunk of its 16,000-strong workforce, the BBC understands.

Virgin Media O2 has billions in debt on its balance sheet and is tackling both smaller providers as well as giants like BT, which has agreed a deal to acquire rival TalkTalk, which Virgin Media O2 has firmly opposed.

The company declined to comment. Prospect, a union which represents Virgin Media O2 staff, said the cuts were "disappointing and very unsettling".

Noel McClean. deputy general secretary at Prospect, said the union was "committed to supporting our members through what is a difficult time, as well as also working with employers to explore ways to mitigate the number of redundancies and ensure retraining and redeployment of staff affected".

The majority of the affected staff are in roles that are not customer-facing and a number of senior leadership changes are already under way, the BBC has been told.

Virgin Media O2 is also expected to review its use of contractors and suppliers in conjunction with the job cuts.
