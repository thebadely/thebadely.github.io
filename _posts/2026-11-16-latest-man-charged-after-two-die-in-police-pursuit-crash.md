A man has been charged with causing the death of two people by dangerous driving after a fatal crash in Leeds. 

Leigh Ross, 39, is due to appear at Leeds Magistrates' Court later after the crash at the junction of Gelderd Road and Lowfields Road at 00:44 GMT on Sunday. 

West Yorkshire Police said officers were following a blue BMW 123D when it failed to stop and crashed into a white Volkswagen Scirocco. The driver of the VW, a man aged 22, died at the scene. A 23-year-old female passenger died later in hospital. Both were from York. 

Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl who were in the BMW, were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving but were released on bail.

The teenagers were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life-threatening.

Ross, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has also been charged with two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified; two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed/uninsured; failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required by a constable; and aggravated vehicle taking.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for any witnesses to any part of the incident.

A spokesperson said specialist family liaison officers were supporting the families of the two people who died. 

Anyone with information about the incident, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers.

A spokesperson for the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) said on Sunday it had launched an independent investigation at about 02:30 GMT and sent investigators to the crash scene to "begin gathering evidence".

The spokesperson said that inquiries were "at a very early stage".
