Min Woo Lee was missing his eight-iron for the first round of the Australian PGA Championship after he gave a golf lesson to Formula 1 stars Carlos Sainz and Lance Stroll - but still managed to finish four shots off the lead.

The Australian was in Las Vegas for the most recent round of the F1 calendar, with Sainz finishing the race eighth for Williams and Stroll in 17th place for Aston Martin.

"Funny story, I was with Carlos Sainz and Lance Stroll and I was giving them a lesson and I think they were touching the clubs and it's gone somewhere, so I had no eight-iron," said Lee.

"The third hole of the day I needed an eight-iron, I needed it a few times today. 

"It's actually a really fun way to play golf - it's either a nine-iron I got to hit it harder or I've got to add some spin with a seven-iron."

Lee carded a four-under par 68 at the DP World Tour event in an opening round that included five birdies and a bogey as he finished four shots behind leader Haydn Barron.  

"I felt like I hit really good shots, better shots than I would have with an eight-iron so I wasn't stressing. 

"It's here now, we just got the update that it's here in Sydney at the house so, hopefully, I hit it better tomorrow maybe not I might keep it out."
