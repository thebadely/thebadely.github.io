Let's dive into the details: A Worthing charity says families it supports are struggling to afford Christmas

Christmas is likely to see families cut back on essentials to try and meet the expenses of the festive period, a West Sussex soup kitchen has warned.

Katrina McCormack, chief executive of the Shout Worthing Soup Kitchen, told BBC Radio Sussex that they are forced to limit the amount of support they can give due to high demand at Christmas, and make sure their shelves are stocked.

 It comes at the start of a campaign to raise thousands for turkey and other Christmas trimmings for struggling families, which the charity says is a "driving force" for their work in December.

McCormack said: "We ran an afternoon tea with Father Christmas, and last year we had children asking him for food."

She added: "A lot of families are struggling to pay that extra, they are struggling to put food on the table.

"Most of our families won't have a Christmas dinner without this."

New research from charity Save the Children found that, of those surveyed, 65% of parents of children under 16 in the UK were concerned about being able to afford Christmas.

It added that nearly a third of parents will buy fewer presents than normal, and 16% will reduce heating use in December to save money.

McCormack said that, due to increasing cost of living, the charity had seen an increase in the number of people needing support, including families who are asking for help when they would not be needed in other parts of the year.

"Poeple have come up and asked us if we can help them get an air fryer because they can't afford to put a cooker on," she added.

Nicky Chisholm raises funds to help feed people at Christmas

Ahead of Christmas, Nicky Chisholm, from Brighton, is aiming to fundraise to feed 500 people on Christmas Day.

The campaign, which she has run online for the past 16 years, raises money for the soup kitchen and domestic abuse charity Safe in Sussex.

Chisholm said: "Each year it fills an entire campervan of food. It gives you hope that people will donate and help out.

"On Christmas Day I just want people to feel loved.

"I would hate to think that there's people out there who couldn't have a Christmas dinner."
