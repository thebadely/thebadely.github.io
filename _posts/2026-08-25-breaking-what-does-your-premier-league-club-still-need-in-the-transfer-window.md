The first weekend of the Premier League season is complete but clubs still have one week of the transfer window remaining to secure last-minute squad additions.

Results are one thing but for many managers and sporting directors their opening match was an eye-opener in what positions need to be strengthened, who is no longer needed and what gaps still need to be plugged.

With the transfer window closing on Tuesday, 1 September at 23:00 BST, BBC Sport takes a look at what each top-flight club still needs and fans also have their say.

Alex Howell, football reporter

The Premier League champions have already strengthened the middle of the pitch with Bruno Guimaraes, the wing with Christos Tzolis and their defence with Ezri Konsa.

But Arsenal are still in the market for a forward and have a long-standing interest in Julian Alvarez. 

Although it appears to be a difficult deal due to many factors, the Gunners will be ready to act if there is a chance to get it done.

Watson, Arsenal fan

If you want someone who is Premier League proven, who has won it all from the World Cup to the Champions League, Julian Alvarez looks like the guy that can take Arsenal to the promised land of finally getting over the line in Europe. Especially when there aren't that many elite forwards in the world.

Nick Mashiter, football reporter

What don't Aston Villa need after a raft of star departures this summer?

Ollie Watkins' transfer saga, with Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal trying to sign the striker, leaves Villa looking for a likely replacement with Nicolas Jackson a target. 

A winger is also on the agenda while they need a defender after Ezri Konsa's move to Arsenal, and have opened talks for Club Brugge central defender Joel Ordonez after bids for Southampton centre-back Taylor Harwood-Bellis were rejected.

Amy Finley, Aston Villa fan

It is clear Aston Villa desperately require a striker more than anything right now. 

Although I massively rate Victor Lindelof and Pau Torres, the display against Brighton has worried me slightly. 

Therefore, I would welcome another centre-back as well. 

It looks like we will be OK in midfield once Johan Manzambi is fit and with the addition of Leon Goretzka.

Nizaar Kinsella, football reporter

Bournemouth are used to being picked off and will simply be hoping to come through the final eight days of the transfer window unscathed. 

It is clear that big clubs are circling for forward Eli Junior Kroupi and midfielder Alex Scott, who the Cherries insist are not for sale.

In terms of incomings, Bournemouth are largely done as they close in on a move for Juventus goalkeeper Michele Di Gregorio.

Phil McNulty, chief football writer

Brentford are in the fortunate position of being able to choose their deals carefully after some excellent squad strengthening. 

The Bees have been looking at left-back, with West Ham's El Hadji Malick Diouf strongly linked, but it hardly seems a matter of urgency with Keane Lewis-Potter able to play as a wing-back with Rico Henry and Aaron Hickey also in the mix.

There is experienced striking cover in the shape of former England international Callum Wilson and an all-round depth to Keith Andrews' squad that was missing last season. 

Do not expect it to be a hectic closing few days in the transfer window.

Michael Johnston, Brentford fan

Another forward option - particularly down the left - would take this window for Brentford from a nine to a 10 out of 10. 

Mykhailo Mudryk's name has been doing the rounds in recent weeks and Brentford have history there, having chased him prior to his move to Chelsea. 

Get someone like Mudryk in, and I don't think the transfer window could've gone much better for Andrews.

Simon Stone, football reporter

It is anticipated Yankuba Minteh will leave for Liverpool, which might create space at Brighton for another forward and Carlos Baleba's exit for Manchester United may see the club make a move for midfield reinforcements.

It is possible there may be other exits but, as usual, Brighton will stick to their data driven recruitment plan.

"We must continue to evolve, invest and innovate while remaining true to the principles that have brought us this far," was chairman Tony Bloom's message to fans before the 4-0 win against Aston Villa.

Maz Mirzadeh, Brighton fan

With Baleba's anticipated departure and Minteh looking likely that he might leave as well, we need to bring a defensive midfielder who can bring a physical presence to our midfield and a winger who is proven at the top level. 

We have a lot of games this season with European football, so we need to have the squad depth to compete on all fronts if we want to secure a top-10 finish again.

Chelsea supporters may want another star forward or a new goalkeeper to replace the unconvincing Robert Sanchez, but Xabi Alonso feels his squad is largely complete for this summer.

However, there is a huge amount of work to do to reduce the squad from 39 players to around 25 within the next eight days. That is the priority, as the Blues will not want frustrated players in their squad this season.

Chelsea need to focus more on the outgoings than incoming players. 

With no Europe it's important we do not carry a squad that's too heavy, as we won't have too much room for rotation. 

If we were to bring anybody in, I think the priority is a top-drawer goalkeeper.

Honestly, I think we have done enough in the transfer window to build a team that can stay up in this league.

There has been a lot of change at Palace with the loss of Maxence Lacroix and a new manager starting the season in Pierre Sage.

The Eagles have been building depth for their Europa League campaign but still need a few bodies in the building.

Sage has made clear he wants a senior centre-back and Palace could be pushed to sign another defender too after the injury to Chadi Riad in the first game of the Premier League season against Everton.

Ollie Thomas, Crystal Palace fan

Riad's injury has amplified the need for another centre-back who is first-team ready.

Axel Disasi is a good start, but a bigger, permanent move would be great. That, plus a central midfielder, and we are set.

Phil McNulty, chief football writer

Everton simply must sign a recognised right-back before the window shuts. It has been a glaring problem for more than a year. 

It is a weakness recognised by everyone at the club but either a prime target cannot be agreed upon or is out of reach. 

Talented midfielder Merlin Rohl took on the job for the opening game against Crystal Palace, while Jake O'Brien and James Garner have also been pressed into service.

Time to finally make a decision and get one in. Maybe David Moyes will also look at adding another striker with questions still hanging over Beto and Thierno Barry. 

Chelsea outcast Liam Delap is one name regularly linked with Everton.

Nizaar Kinsella, football reporter

Fulham manager Alvaro Arbeloa has been clear that he is happy with the club's business so far, but more could be done to make his squad more balanced. 

There have been attempts to sign an additional central defender, with a move for Rennes' Abdelhamid Ait Boudlal explored two weeks ago, and that could be a key area the Cottagers look to strengthen. 

This is a club that does not operate at the very top end of the transfer market and one that enjoys a deadline-day deal.

John Charles, Fulham fan

It still feels like Fulham need a right winger - whether that is sticking with Oscar Bobb or providing serious competition for him - I think we need to cover for the loss of Harry Wilson's goals. 

We also need a right-sided centre-back as there are serious concerns over how Joachim Anderson will fit Arbeloa's new system.

Jonty Colman, journalist

It has already been a busy summer for Hull City's recruitment team but the ambitious Tigers are still working on adding two new forwards in Mohamed Ali-Cho and Ilyas Ansah.

There has also been interest from the club in Real Betis winger Pablo Garcia, although that deal has taken longer than hoped after the La Liga side raised their valuation of the 20-year-old. Stoke City's Sorba Thomas is also a player of interest.

A goalkeeper, midfielder and right-back also could be on the agenda for Sergej Jakirovic's side in the final week of the window.

Ryan Frankish, Hull fan

Over the next week, if we bring in a left winger, one more striker - assuming the Ali-Cho deal also gets completed - a central midfielder, an attacking midfielder and a right-back to add depth and challenge Lewie Coyle for the starting spot, we will be in a strong position to compete.
