It has been recently reported that Max Verstappen said George Russell's lock-up was "karma" for his comments about the Dutchman earlier in the Las Vegas weekend 

Max Verstappen sealed his fourth win of the season with a stunning victory at a chaotic Las Vegas Grand Prix. 

The Red Bull driver fought off his rivals for the lead throughout the race and crossed the line ahead of the two McLarens of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri. 

George Russell, who started second, locked up late on attempting a move on Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli and finished ninth, while Charles Leclerc was given a 10-second time penalty for making contact with Ferrari team-mate Lewis Hamilton. 

Here are my ratings for how the drivers performed across the whole weekend in Vegas. 

Max Verstappen - 10/10

A superb display of racecraft. Inherited pole after Kimi Antonelli's grid penalty and spent virtually the entire race being attacked, passed and then finding a way back through, while also working out the optimum energy deployment from inside his Red Bull. One of his finest victories. 

Lando Norris - 9/10

A remarkable recovery after dropping as low as 11th in the madness of the opening part of the race. Had genuine pace throughout and fought all the way back to second, finishing less than a second behind Verstappen.

Oscar Piastri - 9.5/10

One of the drives of the race. Qualified seventh but started 17th because of his engine penalty and charged all the way through to the podium, gaining 14 places and joining McLaren team-mate Norris in passing Antonelli right at the end.

Kimi Antonelli - 9.5/10

Fastest of everyone in qualifying and a genuine contender for victory despite starting sixth because of his engine penalty. Battled Verstappen superbly before his Mercedes' energy deployment problem on the final lap cost him two places, although he still recovered fourth from Pierre Gasly before the flag.

Pierre Gasly - 9/10

An excellent weekend and comfortably Alpine's best result of the season. Reached Q3, stayed with the leading group throughout and was less than three seconds away from victory at the finish.

Franco Colapinto - 8.5/10

Another huge result for Alpine. Qualified inside the top 10, stayed out of trouble while plenty around him didn't and converted ninth on the grid into sixth place.

Lewis Hamilton - 6.5/10

A messy weekend but there was still plenty of fight in the race. Missing Q3 was disappointing and the 10-second time penalty for passing Norris off the circuit was harsh, although being spun by Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc later on was not his fault and he recovered to finish seventh.

Carlos Sainz - 8.5/10

A superb recovery from 20th on the grid after his power-unit penalty. Kept himself in contention throughout, made up 12 places and even pinched eighth from Russell at the line to secure Williams' joint-best result of the season.

George Russell - 6.5/10

For 48 laps this was heading towards a very high score. Fastest in both second and final practice, qualified third and spent most of the race going toe-to-toe with Verstappen for the lead. But locking up and putting it into the barrier with two laps remaining was an enormous and costly mistake.

Charles Leclerc - 5/10

A frustrating weekend from start to finish. Ferrari's qualifying struggles left him 11th and although he recovered well enough to finish sixth on the road, spinning his team-mate Hamilton earned a 10-second time penalty and dropped him to 10th.

Nico Hulkenberg - 6.5/10

A solid weekend without quite finding the points. Led Audi in qualifying and again in the race, eventually finishing 11th after spending much of the evening embroiled in the midfield fight.

Gabriel Bortoleto - 6/10

Similar story to team-mate Hulkenberg but just a little further back. Started 13th, finished 12th and narrowly held off Haas' Oliver Bearman at the flag.

Oliver Bearman - 7.5/10

A very good recovery after starting last because of power-unit penalties. Climbed nine positions, raced aggressively with both Audis and finished only two-tenths behind Bortoleto.

Liam Lawson - 7.5/10

Qualified eighth and had looked set for another strong result before a fuel leak forced him to start from the pit lane. Recovering to 15th after effectively beginning the race out of contention was a decent salvage job.

Isack Hadjar - 8.5/10

The final position tells almost none of the story. Qualified fifth, started fourth and was part of the lead fight until his Red Bull front wing failed over the bumps after the safety-car restart. The pace was there for a huge result. He lost a position after the race because of a 10-second penalty for driving slowly and holding up Russell before they pitted under the safety car.

Rafael Camara - 6.5/10

Another respectable showing in only his second grand prix for Haas. Narrowly missed Q2 and was running steadily before being spun in the Sergio Perez collision, then recovered from the back after the safety car to finish 16th.

Lance Stroll - 4.5/10

Another difficult weekend for Aston Martin. Qualified 20th before his engine penalty pushed him further back and there was little progress to be made during the race.

Valtteri Bottas - 5/10

Qualified at the back and Cadillac again lacked the pace to threaten the midfield. At least he stayed in the fight with the Aston Martins and made it to the finish after several recent retirements.

Fernando Alonso - 4.5/10

Aston Martin simply never looked competitive. Qualified 19th, spent the race towards the back and retired late on. 

Arvid Lindblad - 5.5/10

A real missed opportunity for the Briton and his Racing Bulls team. Sixth in qualifying and fifth on the grid was superb, and he was still right in the leading train when the safety car came out. Spinning while trying to warm the tyres behind it and eventually retiring was a costly unforced error.

Sergio Perez - 5.5/10

Qualified only 18th but appeared to have better race pace and was making progress before the collision with Camara ended his afternoon.

Alex Albon - 5.5/10

A weekend that ended before the race even started. Qualified 16th and was due to start 14th, but overheating rear brakes on the laps to the grid caused enough damage to prevent Williams from racing the car.
