Despite questions within his party about where the money would come from, voters in the South the BBC spoke to have reacted positively to the announcement.

By Thursday, Burnham's attention had turned to businesses, announcing a 20% cut to rates for pubs and small live music venues.

Aidan Lavin co-owns the Raven and Bine pub in Southampton and said: "It's a promising start from the new prime minister, however, I fear it's nothing even close to enough.

"The increasing pressure from VAT to business rates to an increased minimum wage, it just feels like the walls are closing in from all angles." 

Lavin said he believes that without further measures, businesses across the South would not survive.

"It's coming to a point now where we are going to lose some of our inns and taverns, never to be returned," he told the BBC.
