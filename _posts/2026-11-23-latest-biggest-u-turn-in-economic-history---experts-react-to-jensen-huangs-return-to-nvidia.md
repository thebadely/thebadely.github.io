Nvidia's board members have announced that they unanimously voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as the company's boss "effective immediately" - capping a remarkable U-turn in the technology firm's fortunes.

The board had previously ruled out Huang's return after he admitted an affair with a woman who was alleged to be a Chinese spy.

US President Donald Trump praised the decision, telling CNBC he made it happen, while Nvidia's share price shot up by more than 30% after a week of poor performance in the wake of the initial scandal.
