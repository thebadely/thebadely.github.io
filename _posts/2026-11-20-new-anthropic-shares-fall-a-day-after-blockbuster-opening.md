Here’s what you need to know: Anthropic's share price has dropped to near $88, back towards its initial public offering price of of $83, after a soaring market debut.

The artificial intelligence (AI) company's debut on the Nasdaq stock exchange on Thursday was a crucial test for the industry, and was largely seen as a success with trading beginning at $111.50  and peaking at $115.

But while Anthropic's shares flew past the allocation price on Thursday, other tech stocks suffered.

Those falls continued into Friday, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite Index opening down 1.3%. The S&P 500 also opened down 0.4% while the Dow Jones Industrial Average opened slightly higher.

The maker of the Claude chatbot raised a record $81bn in its initial public offering (IPO), but its share priced move significantly in the first day of trading, ending 17% up at $97.17.

Analysts said volatility was to be expected in the first days or even weeks of Anthropic's trading, as the company was the first frontier AI firm to go public so had no direct peers.

Some have also noted the similarities with Elon Musk's SpaceX, which at the time of its debut in June was the largest IPO on record. Shares in the rocket and AI company experienced large swings in its first few weeks of trading.

"There's going to be some volatility like we had with SpaceX," Rob Hackel, chief executive of financial advisory and brokerage firm RF Lafferty & Co told Reuters.

"A lot of people are going to have FOMO [Fear Of Missing Out]. I don't think there's any need to get on it on the first trade."
