Here’s what you need to know: Will Jacks has taken 24 wickets in T20 internationals for England

England all-rounder Will Jacks has been ruled out of the rest of the tour of Australia with a broken hand.

The 28-year-old was injured after four balls of the penultimate over as Australia took victory in the first T20 international in Melbourne on Saturday.

Jacks was hurt trying to take a caught-and-bowled chance off Ben Dwarshuis.

The injury means versatile Tom Banton will skipper the side for the fifth and final T20 in Canberra on 2 December.

Jacks had been pencilled in for the job as regular captain Harry Brook will leave Australia early to prepare for the tour of South Africa.

England begin the first of three Tests against South Africa on 17 December.
