More than 20 young people in the UK were infected with a severe strain of meningitis B in March. 

Scientists say they have now discovered what made this particular strain so dangerous. 

The outbreak was traced back to a nightclub in Canterbury and is believed to have been transmitted between people by kissing, or sharing drinks and vapes.
