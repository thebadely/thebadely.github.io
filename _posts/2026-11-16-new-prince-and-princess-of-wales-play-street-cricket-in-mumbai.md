The Prince and Princess of Wales played a game of street cricket in a business district in Mumbai, as they began a three-day official visit to India.

They were joined by cricketing superstar Sachin Tendulkar, to the delight of local children who only found out who their VIP guests were at the last minute.

It marks the first time in four years that the royal couple has been on an official overseas trip together - following Catherine's treatment for cancer.

The pair will attend a number of engagements over the coming days, including events for the Earthshot Prize which was founded by Prince William.

They are also set to meet Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi later in the week.
