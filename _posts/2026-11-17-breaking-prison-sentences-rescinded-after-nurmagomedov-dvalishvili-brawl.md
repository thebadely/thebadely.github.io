The eight people who received prison sentences for their part in a mass brawl involving MMA fighters Usman Nurmagomedov and Merab Dvalishvili have been released.

Last week, former UFC bantamweight champion Dvalishvili and PFL lightweight champion Nurmagomedov were involved in a mass brawl in Abu Dhabi, which led to 10 arrests.

A video of the incident appeared to show Russian Nurmagomedov slap Dvalishvili after approaching the Georgian from behind.

Fighting then broke out between the two and members of their teams in a hotel lobby.

The Abu Dhabi Criminal Court sentenced, external eight of those arrested to six months in prison, along with a fine and a deportation order following their jail terms. 

But in a new statement, external, the court said "the revised penalty" was now a fine for each defendant.

Abu Dhabi news outlet the National reported, external Nurmagomedov was one of those released along with seven of his associates.

"The ruling is consistent with the legal framework governing the criminal case and considers the settlement between the parties and their withdrawal of mutual claims and disputes arising from the incident," the statement read.

"The court had previously convicted six defendants of disturbing public security, spreading panic and endangering people's lives, while two others were convicted of using information technology to film and publish footage of the brawl online."

Dvalishvili released a video after the incident calling Nurmagomedov and his team "snakes" as he received treatment on his bloodied face.

He later posted a message on X, external saying he hoped the authorities would "release everyone" and that he would not be pressing charges.

The Georgian fought at the Real American Freestyle (RAF) event in the United Arab Emirates on Saturday, where he beat Brazilian Marlon Moraes.
