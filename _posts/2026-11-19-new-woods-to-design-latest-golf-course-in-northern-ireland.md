Tiger Woods is to design Northern Ireland's first new championship links golf course in more than a century. 

On Thursday, Woods and his course architecture firm TGR Design unveiled plans for Bellarena Golf Links on the Causeway Coast.

The course will be located on Northern Ireland's north-west coast, about 20 miles west of three-time Open Championship venue Royal Portrush.

Fifteen-time major champion Woods said the opportunity to create a new course in one of golf's most celebrated destinations was a special one.

"Northern Ireland is home to some of the greatest links golf in the world, and building one on the Causeway Coast is a special opportunity for our team," said Woods.

"We want Bellarena to feel as though it has always been part of this coastline, with a course that challenges the best players and is fun for everyone who plays it."

The development, a partnership between Galgorm Collection and US-based Reef Destinations, is expected to break ground in 2027, subject to planning approval, with a target opening date of July 2029.

The course is expected to open in 2029

"To have Tiger Woods and his company design a golf course in Northern Ireland is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, it's unbelievable," said Galgorm Collection chief executive Colin Johnston. 

"It's bringing together fantastic companies and partners and creating something that will benefit Northern Ireland for many years to come."

Johnston said the dramatic setting was one of the key reasons the site stood out.

"The backdrop of Binevenagh Mountain and the views over Lough Foyle and into the Donegal hills are just stunning," he said. 

The project could provide a boost to tourism in the north west. 

"We know golf tourism is huge and we know it's close to capacity," Johnston said. 

"If we want to bring more people to this part of the world, we need to increase that capacity, and it needs to be at the elite level people expect when they come here."

Johnston believes Bellarena can complement Northern Ireland's world-renowned venues such as Royal Portrush and Royal County Down.

"This is about bringing something new and exciting to this part of the world, bringing more people here and increasing tourism overall," he added.

Unlike some exclusive championship venues, Bellarena is expected to operate as a non-member, pay-and-play course.

TGR has designed courses in the United States but Bellarena will be the firm's first project in Europe.
