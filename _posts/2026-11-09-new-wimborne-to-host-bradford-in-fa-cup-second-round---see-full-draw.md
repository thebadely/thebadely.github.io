Live reaction as Wimborne Town draw Bradford City

Seventh-tier side Wimborne Town will host League One Bradford City in the second round of the FA Cup. 

The Magpies shocked Crawley Town on Sunday in the first round, scoring twice to dump their League Two opponents out of the competition and progress to the second round for the first time.

The Bantams thumped Marine 7-0 on Saturday and sit seventh in the third tier.

Plymouth Parkway, also from the seventh tier, beat Berkhamsted 2-1 on Monday night to set up a tie against League Two high-flyers Barnet. 

Elsewhere in the draw, former Premier League and FA Cup winners Leicester City will travel to National League South side Dover Athletic.

Fresh from beating Sholing 6-0, Salisbury FC of the sixth tier will face League One Blackpool at the Ray Mac Stadium. 

National League side AFC Fylde will host Jack Wilshere's Luton Town and seventh-tier Guiseley AFC will take on Notts County of League One.

The second round will take place on the weekend starting Friday, 4 December.
