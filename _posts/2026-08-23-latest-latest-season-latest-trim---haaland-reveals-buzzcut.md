Whether it be Zlatan Ibrahimovic, Gareth Bale or Erling Haaland, football has witnessed some iconic man buns over the years.

However, Haaland has decided to begin the new Premier League season with a new - and very different - look.

The Manchester City striker revealed on social media that he had shed his famous blonde locks, posting the caption: "New season, new trim."

Haaland will be hoping his new look brings even more goals as he aims to get off the mark when Enzo Maresca's side face Bournemouth at 14:00 BST on Sunday.
