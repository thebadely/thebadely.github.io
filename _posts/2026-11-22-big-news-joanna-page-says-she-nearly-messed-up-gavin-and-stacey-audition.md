It has been recently reported that The Swansea actress says she was asked to read for the part in a Cardiff accent

Joanna Page has said she nearly messed up her Gavin And Stacey audition after failing to do a Cardiff accent.

The 49-year-old rose to fame after starring as Stacey in the BBC comedy opposite creators James Corden and Ruth Jones.

Page, from Swansea, told campmates on ITV's I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here! that she had initially read some scenes for Jones using her actual accent.

She recounted that she was then asked to do it in a Cardiff accent, as that was where her character was supposed to be from, but Jones told her it sounded "awful" and to "just be you".

Speaking about her preparations for the life-changing audition, she said: "I remember reading the script, Ruth and James had written it and I remember thinking, 'This is me, she is me, I can do this'."

Page said she got the offer for the part in the Bafta-winning sitcom two weeks after being rejected from a West End show.

She said: "I was working in a shoe shop in East Dulwich and I'd just missed out on getting the part in Dirty Dancing The Musical.

"And so if I'd have got that, I never, ever would have done it."

Page also recalled her first impression of Mathew Horne - who played Gavin in the series - during their chemistry read and said "he seemed really cool, and I thought I'm not cool and I'm just a nerd".

The hit series aired for three seasons from 2007 until 2009 followed by Christmas specials including the series finale in 2024.

Joanna Page is taking part in the 26th series of I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!

Page's autobiography, entitled Lush, recounts her youth in a small village near Swansea, graduating from the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (Rada) and early stage and screen performances before her big break in Gavin and Stacey.

Her TV and film credits include romantic comedy Love Actually, Nativity 2: Danger in the Manger and Doctor Who, where she played Queen Elizabeth I in The Day of the Doctor, and she also does presenting and podcasting.
