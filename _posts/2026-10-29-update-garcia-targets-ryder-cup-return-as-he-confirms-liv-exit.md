Sergio Garcia has become the latest big name to quit LIV Golf while targeting a return to Europe's Ryder Cup team.

The one-time major champion said he will play on the Europe-based DP World Tour in 2027.

Garcia won a combined 27 titles on the American and European circuits between 1999 and 2020, including the 2017 Masters.

He joined LIV in June 2022 after it launched as a Saudi Arabia-backed project but the former world number two will not be part of the second phase of the breakaway circuit.
