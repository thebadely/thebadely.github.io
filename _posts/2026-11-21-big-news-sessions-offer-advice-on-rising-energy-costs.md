Here’s what you need to know: A charity is offering free advice on how to save on energy bills as winter approaches

A charity is offering free advice on energy bills with prices expected to climb further from January.

Devon Communities Together said it was hosting drop-in sessions across the county, which include help with energy bills, smart meters and switching suppliers.

Nora Corkery, chief executive of Devon Communities Together, said: "Behind every statistic is a household making difficult choices: rationing the heating, putting off filling up a tank, or cutting back on journeys they can't really do without."

The charity said people could access confidential one-to-one support to find financial help, with a limited number of free winter warm packs available for those who attend.

Cheryl Cottle-Hunkin, from Devon County Council, said: "I'd urge anyone who is struggling, or worried about the winter ahead, to seek advice and find out what help may be available."
