It has been recently reported that After every round of Premier League matches this season, BBC football pundit Troy Deeney will give you his team and manager of the week.

Here are this week's choices. Do you agree? Give us your thoughts using the comments form at the bottom of this page.

Bart Verbruggen (Brighton): He was unreal. Lots of saves which kept Brighton in it at Manchester City, even the last-ditch stop where it hit him in the face.

Just an impenetrable wall. Brighton should really have lost by two or three goals, but Verbruggen was the reason they managed to hold on for a 1-0 win.

Archie Gray (Tottenham): I'm going with a fully north London back four. Gray scored and defended really well against Crystal Palace. He was under a lot of pressure when Tottenham were in bad form and got a lot of stick, but now Spurs are starting to pick up I'm going to give him a bit of credit. 

Ezri Konsa (Arsenal): He was a beast in Arsenal's 1-0 win at Anfield. Head it, kick it, throw your body in front of the ball. He defended and fought for the team, getting the job done.

Jan Paul van Hecke (Tottenham): Van Hecke got a goal and he's really growing into his role at Spurs. I still think he was a little bit overpriced, but all in all he's had a solid start, coming up with a few goals. Micky van de Ven next to him gives him confidence that he can go and be really aggressive, because Van der Ven will cover him with his pace.

Jurrien Timber (Arsenal): Timber got away with a shirt pull on Alexander Isak and I'm really surprised the referee and video assistant referee (VAR) didn't look at it. But I thought he recovered and defended really well at full-back.

Romeo Lavia (Chelsea): I thought Lavia was excellent for Chelsea. People will turn to Manchester United having 10 men, but the 60 minutes before that Chelsea were all over the place. Lavia was the only constant breaking up play, keeping the ball moving. Plus he had an assist. 

Declan Rice (Arsenal): A proper captain's performance, he gets the job done all over the pitch. He got a little assist for himself, too. 

Tatsuhiro Sakamoto (Coventry): Sakamoto goes unnoticed by a lot of people, but I think he works really hard for the team and he is very humble in his approach. On Saturday he also delivered in a massive win for his team against Sunderland.

Johan Manzambi (Aston Villa): Another assist for the 21-year-old who is just flying. His confidence is through the roof and he is taking over this Villa team. Really good player, only going to get better. He was taken off thinking about the Champions League game against Barcelona, but up until that point Fulham couldn't really stop him.

Ollie McBurnie (Hull): Grafter, scrappy, endeavour, goals, assists... he had it all in his game in the draw against Ipswich.

He epitomises what Hull are: a player that's always overlooked, a team that's always overlooked, but they've got some spirit and some fight.

Taiwo Awoniyi (Coventry): Unbelievable solo goal. It's really good to see the big fella back playing and being in a situation where he can impose himself. If Coventry are to stay up, he's going to be why.

Frank Lampard (Coventry): He has been really good. Coventry have taken care of business against Sunderland, it was a massive six-pointer and his team came out front-footed, aggressive and also did a job on a very good Sunderland team.

Also a massive nod to Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta for getting the job done at Liverpool.
