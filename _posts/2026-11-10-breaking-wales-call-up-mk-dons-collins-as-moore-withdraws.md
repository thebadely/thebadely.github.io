Wales have called up uncapped MK Dons striker Aaron Collins for this month's Nations League matches following Wrexham frontman Kieffer Moore's withdrawal due to injury.

Moore, 34, left the field during Wrexham's 1-1 Championship draw with Wolves on Saturday.

Wales have not specified what kind of injury the 57-cap forward has sustained.

He is replaced in the Wales squad by Collins, who has scored 17 goals in 16 games across all competitions for League One side Dons this season, including four goals in a game against Doncaster last month.

The 29-year-old former Newport County player joins Craig Bellamy's squad for concluding Group A4 games in Norway and at home to Portugal.

Newport-born Collins has played for 10 different - mainly lower league - clubs since leaving Rodney Parade for Wolverhampton Wanderers in 2016.

Wales, who are looking to preserve their place in the Nations League's top tier, face Norway in Oslo on Saturday before finishing their campaign at home against Portugal next Tuesday.

Wales are third in Group A4, with their only win from four matches so far coming against World Cup quarter-finalists Norway in Cardiff last month.

Bellamy is without midfielder Jordan James but has influential Leeds pair Harry Wilson and Joe Rodon back, along with Cardiff playmaker Rubin Colwill.

Bellamy's side could still finish in second, third or fourth place.

A top-two spot would take Wales to the Nations League quarter-finals in March 2027.

Finishing third would mean a relegation play-off against a League B team in March, while finishing bottom of the group would result in either a relegation play-off or direct relegation, depending on the ranking with other League A groups.
