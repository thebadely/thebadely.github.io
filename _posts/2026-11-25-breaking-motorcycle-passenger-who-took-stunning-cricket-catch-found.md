Greatest catch ever? Man on motorbike takes incredible catch

"I told my friend riding the motorcycle to duck his head. I'm going to catch it."

Mehran Khan amazed sports fans around the world this week when he caught a cricket ball while sitting on the back of a moving motorcycle.

Mehran was passing a match in the Swat Premier League in Mingora, Pakistan, when a ball sailed out of the ground towards him, with the passenger reaching out to catch it one-handed.

Speaking to the BBC, he said of the grab, which has gone viral online: "I didn't think I would actually catch it." 

Mehran was on his way home from work at the Public Health Engineering Department when he saw the ball being struck.

"I'm a cricket fan," he said. "I play cricket myself and I watch it too. I saw that Abrar Lala was at the crease and then he hit a six.

"I told him [the friend who was driving] to duck his head. I'm going to catch it. Then I caught it.

"I made the effort, but I didn't expect to catch it. I thought it would fall somewhere nearby, but by the grace of God I managed to catch it."

According to Mehran, immediately after the catch the Swat Premier League organisers announced a prize of 10,000 Pakistani rupees (about Â£27.60) for him, funded by the Pakistan Army.

The Marghazar resident was caught by surprise when the recording of the catch spread so quickly online, saying: "The video went viral overnight. I didn't even know about it."

He expressed his happiness at receiving the award, saying he was grateful to God, and added that he had returned the ball after taking the catch.

"People are saying I was taking the ball away with me, but that's not true. I returned it," he said.

Swat Strikers won the match against GKR Dir by 34 runs, and Mehran urged the organisers to continue holding the tournament every year, describing it as an "excellent competition".

Mehran Khan is from Marghazar in the Swat region of northern Pakistan

There have been several remarkable catches by spectators at cricket matches in the past.

In 1987, during a match in Australia, a cameraman casually caught one of former England all-rounder Ian Botham's sixes while holding a cigarette in his mouth.

In 2014, during a New Zealand domestic match, a spectator slid down a slope and completed a one-handed catch.

Last year, in a match between Australia and South Africa, a fan caught a ball with one hand while holding two drink cans in the other.

Mehran's one-handed catch while sitting on a moving motorcycle will join them in cricketing history.
