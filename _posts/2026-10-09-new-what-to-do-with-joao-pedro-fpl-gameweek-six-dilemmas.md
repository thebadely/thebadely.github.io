Despite being the most sold player of the international break, Chelsea forward Pedro is still owned by about 65% of FPL managers. Fernandes has the best fixture and the best chance of a big score, but not many own him. Of the rest, you can talk me into any of them. I am leaning safe and sticking with Haaland. Even in a tougher game, if Man City score he should be the one involved.
