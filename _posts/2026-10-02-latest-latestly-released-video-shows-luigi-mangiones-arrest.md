Newly released bodycam footage shows the moment Luigi Mangione is arrested in a McDonald's restaurant, days after the killing of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson.

In one clip, a police officer can be seen finding a gun magazine in a backpack belonging to Mangione. Later, officers are also seen finding a gun in the backpack.

Mangione has pleaded guilty to federal charges relating to Thompson's killing.
