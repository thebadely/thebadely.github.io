Experts revealed that Stafford McDowall takes over as captain from the injured Rory Darge

Stafford McDowall was working on his dad's farm when he got the call to play for Scotland against Australia on Sunday - and six days later will be captain against Japan after Rory Darge suffered a late injury blow.

It is the second change of captain head coach Gregor Townsend has had to make in a week after Sione Tuipulotu was ruled out of the Nations Championship matches against Australia and Japan.

Darge produced a man-of-the-match performance against the Wallabies in his place and the Glasgow Warriors forward was initially named again in the starting line-up despite a shoulder injury.

But, following further assessment and discussion within the Scotland camp, it has been decided he will sit out the game against Japan at Murrayfield.

Club-mate Matt Fagerson will start instead at number eight, with his place on the bench filled by Edinburgh flanker Freddy Douglas and Newcastle Red Bulls' Ollie Leatherbarrow moving forward in the back row.

Glasgow centre McDowall takes over as captain and revealed that he had returned to help out on his father's farm in Dumfries not expecting to come off the bench against the Wallabies as a second-half substitute.

"Originally I was not involved at the weekend, not travelling reserve, so I took the opportunity to go home and help dad out," he said. 

"I got there for about 12 hours, got some work done on the Saturday morning, then got the call at about 11 o'clock in the morning. Quick shower, then up the road.

"Dad wasn't too happy. I phoned him at four o'clock and asked what he was doing and he said he was doing all the jobs he had lined up for me. 

"But it was awesome. Dad came up for the game the next day, so he said it worked out better than me doing jobs on the farm anyway.

"It was class. It was a bit of a whirlwind weekend."

Townsend had already made 10 changes for Japan's visit with several top players, including fly-half Finn Russell, rested before deciding not to risk Darge.

Darge will remain in the squad with the aim of being involved next week, when the Nations Championship comes to a conclusion.

Scotland are fourth in the Northern Hemisphere group and could yet be involved in a play-off for third, fifth, seventh or ninth places depending on this weekend's results. 

Starting XV: Ollie Smith; Darcy Graham, Stafford McDowall (capt), Rory Hutchinson, Kyle Rowe; Tom Jordan, Jamie Dobie; Nathan McBeth, Gregor Hiddleston, Zander Fagerson, Jonny Gray, Max Williamson, Liam McConnell, Ollie Leatherbarrow, Matt Fagerson.

Replacements: Seb Stephen, Rory Sutherland, Will Hurd, Gregor Brown, Magnus Bradbury, Freddy Douglas, George Horne, Blair Kinghorn.
