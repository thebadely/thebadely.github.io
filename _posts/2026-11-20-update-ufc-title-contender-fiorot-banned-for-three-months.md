Highly ranked UFC flyweight Manon Fiorot has been banned for three months for violating the promotion's anti-doping policy.

The UFC issued a statement on Thursday saying the 36-year-old Frenchwoman had accepted the ban after testing positive for low levels of Ostarine.

Fiorot, ranked fourth in the division, tested positive on 12 September, the day she fought and lost to Alexa Grasso in a title eliminator bout.

Combat Sports Anti-Doping's (CSAD) statement explained Fiorot said her nutritionist supplied several dietary supplements that were new to her routine in fight week and tests ran on those showed trace amounts of Ostarine.

A second sample collected on 16 October, prior to Fiorot being notified of the violation, returned negative results.
