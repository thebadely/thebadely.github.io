Here’s what you need to know: A woman died at the scene of the crash on Rugeley Road in Hednesford

Two police forces are being investigated after a 21-year-old woman died in a crash following a police pursuit in Staffordshire.

The front-seat passenger of a Seat Ibiza, which had been followed by officers, died at the scene of the crash in Rugeley Road, Hednesford, just after 23:00 GMT on Friday, Staffordshire Police said.

The force said the Seat collided with a Vauxhall Astra, which was also being driven by a member of the public, after the police pursuit.

A 23-year-old man from the Walsall area was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving. The force said the Astra driver was helping with police inquiries.

The incident has been referred to the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) due to the police involvement before the collision.

The IOPC has confirmed it is investigating the involvement of both West Midlands Police and Staffordshire Police, and added it would be contacting the woman's family to offer its condolences and explain its role.

West Midlands Ambulance Service took two other people from the scene to hospital with injuries not believed to be life-threatening or life-changing.

The woman's next-of-kin have been informed and are being supported by trained police officers.

The route has reopened to traffic after being cordoned off following the incident.

Staffordshire Police has appealed to witnesses who were in the area at the time or saw the vehicles beforehand to get in touch.

The IOPC began its independent investigation just after midnight on Saturday after being alerted by police, and sent investigators to the scene to begin gathering evidence.

In a statement, an IOPC spokesperson said they understood a police patrol car had begun a pursuit after a Seat Ibiza, believed to be being driven using cloned plates, after it failed to stop for police in Hednesford.

"During the pursuit, the Seat crashed and collided with a Vauxhall Astra."

The investigation would look at "police actions" prior to the fatal collision, they added.
