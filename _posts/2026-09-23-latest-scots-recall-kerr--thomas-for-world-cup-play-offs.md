According to new developments, Martha Thomas (left) and Sam Kerr (right) return to the Scotland squad for the first time since October

Sam Kerr and Martha Thomas have been named in their first Scotland squad in almost a year, as the experienced duo return for next month's Women's World Cup round one two-legged play-off against the Czech Republic.

Melissa Andreatta's side travel to Pardubice, east of Prague, on Friday, 9 October before welcoming the Czechs to St Mirren Park four days later, eyeing progression to another two-legged play-off against either Sweden or Lithuania with a place at next summer's tournament in Brazil at stake.

Liverpool midfielder Kerr - who has been playing out of position at right-back in the WSL - has yet to feature under Scotland women's Australian head coach, with the last of her 34 caps coming in February 2025 off the bench against the Netherlands, while Thomas - who joined WSL2 side Newcastle United in the summer - earned her 50th cap in October, in a friendly defeat by Switzerland.

Club-mate Freya Gregory - and Como defender Sophie Howard - who were forced to pull out of the last camp for the qualifying double-header against Israel, are also back in the squad.

Celtic midfielder, Maria McAneny - who last featured when scoring in a seven-goal thrashing of Luxembourg in March, returns too.

Defenders Leah Eddie and Kenzie Weir drop out, as do strikers Eilidh Adams and Laura Berry.

Mia McAulay was a late addition for the June double-header, but the Aston Villa youngster retains her place.

Andreatta's unbeaten side topped their qualifying group on goal difference - one better off than second-placed Belgium - and are now four games away from their first major tournament since the 2019 World Cup, having missed the past two European Championships and the 2023 World Cup.
