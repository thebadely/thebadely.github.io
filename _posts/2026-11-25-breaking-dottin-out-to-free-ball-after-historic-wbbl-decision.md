Let's dive into the details: Dottin's wicket was the third to fall in six balls as Melbourne Renegades' innings faltered

A bizarre dismissal in the Women's Big Bash saw Brisbane Heat's Sarah Glenn bowling to an empty set of stumps, external after Melbourne Renegades new batter Deandra Dottin was ruled to have taken too long to face the delivery.

Experienced West Indies all-rounder Dottin walked out to bat, following the dismissal of Georgia Wareham, with her equipment in disarray and proceeded to adjust her helmet and begin putting on her gloves after arriving in the middle.

Rules introduced before the 2021 WBBL season to speed up play state that an incoming batter must be ready to face - or the non-striker to receive - the next delivery within 75 seconds of a dismissal or retirement.

The umpires adjudged Dottin to have exceeded this time limit and instructed her to stand aside from stumps, while awarding Glenn a free ball.

Glenn hit the middle and leg stumps to remove Dottin for a golden duck. The bowler was initially uncertain how to celebrate - she laughed and covered her face with her hands - before being mobbed by her team-mates.

However, despite the wicket reducing the Renegades to 97-5 with 41 runs needed from 34 balls, they completed their chase of 138 off the final delivery of the match to win by three wickets.
