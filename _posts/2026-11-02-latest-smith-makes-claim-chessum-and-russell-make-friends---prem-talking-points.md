The viral moment of the weekend came at The Rec.

Defeat by Gloucester last weekend meant that Bath fly-half Finn Russell had been denied his traditional shushing of the Shed at Kingsholm.

He clearly had some housery to get out of his system.

Midway through the first half, with Bath having just retaken the lead against Sale, Russell gathered George Ford's chip ahead and, as the referee's whistle blew for a penalty for the visitors, bumped the chasing Sale fly-half to the floor with a shoulder to the chest.

Russell then dropped the ball on to Ford's head as his opposite number attempted to get to his feet., external

A melee ensued. Russell escaped censure.

Referee Anthony Woodthorpe could have punished Russell with a penalty for breaching the spirit of sportsmanship, advancing Sale up the pitch and giving them a shot at a line-out from close range - the source of their only try in the match to that point.

It wouldn't have changed the outcome of the match. Sale failed to capitalise on a similar platform a few minutes later.

But it might discourage such cheap-shot taunts as Russell's.
