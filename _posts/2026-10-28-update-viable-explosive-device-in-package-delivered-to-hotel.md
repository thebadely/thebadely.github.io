Experts revealed that The package was discovered on Tuesday afternoon

A suspect package delivered to a hotel in Londonderry contained a "viable" explosive device, police have said.

Army bomb experts were sent to the hotel at Ebrington Square in the Waterside after the package was discovered at about 15:20 GMT on Tuesday.

They carried out a controlled explosion on the device, which was later taken away for a forensic examination.

Police Service of Northern Ireland Det Supt Kearney condemned it as a "reckless and targeted attack".

The hotel had to be evacuated along with staff from neighbouring premises

The detective said that a number of people were evacuated from The Ebrington hotel, along with staff from neighbouring premises.

The chair of a local policing watchdog said he believed it may be linked to the Ministry of Defence (MoD) having a stall at a careers fare at the same hotel last month.

A Halloween funfair had been taking place a short distance away from the hotel.

It remained open during the security alert.

Teresa Boyle said no one wants to return to the dark days of the past

"No one wants it and no one supports it," she said.
