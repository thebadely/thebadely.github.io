Average income for islanders is 12% lower in real terms than five years previously, a survey says.

More than 2,000 people responded to the 2023/24 Household Expenditure Survey to find the average cost of living in Guernsey.

The survey said 41% of respondents reported having either no savings or less than one month's worth of income in savings.

The survey added on average, people in the affordable rental or partial ownership market spent 33% of gross income on housing-related issues, while private market renters averaged 22% and owners with mortgages averaged 19%.

Helen Walton, head of the Data and Analysis Service, said the data was "important" as it helped make sure inflation figures remained accurate and reflected the reality of what islanders spend money on. 

"It also provides an updated data set to help inform policy decisions and market research," she said.
