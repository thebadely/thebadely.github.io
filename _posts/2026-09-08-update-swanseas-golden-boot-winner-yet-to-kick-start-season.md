So is now the time?

With Stewart injured for Tuesday's game against his former side, and Swansea likely to rotate personnel as they play a third match in seven days, Matos was asked whether this was the game to reintroduce Vipotnik to the starting line-up.

"We need to see what is best for the team," said Matos, offering a smile but no commitment.

What he did offer, though, was conviction that the player of last season is still there in more ways than one.

"Besides the goals, he contributes a lot for the other parts of the game," said Matos.

"So all that is coming - then it's just a matter of scoring and he will score for sure."

Swansea would welcome that as they look to show their impressive start should be taken seriously.
