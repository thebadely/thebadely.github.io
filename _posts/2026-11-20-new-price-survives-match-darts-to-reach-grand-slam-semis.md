Here’s what you need to know: Gerwyn Price won the last of his three Grand Slam of Darts titles in 2021 

Gerwyn Price faced two match darts before winning a thrilling quarter-final against Nico Springer at the Grand Slam of Darts, as Luke Littler's conqueror Jermaine Wattimena was knocked out.

Three-time champion Price led for most of the match but his German opponent refused to let him out of reach.

Springer missed a number of chances to level, including three darts at double top to make it 13-13 as he fell 14-12 behind, before he eventually made it 14-14 and then took a 15-14 lead.

However, Price produced a stunning 143 checkout to take it to a decider in which Springer had the throw.

The German left himself 73 after his 16th 180, only to miss double eight and double four for the match prior to Price taking out 78 with treble 18 and double 12.

"I'm quite lucky to come through that game," Price told Sky Sports. "I missed so many chances - I should've punished him.

"I kept him in the game, I kept missing doubles after doubles. Even though he was scoring as well as I was, I was missing opportunities after opportunities but I'm glad I had another one at the end."

Price will play Kevin Doets in the semi-finals after the Dutchman beat compatriot Wattimena, who beat world number one and world champion Littler in the second round.

Doets was 5-4 down before taking a dominant hold of the match with a remarkable 10-leg burst.

He hit two 170 checkouts as he surged into a 14-5 lead before Wattimena managed to temporarily halt him by taking the 20th leg.

However, Doets quickly went on to wrap up victory as he finished off the 23rd leg with a 180 - his eighth of the match - and double 18.

"The beginning was very tight but, after the 5-5, I just felt like I was walking away with it," said Doets.

When talking about his 170 finishes, he added: "It's amazing to do that - it feels really good to do that."

Saturday's quarter-finals will involve Gian van Veen playing Nathan Aspinall and Michael van Gerwen facing Tom Sykes, who beat Luke Humphries.
