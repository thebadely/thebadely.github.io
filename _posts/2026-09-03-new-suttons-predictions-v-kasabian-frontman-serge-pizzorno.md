Since then, they have seen their side reach the Champions League quarter-finals, lift the FA Cup and Community Shield, win promotion from the Championship and suffer three relegations, including last season when they went down to League One.

What comes next?

"Promotion!" said Serge. "It has to be. I love the play-offs, so a win at Wembley would do me."

A new-look Leicester side took their first step towards bouncing back when they picked up their opening win of the season against Plymouth on Tuesday, but Serge added Russell Martin's youthful side will need time to fully find their feet in the third tier.

"I hope they get it," he said. "Patience is so important in football, and young teams should play with no fear."
