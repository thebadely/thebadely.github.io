Scotland fell to a final-over, seven-wicket defeat by the Netherlands in their opening match in the Super Six stage of the Women's Emerging Nations Trophy. 

The Scots, who qualified second in their group behind Sri Lanka in Dubai, reached 154-7 in their innings.

But the Dutch, who were second behind Pakistan in their section, surpassed that for the loss of just three wickets, albeit with only two balls to spare.

Scotland captain Kathryn Bryce said: "I feel like the pitch changed significantly in the second innings, but I still felt we had a total on the board that we should have been able to defend.

"We weren't able to have a good period of pressure on them - probably gave them too many boundary balls early on and they batted nicely.

"It's not great margins. It's just being a little bit sharper and more switched on in different areas."

Dutch captain Sterre Kalis led from the front with an unbeaten, 27-ball 52 as her side go second in the table behind Sri Lanka.

Scotland had reached 60 before the loss of the first wicket, Katherine Fraser having hit 25.

However, the run rate declined after captain Bryce was bowled for 34 with her side on 123-4, although Pippa Sproul finished unbeaten on 23.

The Dutch put on 87 for the first wicket before Sanya Khurana was run out for 41.

Fellow opener Phebe Molkenboer helped add seven more runs to the total before she was out for 43.

But Kalis took up the challenge with six fours and two sixes in her half-century before the victory was sealed with a two leg-byes from the fourth ball of the final over.

Scotland's second defeat of the tournament leaves them sitting third in the table before Pakistan and Ireland play their game in hand later in the day.

The Scots next face Pakistan on Sunday as the Dutch take on Sri Lanka.
