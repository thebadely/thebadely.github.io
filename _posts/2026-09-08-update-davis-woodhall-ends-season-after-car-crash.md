Olympic long jump champion Tara Davis-Woodhall has decided to end her 2026 season after suffering "severe concussion" in a car crash. 

The decision means the 27-year-old American will miss this week's World Athletics Ultimate Championship in Hungary. 

"This one hurts. I'm sad, disappointed and just hurt by all of it," wrote Davis-Woodhall on social media., external "I know this has to be part of my story. 

"There has to be a reason for this. I'll never questions God's path for me. I will be back. Just a hitch in my giddy-up."

Davis-Woodhall won long jump gold at the 2024 Olympics in Paris, before topping the podium at the Worlds in Tokyo a year later.

She was involved in a "very scary" head-on collision in August when she was on her way to training.

Afterwards, she said her injuries were not life-threatening but that both cars, which were going at about 20mph, were written off.
