Here’s what you need to know: Florentino Luis made 33 appearances for Burnley in all competitions last season

Newly promoted Ipswich Town have signed Portuguese defensive midfielder Florentino Luis from Burnley for an undisclosed fee.

The 26-year-old, who made 31 league appearances last season for a relegated Burnley side, has signed a five-year contract at Portman Road. 

Fellow Premier League newcomers Hull City were also interested in the player. 

"It feels brilliant to be a part of this club," Florentino said.

"I like how the club sees the future and there are a lot of good players here. I can bring experience and balance to the team, between defence and attack, and I like to bring the connection." 

Ipswich manager Gary O'Neil added: "He brings experience of playing in the Premier League as well as other major competitions in Europe, including the Champions League, and makes us stronger in central midfield."
