Experts revealed that Judoka Summer Shaw has her sights set on the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics

"Someone said to me: 'It's crazy that you can win a Commonwealth medal and then come back to work.'"

So 22-year-old Summer Shaw decided to make a TikTok video, external about her swift change of scenery while on a work break at a McDonald's restaurant.

The five-second clip that Shaw posted "for funsies" went viral, and has been watched over one million times. Then came the comments...

"People were asking: 'How is this possible?'

"They were like: 'This shouldn't be allowed.'"

Shaw won Scotland's first judo medal at the recent Commonwealth Games in Glasgow, a bronze in the 48kg event.

"It's so cool when it's something you've dreamed of doing since you were eight years old - and suddenly you're there," Shaw told BBC Sport.

"To get there was crazy, and to come away with a medal was even better."

Shaw won that medal on the last Friday of the Games. The next week, she was back at the family home in Surrey - and back to work.

"I picked up that part-time job [at McDonald's] when I was studying for my A-levels," said Shaw, who qualifies to represent Scotland through her father's side of the family.

She has recently been based largely in Birmingham while studying for a biology degree. That means she has been close to the British Judo Olympic and Paralympic National Training Centre in Walsall.

"They've been really flexible with me, letting me work when I can. They've been really good with me," she said.

"It's mostly seasonal because my job's back at home, but when I am back it is like two, three shifts a week.

"It's hard to find jobs like it though that are flexible with your hours. I've heard from other people in their part-time jobs saying their boss won't give them the weekend off."
