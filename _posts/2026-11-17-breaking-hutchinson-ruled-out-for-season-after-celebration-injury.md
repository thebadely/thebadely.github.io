Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson will miss the rest of the NFL season after sustaining a serious knee injury while celebrating.

Hutchinson, 26, jumped in the air after bringing down New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye in the first quarter of the Lions' 35-10 win in Munich.

The 2022 second overall pick fell to the ground shortly after landing, clutching his right knee.

Hutchinson walked to the sideline after medical treatment on the field but did not return to the game - and has torn his anterior cruciate ligament.

It is the second season-ending injury of Hutchinson career - but Lions head coach Dan Campbell has no plans to stop his players from expressing themselves.

"I hate it. I think we all understand what it is, and let's be smart, but no, I'm not gonna say 'nobody celebrates any more'," Campbell said.

"The number of moves that I've seen 'Hutch' make and the number of plyometrics, explosive movements, the spins, the one-leg lateral movements, lateral balances... it was one of those things, man."

This was the sixth regular-season NFL game to be played in Germany and the third to be held at Bayern Munich's Allianz Arena. 

The Lions' win means they are top of the NFC North and have the second-best record in the conference.

Hutchinson is regarded as one of the premier pass rushers in the NFL, and is currently tied for 13th in the league with six sacks this season.

Campbell is confident Hutchinson will come back stronger on his return from the injury setback. 

"He's such a phenomenal player, an impact player, but more importantly he's an unbelievable team-mate and he's one of our captains," Campbell added.

"What a hell of a year he was having. He's just improved every year and to come off the injury he had [in 2024]. 

"This guy will overcome this and he will come back better than he was. It's just the way he is, it's the way he's wired."

Drake Maye led the New England Patriots to Super Bowl 60, where they lost to the Seattle Seahawks

Maye reportedly sustained a fractured ankle during the tackle Hutchinson was celebrating, so the Patriots will be concerned the quarterback could miss the remainder of the season too.

Head coach Mike Vrabel confirmed Maye had remained in Germany, telling Boston radio station WEEI the 24-year-old stayed "out of caution".

After being beaten by the Seattle Seahawks in the 2026 Super Bowl, the Patriots are second in the AFC East having won five and lost five this season.

Their chances of reaching the playoffs will likely be reliant on securing a wildcard place as the Buffalo Bills currently top the division with an 8-1 record.
