Durham have appointed Ottis Gibson as their head coach on a three-year deal.

The 57-year-old takes over from Ryan Campbell, who moved up to the director of cricket role in September.

Campbell led the team to promotion back to Division One of the County Championship this year before stepping into the role vacated by Marcus North leaving to join England as national selector.

Gibson, who spent two seasons with Durham at the end of his playing career in 2006 and 2007, coached Yorkshire for three seasons from 2022-2024.

"I'm super excited to be given the opportunity to be head coach of Durham at this time," he told the club website., external

"I had two fantastic seasons in Chester-le-Street as a player at a time when we had some success and I saw what that meant to the north east region.

"I believe we have a fantastic squad, a good blend of experienced senior professionals and very exciting young talent that is ready to challenge for titles across all formats in the next few years and I can't wait to get started."

Gibson played in two Tests for the West Indies and had a spell as their head coach as well as holding the same role with South Africa.

He also had two stints as England bowling coach and led Yorkshire to promotion back to Division One of the County Championship in 2024 before leaving his position.
