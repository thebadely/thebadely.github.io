James Wade won his first European Tour title since 2016 with an 8-4 victory over fellow Englishman Ross Smith in the final of the Flanders Darts Trophy.

With Luke Littler and Luke Humphries - the current world top two - not involved in Antwerp, Gian van Veen was the top seed, with Smith beating him 7-6 in the semi-finals.

Wade, 43, thrashed Danny Noppert 6-0 in the quarter-finals and defeated Damon Heta 7-4 to reach the final.

Victory in the final ended a decade-long wait for a continental title, stretching back to the 2016 European Darts Matchplay in Hamburg.
