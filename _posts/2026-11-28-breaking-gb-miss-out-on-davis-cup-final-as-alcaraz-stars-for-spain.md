It has been recently reported that Great Britain had not reached the final of the Davis Cup since they won the tournament in 2015

Great Britain were unable to give Leon Smith the perfect send-off as Davis Cup captain as Carlos Alcaraz inspired Spain to a 2-0 victory in the semi-finals.

Competing in their first Davis Cup semi-final since 2019, Britain fell short as Arthur Fery was unable to match the dazzling brilliance of Alcaraz, with the seven-time major winner surging to an emphatic 6-2 6-1 win.

That was after rising star Rafael Jodar defeated Cameron Norrie 6-2 7-5 in the opening singles match to put Spain in the driving seat in Bologna.

The defeat brings the curtain down on Smith's near 17-year tenure as captain, during which he led Britain to Davis Cup success in 2015.

Spain captain David Ferrer, who is also stepping down after this year's tournament, will have the chance to guide his nation to their first Davis Cup title since 2019 when they face Italy in Sunday's final. 

Italy are the three-time defending champions, although they are without talisman and world number two Jannik Sinner because of injury.
