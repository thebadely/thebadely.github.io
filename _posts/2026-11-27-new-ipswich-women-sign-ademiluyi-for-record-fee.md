Let's dive into the details: Ademiluyi had been on loan at the Tractor Girls from NWSL side Gotham FC since February

Ipswich Town Women have confirmed the permanent signing of England youth international Princess Ademiluyi for an undisclosed club record fee.

The 20-year-old forward has been on loan at the Tractor Girls from NWSL side Gotham FC since February and has agreed a three-and-a-half year deal at the WSL2 side.

Ademiluyi has been capped by England at under-19 and under-20 level, and has been called up for the under-23 side for the first time ahead of their internationals against Belgium and Portugal next week., external

"I have really made a home for myself in Suffolk and I am so excited to continue building what has been a positive start to the season for us as a group," she told the club's website., external

"The players, staff and supporters have all been brilliant with me since I arrived and I can't wait to see where this club can go and to continue making more memories together."

Ipswich Town chairman Mark Ashton said: "Signing Princess on a permanent basis has been a real priority for us and completing such a significant deal as this highlights the club's ongoing commitment to reach the top level of the game.

"This is a statement signing for the club, with further announcements to come in due course as we reaffirm our commitment to our women's set-up in both the short and long-term."

Ademiluyi started her career at WSL side West Ham in 2023 before moving to US-based Gotham and is Ipswich's top scorer so far in 2026-27, with four goals.

The Tractor Girls finished ninth in WSL2 last season following promotion to the second tier in 2025, and were in the fourth tier as recently as 2021., external

Under the management of former men's team player David Wright, appointed to the role in April, the Blues are third after eight games this season, three points behind leaders Newcastle United.

Ademiluyi has played an important role in helping the Tractor Girls transform from relegation candidates to title contenders.

She made an immediate impact on joining in February, scoring goals against Durham and Birmingham City to help secure the team's survival in WSL2.

The forward has continued to find the net this season, and won the WSL2 player of the month award for October., external

There is a growing feeling within the women's game that the Blues will be playing at the highest level in the not too distant future and securing a proven goalscorer whose stock is also rising on the international stage will only strengthen that train of thought.
