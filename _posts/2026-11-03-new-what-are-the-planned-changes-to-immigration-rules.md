Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood has set out plans to overhaul immigration rules.

Proposals for stricter requirements to qualify for permanent settlement in the UK were first outlined in detail in November 2025, when Mahmood was home secretary in Sir Keir Starmer's government.

She has argued that settlement should be "earned", rather than being granted automatically, with those who contribute more to the country able to settle fastest.

Following a consultation, external, Mahmood has now announced her final decision on what changes should be taken forward.

Elements of her original proposals have been revised following a backlash from some Labour MPs, with plans to make foreign care workers already in the UK wait 15 years to qualify for settlement scrapped.
