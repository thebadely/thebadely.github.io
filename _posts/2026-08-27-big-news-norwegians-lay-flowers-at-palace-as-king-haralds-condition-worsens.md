Here’s what you need to know: Norwegians have laid flowers in front of the palace in Oslo after the royal household said King Harald V's condition in hospital had become "extremely serious".

His wife, Queen Sonja, and his son, Crown Prince Haakon, cancelled royal engagements to be at his bedside at Oslo University Hospital with other members of the family. They left later in the day.

The 89-year-old has been in hospital for 10 days receiving treatment for a rare blood condition, haemolytic anaemia. Late on Thursday afternoon the palace issued an update to say his condition was unchanged.

Having been on the throne since 1991, King Harald is Europe's oldest head of state.

Members of Norway's royal family, including King Harald's son, Crown Prince Haakon, have gathered at the hospital
