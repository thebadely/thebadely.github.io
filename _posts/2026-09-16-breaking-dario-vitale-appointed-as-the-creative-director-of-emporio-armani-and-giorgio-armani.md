It has been recently reported that <p>The Armani Group has confirmed Dario Vitale as the new creative director of Emporio Armani and Giorgio Armani Accessories. The announcement was made in Milan on the 16th of September, marking a historic first for the house. Vitale becomes the first person outside the Armani family to hold this creative position since the brand&#8217;s founding. [&#8230;]</p>
