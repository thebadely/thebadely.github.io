Australia are set to drop off-spinner Nathan Lyon for the first Test against New Zealand, with uncapped Corey Rocchiccioli expected to be named in the squad.

The omission of Lyon for the Test in Perth from 9-13 December is not thought to be linked to the sandpaper controversy. 

Lyon was part of the Australia XI in Cape Town in 2018 and, three years later, joined his fellow bowlers in releasing a statement denying involvement in the cheating plot.

Lyon has not spoken publicly since the debate over the sandpaper saga re-emerged with the allegation raised in former batter Usman Khawaja's autobiography that the entire team knew about the ball-tampering.

The decision to omit Lyon, a mainstay of their team for 15 years, is a significant move by Australia and leaves the 39-year-old's international career at a crossroads.

With 577 Test wickets, Lyon is sixth on the all-time list, with more wickets than any Australian apart from the legendary Shane Warne. In terms of off-spinners, only Sri Lanka great Muttiah Muralitharan has more.

But Lyon has struggled with form and fitness ever since hobbling out of the second Ashes Test at Lord's in 2023 with a calf injury.

In the most recent Ashes series of 2025-26, Lyon did not bowl a ball in the first Test in Perth, was left out of the day-night Test in Brisbane, then sustained a series-ending injury in the third Test in Adelaide.

Whereas the omission of Lyon would once have been unthinkable, Australia have recently left him out of their XI on a regular basis in order play an extra seamer.

Lyon has repeatedly stated his desire to extend his career in order to win Test series away in India and England - challenges Australia face in 2027. 

He may still be required on the spin-friendly pitches of India, but 29-year-old Rocchiciolli now has the opportunity to be become Australia's number one.

Also an off-spinner, Rocchiciolli offers Australia the option of batting depth - he made 140 for Western Australia against Tasmania in the Sheffield Shield earlier this week.

Australia host New Zealand for four Tests in December and January.
