Here’s what you need to know: The assistant referee had a 60-day recovery period after being operated on

An Ecuadorian footballer has been banned for eight years for throwing a rock at an assistant referee, leaving him requiring surgery to his face.

Jhon Perlaza was playing for Club Ciudad de Yantzaza against Club Rio Aguarico FC in a third-tier match in October when the incident took place. 

The stone struck assistant referee Lenin Giovanni Ojeda Nunez in the face in the 96th minute of the game, which was subsequently abandoned. 

A video on social media shows Perlaza standing up in the team's dugout to throw the stone, with Nunez instantly falling to the floor and holding his face after the object hits him. He then receives medical attention. 

"The assistant referee suffered serious injuries that required surgery and a 60-day recovery period," the Ecuadorian Football Federation (FEF) said.

"The disciplinary committee found the player responsible for physical aggression against a match official and suspended him for eight years from all football-related activity, until 19 October, 2034.

"In addition, the FEF will ask Fifa to extend this sanction worldwide."

The FEF also fined his club Yantzaza $2,000 (Â£1,521) and ordered them to pay Nunez's medical fees of $4,369 (Â£3,323). 

In a statement released on 20 October, three days after the match, Yantzaza apologised to the refereeing team and the fans, and confirmed Perlaza had been sacked as a result of the incident. 

"As an institution, we categorically reject all acts of aggression, whether verbal or physical, both inside and outside sporting venues. We firmly believe that football should be a space of respect, coexistence, and fair play," they said.

"Once the person responsible for this regrettable incident was identified, the institution made the decision to remove him immediately, and he is therefore no longer part of our club."
