Wrexham manager Phil Parkinson says Swansea City captain Ben Cabango got away with a "leg-breaking challenge" in their Welsh derby stalemate.

Parkinson claimed the Wales defender should have been given a straight red for the lunge on midfielder Callum O'Hare late on in the match at the Swansea.com Stadium.

Cabango, who had come on as a second-half substitute, was booked for the foul that prompted an angry reaction from the visitors' bench.

O'Hare was replaced soon after as Swansea maintained their unbeaten start to the season to stay top of the early Championship table.

"I know we want to protect our players but, when someone flies in at that speed, that could have been a leg-breaking challenge," Parkinson said.

"I thought the referee handled the atmosphere well, but in the big decision he didn't make the right call.

"I don't think anybody in the ground or whether people watching back on TV can deny that."
