Two men have pleaded guilty to sexual offences against a woman who was drugged and raped by her husband without her knowledge. 

Robert Stewart, 70, from Stockport admitted rape and conspiracy to administer a substance with intent at Manchester Minshull Street Crown Court.

In a separate hearing at Manchester Magistrates' Court, Tommy Golden, 59, pleaded guilty to sexual assault involving the same woman in June 2025.

Golden appeared via video link from HMP Risley, where he is serving a prison sentence.

District Judge Simon Blakebrough said his offending was too serious to be dealt with by magistrates. His case was sent to Manchester Minshull Street Crown Court, where he is due to appear in December.

During the hearing, Golden said that he had not spoken to a solicitor before entering a guilty plea.

The husband of the woman in the case, who is also from Stockport, has previously admitted more than 60 sexual offences against her, including rape, administering a substance with intent, sharing intimate images and assault by penetration.

He cannot be named to protect the woman's identity.

Four other men have also pleaded guilty to a range of offences.

Nine men are currently on trial for a range of charges against the same victim. They deny those allegations.
