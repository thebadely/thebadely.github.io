An MP has urged a council to speed up the process of heating oil support payments as energy prices soar.

About 23,000 Northumberland homes use oil systems as they are not connected to the mains gas supply. 

Labour's North Northumberland MP David Smith said a freedom of information request from 21 September showed just over a third of households who applied for help with heating oil costs had received funding from Northumberland County Council.

However, the Conservative-led local authority said its latest figures showed significantly more applications had now been approved almost two months on.

Energy prices have increased due to ongoing conflicts around the world. 

Unlike gas, oil prices are not protected by the Ofgem Energy Price Cap, and households have experienced huge increases in the cost of filling tanks.
