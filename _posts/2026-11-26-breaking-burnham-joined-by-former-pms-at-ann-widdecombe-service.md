Andy Burnham has been joined by former prime ministers at a memorial service for the late politician Ann Widdecombe.

The Service of Remembrance at Westminster Cathedral is taking place this afternoon, with eulogies to be delivered and a choir set to sing.

About 600 guests are due to attend, with David Cameron, Liz Truss, Boris Johnson and Rishi Sunak among them. 

Bishop James Curry, the Auxiliary Bishop of Westminster, is leading the service after the Archbishop of Westminster, Richard Moth, was taken to hospital earlier.

Reform UK's chairman Lee Anderson, deputy leader Richard Tice and leader Nigel Farage are among those at the service

Moth, who is the head of the Catholic Church in England and Wales, underwent tests after he "felt unwell", Westminster Diocese said.

Also attending are Reform UK leader Nigel Farage, former Conservative MP Philip Hammond, and Speaker of the House of Commons Sir Lindsay Hoyle, among others.

Widdecombe, a former Conservative minister and Reform UK's justice spokeswoman, was found dead at her home in Haytor on Dartmoor, Devon, on 9 July, aged 78.

Politicians past and present have gathered for the service remembering Ann Widdecombe

Joshua Kerry, 28, of Byrley Road, Rotherham, South Yorkshire, was charged with her murder.

A trial before a High Court judge is scheduled for 8 June, and a plea and case management hearing on 29 January.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham shakes hands with former prime minister Rishi Sunak at the service

Archbishop of Westminster Richard Moth is undergoing tests after being taken to hospital

Former Conservative leader Sir Iain Duncan Smith attended the service

Widdecombe's family announced plans for a public memorial service following her death.

Widdecombe served as Conservative MP for Maidstone and the Weald in Kent for 23 years and was appointed to ministerial roles in Sir John Major's government in the 1990s.

She became a member of Reform in 2019 as a party spokeswoman.

Away from politics she appeared on the BBC's Strictly Come Dancing in 2010 and was a runner-up on Celebrity Big Brother eight years later.
