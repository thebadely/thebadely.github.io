A Chinese rocket, the Long March 7A, failed and exploded on its 10 August launch in Wenchang, Hainan, China. 

State media reported that the failure occurred due to an "in-flight anomaly". 

This was the rocket's first failure since its maiden flight in March 2020, according to Reuters.
