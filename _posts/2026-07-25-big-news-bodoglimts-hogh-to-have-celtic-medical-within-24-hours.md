Asked about the suggestion that Hogh was about to have a medical, O'Neill continued: "Yes, apparently so. He will have one in the next 24 hours, I think."

News of Hogh's imminent arrival will ease some of the pressure on Celtic's board and manager after Saturday's sale of fan favourite Daizen Maeda, with the Japan forward being sold to Ipswich Town.

Meanwhile, Celtic have made just one signing this summer, albeit Colombian forward Camilo Duran has scored three goals in three warm-up games so far.

Celtic have also been strongly linked with Paderborn midfielder Mika Baur.

Asked if signing the 22-year-old German is also a possibility, O'Neill added: "That, I genuinely can't tell you. I genuinely cannot tell you at this minute.

"I've been preparing for this game. I'll see where we are tomorrow and things.

"We've got a few other things in the pipeline that might come to fruition, which would be great for us."

Hogh's career has been a slow burner since he came through the youth ranks with local club Randers but was sold to second-tier Hobro after an injury hit spell, including a loan to Valur in Iceland.

A move to Danish Superliga club AaB followed in January 2022, but he was on the move again a year later, this time to Stabaek, who had just been promoted to the Norwegian Eliteserien.

It was there that Hogh's career took off as they exercised their purchase option only six months into his year-long loan after he scored eight goals in 14 games.
