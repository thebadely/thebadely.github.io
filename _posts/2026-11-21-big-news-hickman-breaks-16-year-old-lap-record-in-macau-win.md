Experts revealed that Peter Hickman produced a record-breaking display to take his fifth Macau Grand Prix success

Peter Hickman smashed Stuart Easton's 16-year-old lap record for the Guia circuit in taking a commanding win in the Macau motorcycle grand prix on Saturday.

It was a fifth triumph in the race for the 14-time Isle of Man TT winner and his first since 2023.

Hickman now sits third in the all-time winners' list at the event, behind nine-time victor Michael Rutter and Ron Haslam, who took six successes at the event.

The Lincolnshire rider dominated the race throughout after setting the pole position time in qualifying and had more than 38 seconds to spare over runner-up, Manxman Marcus Simpson, at the chequered flag.

Northern Ireland rider Paul Jordan completed the podium positions in third on his Jackson Racing Honda.

The first attempt to run the 12-lap race over the 3.8-mile circuit was red-flagged after Austrian rider Julian Trummer crashed out on lap one.

The restart saw Hickman control the pace from the front on his Zeeco by 8Ten Racing BMW, breaking the existing longstanding lap record on three occasions.

The Englishman went two tenths of a second inside the previous benchmark set by Scot Easton with a lap time of two minutes 23.40 seconds but bettered that on his next circuit with 2:23.08.

He went faster again on his next lap with a 2:22.69, before slowing his speed in the latter stages of the race.

Magherafelt rider Jordan occupied second place for much of the race but was overtaken by Simpson late on as both competitors secured their first-ever rostrum finishes at the meeting.

Simpson led Jordan by 6.5 seconds by the conclusion of the race.

Italian Maurizio Bottalico was fourth, with Republic of Ireland newcomer Kevin Keyes fifth and Northern Ireland debutant, Portadown man Mark Hewitt, in a very creditable sixth.

Rob Hodson retired with a mechanical issue while Phil Crowe failed to start with a technical problem. Both were expected to challenge for top three positions.
