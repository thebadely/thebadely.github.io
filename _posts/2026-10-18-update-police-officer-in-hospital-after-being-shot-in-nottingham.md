According to new developments, The officer was shot after approaching two people in Angel Row, Nottingham

A police officer is being treated in hospital after being shot in Nottingham city centre. 

Nottinghamshire Police said the officer had approached two suspects in Angel Row shortly before 04:50 BST on Sunday.

The officer is now being treated for a wound to his foot, the force added. 

Detectives said a firearm was recovered and that two suspects, a man and a woman, fled the scene.

Angel Row is currently closed and a large police scene remains in place.

Forensic officers have attended the scene in Nottingham city centre

Assistant Chief Constable Claire Rukas said: "This is a shocking incident and a sobering reminder of the potential dangers faced by officers as they work to protect and serve the public.

"As we investigate what happened and work to apprehend the individuals involved, my thoughts today are with the injured officer, his family and colleagues.

"I also ask that anyone with additional information shares this with us without delay. Even the smallest bit of information could make all the difference to this investigation."

Detectives have asked anyone with information to come forward

Neghat Khan, leader of Nottingham City Council, said she was "deeply shocked and saddened" by news of the shooting. 

"My thoughts and full support are with the injured police officer, their family, and their colleagues across Nottinghamshire Police," she added.

A large police scene has been put in place in Angel Row

Police cars are parked along the road, which is lined by fast food stores and shops, and orange traffic cones have been laid out alongside items left on the road.

The cordon has also been extended to St James's Street, where an officer is standing guard.

In a post on social media East Midlands Ambulance Service sent its best wishes to the injured officer. 

"Sending our heartfelt thoughts and support to the injured officer, his family, and all our colleagues at Nottinghamshire Police today," the post said. 

"We're wishing him a full and speedy recovery."
