Chelsea defender Wesley Fofana was subjected to repeated boos and told "you're not welcome here" by his own fans during Friday's Premier League defeat by Nottingham Forest.

The jeers aimed at the 25-year-old were heard every time he touched the ball and came after an unauthorised interview with French YouTuber Zach Nani was released.

In that controversial two-hour conversation, Fofana also appeared to joke about missing his penalty during last month's Carabao Cup shootout defeat by Liverpool.

Manager Xabi Alonso said the French centre-back had apologised and "it won't be repeated".

But Fofana endured a difficult time in his side's trip to Forest as supporters made their feelings known.

After Chelsea went behind to a brilliant volley from Igor Jesus in the fifth minute, Fofana also conceded a penalty for the home side's second goal from Morgan Gibbs-White in the 51st minute.

He was booked for the foul on Liam Delap in the box, and then replaced by Levi Colwill in the 56th minute - just after Emmanuel Emegha had pulled a goal back for the visitors - before Gibbs-White made it 3-1 in the 72nd minute.

Emegha scored again in the 89th minute to make it 3-2, but Forest held on to secure their first win over Chelsea at the City Ground since 1997.

Alonso defended Fofana after the game and said in his post-match news conference: "We have to stick together, and everyone that is in the changing room, everyone in the team is needed.

"We need to help, and we need to go through these tough moments all together, and we don't leave anyone apart.

"We need to support everyone. That is because we need everyone, and we need the fans.

"We need all the players. We need to stick together because if not, things will get more complicated, and that's what we want to do."

Asked whether the reaction of the Chelsea fans will make it difficult to pick Fofana going forward, he added: "Well, I'm thinking right after the game, and for the next games, we will see."

Wesley Fofana has made 10 appearances for Chelsea this season

Meanwhile, Nani has moved to defend Fofana, saying the player was "clearly" not happy during the interview and admitted he had to perform better.

But he confessed football had reached a stage where he was "more unhappy than happy", before recovering physically and mentally.

The release of the interview has coincided with the club's poor run of form, with Chelsea losing 4-1 at home to Leeds United last weekend to make it three defeats in four matches.

The defence, in particular, has come in for criticism, with the Blues one of four clubs to have conceded more than 20 goals in the Premier League after 11 matches.

Having been subjected to racist abuse in the past, Fofana spoke about handling comments during a club-organised interview on this summer's pre-season tour of Asia.

He is understood to be one of the highest earners at Stamford Bridge.
