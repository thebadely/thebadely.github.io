Singapore is one of the world's fastest aging countries and its seniors are embracing the challenge of staying healthy creatively.

One group has started practicing parkour in a playground. 

Coach Tan Shie Boon says the sport improves their confidence, while they say it has the added benefit of bringing together community. 

The BBC's Kelly NG joined a training session to see their moves first-hand.
