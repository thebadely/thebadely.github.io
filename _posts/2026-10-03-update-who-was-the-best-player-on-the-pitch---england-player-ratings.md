Experts revealed that Harry Kane, Anthony Gordon and Jude Bellingham all scored as England beat Croatia

England produced one of their best performances in recent times as they thumped Croatia 7-0 in Rijeka. 

Harry Kane opened the scoring, and Jude Bellingham, Anthony Gordon and Bukayo Saka also found the net before half-time. 

England captain Kane then added a fifth early on in the second half before Saka scored his second from close range and substitute Morgan Rogers added a seventh to secure a dominant win.

Here's how BBC Sport England reporter Alex Howell rated the players and you can see how you scored them below.
