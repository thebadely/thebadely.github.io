Craig Overton stars against brother Jamie to give Rockets win over Spirit

The Hundred men's competition, Trent Bridge

London Spirit 159-9 (100 balls): Brevis 39 (24); Harrison 2-25

Craig Overton won an entertaining contest against twin brother Jamie as Trent Rockets edged a thriller by four wickets against London Spirit in the men's Hundred.

The home side's chase of 160 in Nottingham went down to the wire with 10 needed off five balls, but Craig audaciously ramped for six before calmly ticking off the remaining runs with two deliveries to spare.

He finished unbeaten on 20 from only six balls as Rockets earned their first win of this year's tournament.

"He got me out last year so I had to get some payback," Craig told BBC Sport.

"It's still a bit weird playing against each other. We're getting used to it after a few years.

"We're both very competitive - we both always want to win - so I think the family will be happy with how we played it." 

Rockets' chase was set up by Ben Duckett's 75 from 47 balls at the top of the order, an innings which included 12 fours and two sixes and led an impressive recovery after he lost his opening partner Finn Allen for a duck.

Duckett fell with 52 runs needed from 26 balls, which looked to have swung momentum Sprit's way, but Sam Billings' 22 off 10 combined with Overton's knock got them over the line.

Highlights: Buttler goes big as Super Giants thrash Phoenix by 87 runs

Jos Buttler produced a masterful 90 from 50 balls as Manchester Super Giants demolished Birmingham Phoenix by 87 runs to maintain their perfect start to The Hundred.

After making his highest T20 score of 131 against India this month, the England wicketkeeper-batter continued his resurgence, striking seven fours and five sixes to lead the home side to 187-5.

It was the second highest Hundred total at Old Trafford and one that Phoenix fell well short of as they were bundled out for 100, handing Super Giants their second win in two matches.

The women's fixture was washed out and the men's game delayed by 30 minutes but, once the action got under way, fans were treated to a sensational innings from Buttler.

Super Giants suffered early setbacks as Tim Seifert and captain Aiden Markram fell cheaply, but Buttler combined power-hitting with deft touches around the ground to bring up his half-century in 31 balls.

Buttler's superb innings came to end with three balls remaining, while Tom Moores added an unbeaten 39 from 17 deliveries.
