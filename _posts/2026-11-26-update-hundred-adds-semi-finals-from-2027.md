An extra match will be added to The Hundred for the first time in 2027 with the introduction of semi-finals.

In the previous six editions of The Hundred, only the top three qualified from the eight-team group stage. First place went straight to the final, with second and third playing in the eliminator.

Now, the top four will play in traditional semis in a move designed to increase the competitiveness of the group stage and create an extra marquee fixture.

The Hundred window will not be expanded to accommodate the semi-finals â the competition will still be completed in just under four weeks.

Instead, finals weekend will be altered. In 2026, for the first time, a reserve day was scheduled for the eliminator, which was played on a Friday before the final took place on a Sunday.

In 2027, the semi-finals will take place on Friday and Saturday, followed by the Sunday final.

The Oval has become the regular venue for the eliminator, with Lord's hosting the final. Having both games in London has minimised travel for the teams having to play an extra game to reach the final.

In the new structure, The Oval is expected to host the Saturday semi-final. The venue for the first semi is to be confirmed.

The tweak in the format of The Hundred comes a year after investors began to exert influence over the eight franchises.

The move to introduce a second semi-final has not been directly influenced by the arrival of external investment, though it has been welcomed by owners.

A primary motivation has come from a desire to maximise the competitiveness of matches played in the latter stage of the group phase.
