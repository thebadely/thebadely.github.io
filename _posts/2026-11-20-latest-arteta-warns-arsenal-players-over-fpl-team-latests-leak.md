Mikel Arteta says his Arsenal players have been warned against inadvertently leaking team news via Fantasy Premier League.

The intervention comes after Christos Tzolis selected and captained himself in his Fantasy Premier League (FPL) team before Arsenal's match against Liverpool on 1 November, after he had missed a month through injury. 

The Gunners manager picked the winger in Arsenal's starting XI at Anfield in what was his first appearance since Greece's Nations League draw with the Netherlands on 1 October.

Before Saturday's match at Newcastle, Arteta said he had to speak to his squad, and those close to the club, about their FPL selections.

"With Christos, with the squad and with my kids as well," he said 

"They're constantly asking me about this as well, because they want to know. It's a topic.

"This is the kind of thing that is happening in our society right now. It's something new. We haven't dealt with it before and we need to put new things in place. 

"Obviously, confidentiality is something critical because it's about trust and about what you are trying to do and that should be kept as a huge secret. We'll take measures to make sure it doesn't happen."

The Spaniard also said his players are restricted from selecting themselves in their FPL line-ups.

In October, a number of FPL teams connected to staff and players at Chelsea removed striker Joao Pedro from their teams, hinting the Brazilian had sustained an injury.
