Derek McInnes says Rangers should not experience any anxiety about the prospect of going top of the Scottish Premiership as the Ibrox side look to extend their four-match winning run.

Rangers host in-form Falkirk on Saturday (15:00 GMT), who have won six on the bounce in the league, and victory would move McInnes' team top before leaders Celtic travel to face Hearts on Sunday.

"I don't think there should be anxiety when you're trying to go top of the league at all," McInnes said.

"Obviously, you want to be top of the league come the end of the season.

"If you've got an opportunity to go there, it's important you try and take it."

Ryan Naderi netted an early second-half equaliser then set up Djeidi Gassama as Rangers turned a 1-0 deficit into a 3-1 lead before Aberdeen scored a consolation in Tuesday's Premiership match at Ibrox.

It was Naderi's 11th goal of the season and McInnes spoke of his awareness of the now Czech Republic international last season while managing Hearts, who have used a data analytics firm for much of their recent recruitment.

"Last January, obviously, when we first saw that Rangers were linked with Naderi, like everything else, I would say to [Hearts sporting director] Graeme Jones, 'how do they rate him?'

"Because you're always curious, it's just the way it is. It's brilliant when you see other teams round about you signing a player that's not rated!

"He was someone that I think was well known to them. Brugge had an interest in Ryan, I think, and I think USG potentially had an interest in him as well but the kind of money, the fee, it just went away from them.

"The feeling was that he could help Rangers and make them better.

"I had a good conversation with Ryan when I first came in the door and what we felt this season could look like for him.

"He's stood up brilliantly to the injuries to Youssef [Chermiti] and Lawrence [Shankland] and he's had to be the guy. He's led the line brilliantly and I love the goals he scores. All top strikers have intelligence to be in the right place at the right time."

And the manager reiterated his stance about keeping hold of assets like Naderi through January.

"These conversations that I have with Jim [Gillespie, chief executive] on a regular basis about players that we should be speaking to and trying to address and secure and give them a wee bit more security and a wee bit more comfort for us, these conversations, I think, have been ongoing," McInnes said.

"The club's well aware of what needs to be done and what we shouldn't be doing and Ryan's been part of that conversation."

McInnes' side won 2-1 away to Falkirk in September but the Bairns held Danny Rohl's Rangers to a 0-0 draw at Ibrox last season.

"What they have got is a bit more physicality," McInnes said of the Bairns.

"They can play when they want to play so they've mixed it up a bit this season, I think, and been a wee bit more pragmatic maybe at times as well, but they've still had those results that's got them where they are in the league and we've got a difficult game ahead of ourselves."
