Super Giants cruise past bottom of the table MI London

The Hundred women's competition, The Oval

MI London 92 all out (99 balls): Chatli 18 (16); MacDonald-Gay 3-19

Manchester Super Giants 95-4 (72 balls): Lanning 43 (31); Gordon 2-14, Kerr 2-21

Manchester Super Giants won by six wickets 

Scorecard. Table

MI London remain without a win in the Hundred after Manchester Super Giants completed a comfortable six-wicket win at the Oval.

The competition's bottom side were bowled out for 92, while visiting captain Meg Lanning struck 43 as her side sealed victory with 28 balls to spare.

MI had a shocking start after being put into bat, with openers Hayley Matthews and Alice Davidson-Richards both bowled by Sophie Ecclestone for ducks.

The next four batters all reached the teens but failed to push on, while the last four MI wickets fell for five runs over the course of eight deliveries as the innings concluded with a ball to spare.

Ryana Macdonald-Gay took the final of wicket of Kirstie Gordon to finish with figures of 3-19, while Ecclestone and Maitlan Brown closed on 2-7 and 2-18 respectively.

The Super Giants opening pairing of Smriti Mandhana and Lanning took their side most of the way to their low target, putting on 73 before Mandhana was caught by Hollie Armitage off Gordon for 34.

Melie Kerr then dismissed Richa Ghosh (4) and Paige Scholfield (0) with successive deliveries, while Gordon trapped Lanning lbw with only four runs required for victory.

Mady Villiers wrapped the game up, hitting Gordon for four to condemn MI to a fourth defeat from five matches.

Third-placed Super Giants move back within two points of Trent Rockets in second, while MI remain bottom with their only points coming from a tie against London Spirit.

Rockets see off late Sutherland fightback to beat Sunrisers

The Hundred women's competition, Trent Bridge

Trent Rockets 139-7 (100 balls): Dunkley 56 (33); Jonassen 2-20

Sunrisers Leeds 134-5 (100 balls): Sutherland 59* (30); Phillips 1-10

Trent Rockets won by five runs 

Scorecard. Table

Trent Rockets held off a late assault from Annabel Sutherland and Lauren Winfield-Hill to beat defending champions Sunrisers Leeds by five runs and extend their winning streak to three matches.

Defending 139 at Trent Bridge after failing to capitalise on Sophia Dunkley's 29-ball half-century, economical bowling helped Rockets reduce Sunrisers to 76-4.

However, with 55 runs required off the final 20 balls, Sutherland dispatched Georgia Elwiss for 23 runs, including sending two full tosses away for six, to drag Sunrisers back into the match.

She and Winfield-Hill reduced the equation to 17 off five but when the latter was run out with three deliveries remaining, she took Sunrisers' hopes of victory with her.

Sutherland finished the match with a six to end unbeaten on 59 (30), with her partnership with Winfield-Hill (15) worth 50 off just 27 deliveries.

Earlier, Rockets had appeared well set for a sizeable total after Dunkley backed up her 57 against Manchester Super Giants on Friday with a knock of 56 (33), including nine fours, before being bowled by Sutherland.

Nat Sciver-Brunt and Ashleigh Gardner briefly maintained the pressure, including taking Dani Gibson's second set for 18 to carry Rockets beyond three figures off just 65 balls, but Sunrisers fought back with the ball.

Jess Jonassen and Hannah Baker took two wickets apiece as Rockets slumped from 105-2 to 118-6, with the hosts also failing to hit a boundary for 32 deliveries as their hopes of a 150-plus total faded.

A trio of fours from Bess Heath in the final set proved crucial, putting Rockets' total just out of Sunrisers' reach.

Trent Rockets' fourth victory of the tournament moves them to within four points of unbeaten Southern Brave at the top of the table. Sunrisers Leeds, who are without a win since their opener, slip to seventh.

Dunkley is the tournament's leading run-scorer, with 194 runs at a strike-rate of 168.69 in five matches
