Ben is a lifelong Brighton fan who is feeling optimistic about the new season despite seeing more star players like striker Danny Welbeck and defender Jan Paul van Hecke leave the club this summer.

"We loved Danny, because he really fitted into our style of play and he had such a great season last year," Ben told BBC Sport.

"We will miss Van Hecke at the back too, but the thing with Brighton is that we always bounce back. We always come through with the players we bring in that I've never heard of, but they get given this chance.

"I just trust in [Brighton owner] Tony Bloom. He knows better than anyone about these things, because he has done it so many times before.

"He has sold so many world-class players to other clubs and we have nurtured so many great players to replace them. 

"So, I don't feel there is any need to worry right now. Just put the trust in the club and see how we get on.

"It's great to be back in Europe [in the Europa Conference League] but with us potentially playing so many more games, I don't expect us to get there again through our league position this time.

"But my hope is that we won't drop into the bottom half of the table. My thinking is that, as long as we finish in the top 10 again and have a good go at Europe, then that's a great season."
