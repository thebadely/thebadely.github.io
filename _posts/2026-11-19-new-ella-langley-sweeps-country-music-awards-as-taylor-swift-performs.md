Here’s what you need to know: Ella Langley said her hit Choosin' Texas was "the gift that keeps on giving"

US singer Ella Langley has swept the Country Music Association Awards (CMAs), following the huge success of her breakthrough hit Choosin' Texas.

The Alabama-born singer was named entertainer of the year at Wednesday's ceremony, weeks after the song became the longest-running number one in Billboard chart history.

"At this point, I'm sick of me," the 27-year-old joked in her acceptance speech, having spent so much of the ceremony on stage. "I could quit tomorrow and have the best career of my life."

Choosin' Texas was also named single of the year and song of the year, leading Langley to joke in an acceptance speech: "This song is the gift that keeps on giving." 

Ella Langley, who won six awards on the night, performed her song Loving Life Again

Langley won six awards in total, including female vocalist of the year, music video of the year and album of the year for Dandelion.

She also performed her song Loving Life Again with a string quartet, and duetted with Miranda Lambert for a performance of Snakeskin Boots.

Choosin' Texas made history in September after spending 23 weeks at the top spot on the Billboard Hot 100 chart. It has now spent 28 weeks in total at the summit.

Following her win, Langley took to social media and said: "Hard to put this night into words and what it means.

"Thank you to my fans. Thank you for loving this record, thank you for coming to my shows, thank you for believing in my artistry.

"This year has been the most incredible journey, and I want to thank you for sharing in that with me. I love y'all."

Langley also performed Snakeskin Boots with Miranda Lambert during the ceremony

Taylor Swift also performed at the CMA Awards for the first time in 13 years.

Her performance began with Swift walking backstage and featured cameo appearances from country stars including Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Sugarland, and Brooks & Dunn.

Swift then sang her recent hit I Knew It, I Knew You, from the Toy Story 5 soundtrack, using a blue guitar with bedazzled clouds on it, in a nod to the animated franchise.

The singer also mixed in some of her 2006 country hit Our Song, while Keith Urban joined her on the banjo.

Taylor Swift used a Toy Story-themed guitar and performed alongside Keith Urban

She also presented a lifetime achievement award to country music duo Brooks & Dunn

Swift's performance came after she was inducted into both the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame and the Songwriters Hall of Fame earlier this year. 

Other winners on the night included Chris Stapleton who won male vocalist of the year, The Red Clay Strays who won vocal group of the year, and Brooks & Dunn with vocal duo of the year.

Meanwhile Stephen Wilson Jr won the new artist of the year award while Charlie Worsham won musician of the year.

The award ceremony began with a tribute to country music legend Dolly Parton, who died in August aged 80.

Ceremony host Lainey Wilson began the tribute by duetting with singer-songwriter Vince Gill on a rendition of Parton's I Will Always Love You.
