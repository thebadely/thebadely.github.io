Experts revealed that Dr Hannah Terry was found dead on Sunday 

The husband of a GP has appeared in court charged with her murder after she was found dead at home.

The body of 45-year-old GP Dr Hannah Terry, also known as Hannah Cain, was found at a property in Fern Hill in Dersingham, Norfolk, on Sunday.

Mark Cain, 45, also of Fern Hill, was charged with her murder on Tuesday.

He appeared in a black sweatshirt at Norwich Magistrates' Court, where he spoke only to confirm his age, name and address. He was not asked to enter a plea and is due back in court on Thursday.

Prosecutor Jacqui Hanlon told the court the ambulance service contacted the police at 02:25 BST on Sunday.

She said Terry was "found in an upstairs bedroom not breathing, not conscious".

She said a post-mortem examination "noted blunt force injury to the right side of her head had been inflicted, also an injury that could be suggestive of compression of the neck".

District judge Matthew Bone remanded the defendant in custody to appear at King's Lynn Crown Court on Thursday.

Mark Cain was remanded back into custody after his court appearance in Norwich

Terry was listed as a member of the practice team at Grimston Medical Centre, which had closed on Monday and Tuesday, following her death.

It reopened on Wednesday and said it would not be commenting "on the circumstances surrounding the tragic death of our colleague, Dr Hannah Terry".

Dersingham village is part of the Royal Family's estate of Sandringham in west Norfolk.
