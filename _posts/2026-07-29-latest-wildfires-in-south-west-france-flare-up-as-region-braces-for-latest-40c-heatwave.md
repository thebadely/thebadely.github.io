The race to stop the French woodland fires in their tracks

Firefighters in France are working around the clock to contain wildfires that have been raging in the Gironde region, while bracing for another sharp rise in temperatures.

"The fire is like David against Goliath," said Eric Brocardi, spokesman of the national firefighters' federation. 

Four of his colleagues had died in recent weeks across France, he said, and 2,000  had been deployed in the Gironde to contain the flames, which for days have ravaged the countryside between the coastline and Bordeaux. 

According to regional prefect Sophie Brocas 14 places that had already been hit by the fires saw new flare-ups on Tuesday. "We are not in the clear," she said. 

This summer's fourth heatwave was expected to start by Wednesday, bringing temperatures in the area to 40C.

Similar temperatures are expected in Spain.

Dozens of communities are still threatened by wildfires that forced more than 70,000 to evacuate their homes over the last week.

Spain has ended some evacuation orders, allowing about 44,000 people to return home or leave confinement.
