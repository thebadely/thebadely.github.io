A bride who became a social media sensation after being jilted at the altar has described the "pure magic" of finally walking down the aisle.

Kayley Stead's fiance did not turn up for her big day in 2022, but she surprised everyone by letting the celebration continue with many agreeing to "just go with this madness".

Four years on and she has found love again, reconnecting with an old university friend who did turn up for their ceremony on Friday 13th November. 

Stead walked down the aisle in a bespoke wedding dress to applause, later saying she felt everyone was relieved the wedding was actually happening this time.

Marrying Richard Perrott was everything she had imagined, Stead told BBC Radio Wales Breakfast.

"As soon as I started walking down the aisle, everyone just started clapping, because I think everyone was relieved that this is actually happening," she said.
