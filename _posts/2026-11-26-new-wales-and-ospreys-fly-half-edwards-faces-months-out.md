Ospreys and Wales have received a major injury blow with fly-half Dan Edwards ruled out for "a few months" by an ankle injury.

Edwards revealed on social media that he has had surgery after suffering the injury during Wales training.

The 23-year-old said he will "catch you all in a few months back on the grass" - although the length of his absence has not yet officially been revealed.

Wales' Six Nations campaign starts with a trip to face France in Paris on 6 February 2027 and ends away against Italy in Rome on 13 March.

Edwards featured as a replacement in the November victories against Japan and New Zealand, with Sam Costelow due to start all four Wales Tests in November.

Cardiff's Jarrod Evans was brought into the squad to replace Edwards, while Scarlets centre Joe Hawkins will cover fly-half for the final Nations Championship match against Japan at the Allianz Stadium in Twickenham on Saturday.

Ospreys head coach Mark Jones will now consider going into the transfer market to replace his star number 10.

Edwards' absence will leave Australian Lawson Creighton as the Ospreys' first-choice fly-half option, backed up by Harri Wilde who is on loan from Cardiff.
