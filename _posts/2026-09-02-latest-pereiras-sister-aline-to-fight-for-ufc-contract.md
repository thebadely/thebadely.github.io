Aline Pereira is set to fight for a UFC contract that would see her join her brother and two-weight UFC champion Alex Pereira in the MMA promotion.

Aline will fight undefeated prospect Smilla Sundell, a Muay Thai champion in the Asia-based ONE Championship, on Dana White's Contender Series in Las Vegas on 13 October.

UFC president White's Contender Series offers a chance for fighters outside the UFC to win contracts in one-off contests.

Pereira, who currently holds a 3-2 record, with all three of her victories coming by decision, confirmed on her Instagram she will face Sweden's Sundell in a bantamweight bout.

The 35-year-old's most recent victory was a split decision against Jade Masson-Wong in May, on the undercard of Jake Paul's Most Valuable Promotions's first MMA event.
