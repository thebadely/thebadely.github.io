Here’s what you need to know: St Mirren are the defending champions after beating Celtic in last season's final

Holders St Mirren have been drawn away to Rangers in the last 16 of the Premier Sports Cup.

There are two other all top-flight ties, with Scottish champions Celtic, who were last season's beaten finalists, visiting Dundee United while Aberdeen host Dundee.

Top-flight Kilmarnock will host Ayr United in an Ayrshire derby.

At least one side outwith the Premiership is guaranteed to reach the quarter-finals after second-tier Dunfermline Athletic were drawn at home to League 1 side Ross County.

Dunfermline were the last side to qualify for the last 16 when a late Chris Kane penalty secured a win away to St Mirren on Sunday that sealed progress as one of the three best runners-up at Championship rivals Queen's Park's expense.

Although St Mirren still topped the group on goal difference, the defeat meant they missed out on being seeded in Sunday's draw. 

Reacting to being drawn away to Rangers, manager Craig McLeish said: "I am disappointed from my point of view that we had a chance to win our group and be one of the seeded sides and the draw has not been good to us.

"But it is a big game and we just have to look forward to the next round now."

Rangers counterpart Derek McInnes was delighted just to get a home draw.

"The likelihood is you probably come up against a Premiership team and that's obviously a challenge," he told Rangers TV. 

"You're up against a team who did so well last season in the cup by winning it. So we know that it's not without its pitfalls, but the fact that we're at home, we've got to be grateful for that."

Dunfermline head coach Neil Lennon thought his side "deserved our victory" after a strong last half hour against St Mirren but was expecting another tough challenge despite County now being in the league below.

"A home tie is the most important thing, but Ross County have been phenomenal this season after a hugely disappointing relegation, so we will not be taking anything for granted," he said.

Aberdeen, Ayr, Dundee United, Inverness Caledonian Thistle, Kilmarnock, County and Stenhousemuir were the other group winners, while Dundee and Partick Thistle progressed as runners-up.

Celtic, Heart of Midlothian, Hibernian, Motherwell and Rangers were given byes to the last 16 as they are representing Scotland in European competitions.

While the Old Firm have been drawn against top-flight opponents, the other three take on Championship sides, with Hearts hosting Caley Thistle, Hibs at home to Partick Thistle and Motherwell away to Stenhousemuir.

The second-round ties will be played on the weekend of 15-16 August.
