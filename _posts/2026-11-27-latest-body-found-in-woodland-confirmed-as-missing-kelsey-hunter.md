Here’s what you need to know: Police have confirmed that a body found in woodland in southern Scotland has been identified as missing 20-year-old Kelsey Hunter.

She had last been seen in Castle Douglas at 17:00 on Saturday and a major search was carried out around the nearby village of Springholm where she lived.

On Tuesday, Daniel McKnight, 21, appeared on petition at Dumfries Sheriff Court charged with murder and two other charges, including defeating the ends of justice.

He made no plea and was remanded in custody.
