England's Charley Hull carded a six-under-par 66 to sit one shot off the first-round lead at the LPGA's lucrative season-ending CME Group Tour Championship.

Australia's Karis Davidson, South Korea's Kim A-lim and Thailand's Jeeno Thitikul, set the pace at seven under at the Tiburon Golf Club in Naples, Florida. 

World number two Thitikul, who is aiming to win the event for the third consecutive season, responded to a bogey on the 12th hole by knocking in five consecutive birdies on the back nine.

Hull, who triumphed a decade ago, is one of nine players a stroke behind the leading trio on a stacked leaderboard that contains the top 60 in this season's points list, who are all chasing the $4m (Â£3.05m) top prize.

"When I won 10 years ago I didn't really take golf seriously. I remember coming into this event when I won, hungover," Hull said.

"I felt pretty good [today]. I kind of like lost my swing feels [at the] end of last week because my hip was very tight, and it just like started rerouting my golf swing," Hull said.

"Today I was nervous on the first tee. I felt like I had no swing feeling. Then as the round developed I felt like I hit it really nice."

Hull's compatriot Lottie Woad made six birdies and a bogey as she signed for five-under 67, along with France's Celine Boutier. 

World number one Nelly Korda, who will qualify for the LPGA Hall of Fame if she wins on Sunday, endured a mixed day but picked up shots on the 17th and 18th as she finished on four under.

With an $11m purse on offer, Korda would also break Annika Sorenstam's career earnings record if she finishes in sixth place or better.

The Swede amassed 10 major titles and earned $22,583,694 during a storied career before retiring in 2008, with Korda now just under $160,000 behind that mark.
