It has been recently reported that Wales won four cycling medals on day three of the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow

She's done it again. 

Three nights at the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome and three gold medals for Wales' cycling superstar.

Emma Finucane became the first Welsh woman to collect that haul at a single Commonwealth Games. 

Just as it was on Friday, Finucane and Anna Morris stole the show, both topping the podium on the same night. 

Team-mate Jess Roberts joined Morris on the podium in the points race by claiming silver, a feat matched by James Ball and pilot Matthew Rotherham in a dramatic men's tandem B sprint. 

Wales have now won 10 cycling medals including five golds, three silvers and two bronze. Eight of those podium positions have been achieved by Welsh women.

Only Australia, with a population nearly nine times larger, sit above them in the track cycling medal standings.

Emma Finucane has won sprint and Olympic titles

The 1,000m time trial was making its debut at these Games and featured a mix of sprinters and endurance athletes for this halfway-house event.

Then came the woman from Carmarthen.

Buoyed by the crowds, Finucane set the pace, clocking one minute 05.285 seconds which guaranteed a bronze medal, with just two left to ride.

Neither England's Sophie Capewell nor New Zealand's Olympic sprint champion Ellesse Andrews were able to match that mark.

You'd imagine that winning a new event would require a lot of practice and a specific plan.

Not for Finucane.

"It was the unknown," she said.

"I went into it not knowing what I could do because I'd never really done one before. I just had to hurt myself as much as I could, and I did.

"I'm not sure I'll be doing one of them again!"

It seems that there is nothing Finucane cannot do. 

Only weightlifters David Morgan and Andy Phillips can match the 23-year-old's achievements at a single Games, when they both won three golds in Auckland in 1990. 

And Finucane has one more race to come. 

"I came into these Games wanting to win a gold," she said. 

"To do it three times, I did not think would happen to me.

"I just can't believe I'm living this life at the moment. I've got one more event, so we'll just see what I can do."

Should Finucane repeat the feat in Sunday's women's keirin, she would become the most successful Welsh woman in Commonwealth Games history, taking the two-day old title of Para-athlete Olivia Breen.

Anna Morris and Jess Roberts were part of the Great Britain team pursuit squad that won bronze at the Olympics in Paris in 2024

The sprinting superstar has an endurance counterpart in team-mate Morris. 

Once again at these Games the Welsh anthem reverberated around the velodrome twice in one night.

Prince Louis even got in on the act, donning a bucket hat with 'Beicio Cymru' - Welsh for Welsh Cycling - written on the front.

This time Morris had company on the podium, with Roberts stood beside her on the next step. 

"This one was super cool," she said.

"We're team-mates and really close friends. We often room together, so to share that moment with her was just amazing."

The qualified doctor won gold on Friday and thought she had won silver too in the women's scratch event before she was relegated to fifth for entering the sprinter's lane when her opponent was already there.

There was no such bad luck today.

"Incredible," she said, when asked how she was feeling.

"To get an individual medal as well, that makes it even more special.

"I'm so surprised but really happy."
