Here’s what you need to know: Murat Gassiev moved to heavyweight in 2020 and has eight wins in nine fights in the division 

Murat Gassiev says he currently has no idea if he still is the WBA heavyweight champion after reports Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua would fight for his belt.

Gasiev, 32, was upgraded to 'Super' champion in July when Oleksandr Usyk vacated the title, having been the 'Regular' belt holder since 2025.

Fury and Joshua are scheduled to fight on 11 December in Cardiff and it was claimed that bout would be for the title Gassiev currently holds.

Speaking to ESPN, external and after reaching out to the WBA, Gassiev says he is still in the dark.

"[The] WBA give me big surprise about my belt. But in press conference when AJ and Fury have face off, I see only Ring Magazine belt, no WBA belt," Gassiev said.

"And to be honest right now, I don't have clear information about this situation. I don't know. Fight for 'Super' belt, another belt, I don't know.

"I check my email. I don't have any messages, any papers, but I'm not manager, promoter [or] lawyer. I'm fighter. I do my job, that's it."

Gassiev is still listed on the WBA's website as their reigning champion, with Fury ranked number two behind American Jarrell Miller and Joshua at three.

Joshua and Fury are both two-time heavyweight world champions and held a launch news conference last week in London.

The heavyweights will meet at Cardiff's Principality stadium after a decade-long effort to bring the British rivals together in the ring.

WBO world champion Daniel Dubois, who has a knockout win over Joshua, says he cannot pick a winner.

"It doesn't seem like it has that same edge to it, if it happened years ago," Dubois said.

"They missed the fire and the edge. But I am sure it will be a great fight regardless."
