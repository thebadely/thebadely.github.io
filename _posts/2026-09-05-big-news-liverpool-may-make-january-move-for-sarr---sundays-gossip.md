Liverpool may make another move for Crystal Palace winger Ismaila Sarr in January, Tottenham's Richarlison may look to Saudi switch as Trabzonspor transfer fails to materialise and Manchester United continuing to monitor Newcastle United's Lewis Hall.

Liverpool will consider making another move to sign Ismaila Sarr from Crystal Palace in January after failing to agree a deal for the 28-year-old Senegal winger before the summer deadline. (Football Insider), external

Tottenham's wantaway Brazil forward Richarlison will be hoping to seal a move to the Saudi Pro League, where signings are permitted up to 12 October, after failing to secure a switch to Trabzonspor before the transfer window closed in Turkey on Friday. (Standard), external

Arsenal are closing in on agreeing a new contract with manager Mikel Arteta, whose current deal runs out at the end of this season as they also make progress on new deals for 27-year-old England midfielder Declan Rice, Norway playmaker Martin Odegaard, 27, and Dutch defender Jurrien Timber, 25. (Teamtalk), external

Arsenal enquired about Germany winger Kevin Schade in the summer but moved on as Brentford had no intention of selling the 24-year-old. (Teamtalk), external 

Manchester United will continue to monitor Newcastle United left-back Lewis Hall, 21, with a view to a possible move for the England international next summer. (Football Insider), external 

Belgium midfielder Amadou Onana has signed a new deal, which runs until 2031, with Aston Villa. (Sacha Tavolieri), external 

AC Milan are still interested in Sporting defender Goncalo Inacio, 25, and could make a move for the Portugal international in the January transfer window. (Tuttomercatoweb - in Italian), external

Manchester United remain in talks about signing English midfielder Louis Page from Leicester City after they failed to complete a move for the 18-year-old on transfer deadline day. (Manchester Evening News via Evening Standard), external

Lyon considered bringing former France striker Karim Benzema back to the club but the 38-year-old's contract as an ambassador with the Saudi Pro League prevented him from making the move. (L'Equipe - in French), external
