Here’s what you need to know: Ronnie O'Sullivan has won the Shanghai Masters a record five times

Ronnie O'Sullivan made a comfortable start to the new snooker season as he won in the first round of the Shanghai Masters.

Playing in the first major event of 2026-27, the seven-time world champion claimed a 6-1 win over 18-year-old Chinese amateur Liu Linhao, who was a wildcard entry.

The 50-year-old Englishman made four 50-plus breaks - with a highest of 75 - as he set up a meeting with top seed and defending champion Kyren Wilson in the last 16 on Tuesday.

England's four-time world champion Mark Selby suffered an early exit as the world number nine was beaten 6-5 by Zhou Yuelong.

The 28-year-old local favourite, currently ranked 22 in the world, made breaks of 83 and then 107 to fight back from 5-4 down.

Barry Hawkins beat Lei Peifan 6-4 to reach the last 16 while fellow Englishman Chris Wakelin cruised through 6-0 against Hewen Tang.

Hawkins will face world number one Judd Trump next in Tuesday's second-round matches, while Hawkins takes on Mark Williams and world number two Neil Robertson meets Xiao Guodong.
