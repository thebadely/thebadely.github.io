Arsenal defender Lotte Wubben-Moy has been called up to the England squad for next month's Women's World Cup play-off ties against Ukraine.

The 27-year-old's inclusion follows the withdrawals of winger Lauren Hemp and defender Jess Carter.

Hemp is managing a minor injury sustained during Manchester City's defeat by Tottenham on Sunday.

Carter is unavailable for the two-legged play-off for family reasons.

Wubben-Moy has earned 19 caps since making her debut in February 2021.

Sarina Wiegman's Lionesses will travel to Spain to face Ukraine in the away leg on Tuesday, 1 December, with the second leg at Wembley on Saturday, 5 December.
