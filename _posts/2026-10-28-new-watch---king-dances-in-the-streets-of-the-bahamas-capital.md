King Charles III danced in the street while taking part in a parade with officials in the Bahamas capital city of Nassau. 

On Tuesday, the King gave a speech at an event celebrating local youth and acknowledged "great suffering of the past" as he faced calls for slavery reparations. 

The nine-day tour will end in the Commonwealth Heads of Government meeting, where the King says leaders will discuss the region's legacy of slavery.
