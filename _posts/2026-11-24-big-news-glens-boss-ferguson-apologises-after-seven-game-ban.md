Experts revealed that Duncan Ferguson jointly holds the record for the most red cards in Premier League history with eight. 

Glentoran manager Duncan Ferguson has been suspended for seven games following his red card in the side's 3-2 defeat by Cliftonville last week.

The former Everton striker was dismissed for how he addressed match officials after believing the Reds' opener at Solitude should have been ruled out for a foul in the build-up.

The 1995 FA Cup winner arrived at the Oval in October and has so far won one, drawn two and lost one of his four games in charge. 

"I want to apologise to the match officials for my behaviour on Friday night," said the former Forest Green Rovers and Inverness Caledonian Thistle boss. 

"I was frustrated by what happened on the pitch and let my emotions get the better of me. I went too far in the way I expressed that frustration towards the officials, and I should have handled the situation better."

"I accept the suspension and I'll learn from what happened. My focus now is on the players, the staff and the work we've got to do together, both on and off the pitch."

The sanction will start with Tuesday night's hosting of Larne in the Bet McLean Cup, a competition in which the east Belfast outfit have been beaten finalists the past two seasons.

Even should the Glens progress to the quarter-finals of the competition expected to take place next month, Ferguson's ban would still include the eagerly anticipated Boxing Day visit from fierce rivals Linfield.
