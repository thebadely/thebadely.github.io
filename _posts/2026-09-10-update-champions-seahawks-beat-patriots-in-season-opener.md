Smith-Njigba scores touchdown in Seahawks opener

Defending champions the Seattle Seahawks came from behind to beat the New England Patriots 13-10 in the opening game of the NFL season.

For the first time in 10 years the season began with a rematch of the Super Bowl, in which the Patriots were dominated by a Seahawks defence known as 'the Dark Side'.

Again they stepped up after Seattle lost quarterback Sam Darnold to a hip injury in the game's opening drive, claiming three interceptions in the final quarter at Lumen Field.

The Patriots went into a 10-0 lead, with rookie tight end Eli Raridon scoring the first touchdown of the season in the second quarter.

Back-up quarterback Drew Lock got the Seattle offence going late in the third quarter, with Jason Myers cutting the gap with a field goal.

Seahawks cornerback Nehemiah Pritchett claimed his first career interception at the start of the fourth quarter, after a Drake Maye pass was deflected by rookie Rodney Thomas.

On the next possession Lock found wide reciever Jaxon Smith-Njigba in space and last season's offensive player of the year charged through the Patriots defence for a 45-yard touchdown.

Julian Love then picked off Patriots quarterback Maye, giving Myers the chance to edge the Seahawks in front with a 26-yard field goal.

New England were well within field-goal range with 21 seconds remaining, but chose to go for the win rather than settle for overtime, and the move backfired as Maye's hopeful pass towards Mack Hollins was intercepted by Josh Jobe in the endzone.

The only game in which Maye threw multiple interceptions last season was the Super Bowl, and he was picked off on the Patriots' final three possessions in the season opener.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel said: "You can't throw three interceptions. Drake knows that."

Maye said his final interception was "unacceptable", adding: "We could have sent it to overtime at the worst, and the only thing I could do was turn the ball over."

Seattle head coach Mike Macdonald said initial tests showed Darnold had not fractured a hip.

Week one continues with the NFL's first regular-season game in Australia. The Los Angeles Rams meet the San Francisco 49ers in Melbourne on Thursday (01:35 BST, Friday).
