Commonwealth Games gold medallist Muhammad Hafizuddin Roslin has had his right arm amputated above the elbow after a motorcycle accident in his native Malaysia.

The 21-year-old weightlifter was injured in a crash on Monday, according to local media reports.

Ayub Rahmat, president of the Malaysia Weightlifting Federation, said that Hafizuddin was conscious and in a stable condition in hospital in Kuala Lumpur on Thursday.

"He is awake and smiling," Rahmat told AFP. "Physically his condition is improving but this incident has had a great impact on his future."

Hafizuddin won Commonwealth gold in the 88kg event in Glasgow in July and was hoping to compete at the 2028 Olympics in Los Angeles.

The Malaysian government has said it will cover all Hafizuddin's treatment and medical costs.

Ayub called it "a shock and a huge loss" and added: "We are already looking at how he can be integrated into the sport, including a future coaching role in weightlifting."
