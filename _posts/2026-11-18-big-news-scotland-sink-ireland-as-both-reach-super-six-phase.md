ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy

Ireland 71 (19.3 overs): Lewis 20; Gordon 4-4, Maqsood 3-15

Scotland 72-2 (8.3 overs): Carter 30, Fraser 21

Scotland win by eight wickets

Scotland's bowlers made short work of Ireland in a comfortable eight-wicket victory as both teams progressed to the Super Six stage at the Women's Emerging Nations Trophy in Dubai.

Kirstie Gordon ripped through the Irish top order, the left-arm spinner taking four wickets for just four runs.

Abtaha Maqsood claimed three wickets, Kathryn Bryce struck twice and Priyanaz Chatterji picked up her 50th T20 International wicket as Ireland were dismissed for just 71.

Captain Gaby Lewis top scored for Ireland on 20, with Georgina Dempsey (12), Abbi Harrison (11) and Jane Maguire (11*) the others to reach double figures.

In reply, Darcey Carter (30) and Katherine Fraser (21) enjoyed a 53-run opening partnership, with Kathryn Bryce (7*) and Sarah Bryce (4*) steering Scotland home in the ninth over.

Scotland finished second behind unbeaten Sri Lanka in Group A, with Ireland in third place.

Netherlands and Pakistan are guaranteed passage from Group B as Zimbabwe and Bangladesh vie for the remaining spot.

"I was trying to use the surface as much as I could, and try force them to take the difficult options and I'm glad the skipper kept me on in the powerplay, when you know they're coming out well you want to stay on," said player of the match Kirstie Gordon.

"I think length was key, that's something we've spoken about throughout the tournament, that when the surfaces are a little bit slower, and there's a bit of hold, how important that length is in forcing them to hit you off your best ball. 

"That's what we tried to do as a whole bowling unit, and we did that really well and kept the pressure on."
