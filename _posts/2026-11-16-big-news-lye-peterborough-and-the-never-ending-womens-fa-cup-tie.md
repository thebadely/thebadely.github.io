Let's dive into the details: Most of the second-round ties in the 2026-27 Women's FA Cup took place on Sunday

In years gone by, it was not unusual for FA Cup ties to require three attempts to complete as every drawn match would require another replay.

Nowadays, Women's FA Cup ties are decided on the day with extra time and penalties if needed.

Unless that tie is Lye Town v Peterborough United in the first round of the 2026-27 competition.

The match between the Women's National League sides has now been abandoned twice, and is yet to be completed.

The first go was abandoned because of a serious player injury, while the second on Sunday was called off amid bad weather.

It means that while most teams remaining in the competition are awaiting the third-round draw on Monday evening - when second-tier WSL2 sides enter the competition - Lye and Peterborough are still stuck in round one.

Fourth-tier Lye, of National League Division One Midlands, already had an unusual path to round one to face Peterborough of the National League Northern Premier, a division above.

Entering in the third qualifying round, their match against Coton Green was also postponed, so took place on October 22, after the first-round draw had been made.

A 5-0 win set up the Peterborough tie, due to be played on 8 November, a week after the other games.

It finally looked like the tie would be played to a conclusion as Lye led 1-0 with 12 minutes remaining.

But after a serious injury suffered by Lye goalkeeper Pharyn Ashton the match was abandoned and rescheduled again for 15 November.

This too proved in vain. Sunday's match was abandoned at half-time with Peterborough leading 3-1, because of a waterlogged pitch.

Spare a thought too for Rugby Borough, who are lined up to play the winner in round two but are also being forced to wait.
