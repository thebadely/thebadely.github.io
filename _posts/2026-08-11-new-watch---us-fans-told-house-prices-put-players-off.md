Cambridge United's director of football says the cost of property in the city is "an issue" when it comes to attracting players.

Mark Bonner made the comment at a fans' forum ahead of the new season.

He was responding to a question about whether the cost of living in Cambridge, one of the most expensive places to live outside London, put off some potential new signings.

The U's kick-off their League One campaign on Saturday when they host Wigan at the Cledara Abbey Stadium.
