It has been recently reported that Rafael Camara will race for Haas for a second time in Las Vegas this weekend

Brazilian Rafael Camara will race for the US-based Haas team for a second time at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The 21-year-old made his debut for Haas at the previous race, the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, alongside team-mate Oliver Bearman.

Camara, who will become a full-time driver for Haas in 2027, is replacing Frenchman Esteban Ocon, who was sacked following an altercation with team principal Ayao Komatsu at last month's Mexico City Grand Prix.

The team have not confirmed who will race alongside Bearman for the final two scheduled races of the year in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

Camara is the leader of the Formula 2 championship, which is also due to end its season at those two events.

Japanese Ryo Hirakawa, the Haas reserve driver who stepped in for Ocon in Mexico, will drive in first practice in Las Vegas in Bearman's car.

Komatsu said: "In Brazil, our 2027 recruit Rafa Camara made his debut under extremely challenging conditions and showed us and the world why we signed him for 2027.

"We're looking forward to building on that for his second race weekend with us."

Camara, who qualified 13th in Sao Paulo and finished last in demanding wet-dry conditions, said: "I'm looking forward to getting back in the car in Las Vegas.

"Brazil was a good learning experience, and now the focus is on building on that, improving step by step and making the most of every session.

"It's a new circuit for me, so there's a lot to learn, but I'll do my best to keep progressing throughout the weekend."
