As the 2026-27 season approaches, BBC Sport is set to be the home of women's football.

Three new teams have joined an expanded Women's Super League for 2026-27, and the BBC has strengthened its coverage to offer the best match action, analysis and debate.

And with the countdown to next summer's Women's World Cup in Brazil already under way, this season's storylines promise to be unmissable.

The BBC is the only place to watch free-to-air WSL football, including 25 matches live on BBC TV and iPlayer throughout the season, kicking off with Chelsea v Aston Villa on Saturday, 5 September at 12:35 BST.

This season, the debate will go further, with a new The Women's Football Fallout segment following every live BBC game. 

Fans will be treated to 15 minutes of bonus reaction and insight from the best pundits in the game, such as England legends Fara Williams and Steph Houghton.

Alongside this is the launch of BBC Women's Football YouTube channel, which will be a dedicated space for original video formats, highlights of every WSL match and the clips, features and interviews fans need to stay across this season.

Similarly on YouTube, fans can find videos from the Women's Football Weekly, as BBC Sounds' expert podcast shifts to a Monday to provide the final word on the weekend's football.

Each week, host Ben Haines and all-time Lionesses top scorer Ellen White will be joined by the some of the sport's biggest names to discuss the latest action, news and developments from the WSL, with no topic off-limits.

Over on the website and app, BBC Sport's senior women's football news reporter Emma Sanders will keep fans updated with everything they need to know, from transfer lines, to player interviews and cutting-edge analysis.

For the first time this season too, WSL supporters can follow their own club, with dedicated pages on the website and app offering a one-stop shop for the best content the BBC has to offer.

If that's not enough, there will be even more bespoke content on our TikTok and Instagram accounts dedicated to women's football.

Finally, there will be the chance to get involved in BBC Sport's new Women's Super League fan chat page, offering a community beyond the action to discuss the latest talking points and issues.

Whether it's watching live matches, catching up on highlights, listening to commentary, downloading the latest podcast or enjoying exclusive digital content, BBC Sport is the destination for women's football throughout the 2026-27 season.
