It has been recently reported that Djed Spence, Billy Gilmour, Scott McTominay and John Stones are among the British players in Serie A

BBC ALBA and BBC Sport will broadcast 38 live matches from Italy's Serie A this season.

One league match per round will be shown, beginning on Sunday with Atalanta v Sassuolo (BBC ALBA & iPlayer from 19:35 BST).

BBC ALBA will broadcast 19 live games with Gaelic and English co-commentary, while BBC Sport will stream a further 19 on BBC iPlayer with English commentary.

Former Hibernian pair Kieron Bowie and Josh Doig will be in the Sassuolo squad for the trip to Bergamo.

Napoli star Scott McTominay remains the main Scottish draw, while club-mate Billy Gilmour is returning from the knee injury that cost him a place at the World Cup and will hope to feature more prominently under new manager Massimiliano Allegri.

A Coppa Italia winner in 2025, fellow Scotland midfielder Lewis Ferguson is preparing for his fifth campaign with Bologna.

Torino striker Che Adams and Udinese teenager Lennon Miller make up the Scottish international contingent, while England defenders Djed Spence and John Stones both joined champions Inter Milan this summer.

AC Milan pair Fikayo Tomori and Ruben Loftus-Cheek have both represented England in the past, as has new Cagliari midfielder Harry Winks.

The deal adds to BBC Sport's European football portfolio which features Bundesliga and La Liga clips.
