According to new developments, Athena, real name Karenjeet Kaur Bains, is among the Gladiators who will not be returning for the new series

Five Gladiators stars will not be returning to the BBC television show for its upcoming series, it is understood. 

Athena, Comet, Bionic, Phantom and Cyclone will not be featured when the show returns in 2027.

A spokesperson for the production said the departing cast members "remain part of the wider Gladiators family" and are taking part in the ongoing arena tour, which comes to an end in December. 

Gladiators relaunched on the BBC in 2024, having originally been aired on ITV from 1992 until 2000, and then on Sky between 2008 and 2009. 

A spokesperson for Gladiators said: "We would like to thank our incredible Superhuman athletes for their contribution to the success and power of the Gladiators TV show.  

"While they will not be appearing in Series 4, they remain part of the wider Gladiators family, and you can currently see them across the remaining shows in the UK Gladiator Arena Tour 2026."

Athena, real name Karenjeet Kaur Bains, 30, is a powerlifter and former track and field athlete, while Comet, real name, Ella-Mae Rayner, 31, is a former elite-level gymnast.

Cyclone is 25-year-old Irish powerlifter Lystus Ebosele. 

Bionic is the alter ego of 30-year-old body builder Matty Campbell, while Phantom is Toby Olubi, 39, who competed for Team GB in the bobsled at the 2018 Winter Olympics. 

The upcoming series is due to introduce the first celebrity Gladiator, former England rugby union international and Celebrity Traitors star Joe Marler, who will use the name Mauler. 

In June, the BBC said the series will return in 2027, hosted by father and son duo Bradley and Barney Walsh. 

A celebrity special will also be broadcast over Christmas, featuring Big Brother presenter and I'm A Celebrity campmate AJ Odudu, TV presenter Chris Ramsey, social media star George Clarke and comedian Sara Pascoe.
