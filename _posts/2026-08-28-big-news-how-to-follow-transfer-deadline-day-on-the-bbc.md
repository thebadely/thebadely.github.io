Here’s what you need to know: This summer transfer window has come close to seeing the biggest spending by clubs ever - could it break the record by Tuesday night?

It's been a summer of big spending in the transfer window - but it's about to be slammed shut.

Premier League and EFL clubs have until 23:00 BST on Tuesday to finalise any deals, before a long wait until the window opens again in January.

This is how you can watch, listen and read up on all the last-minute action across the BBC all day.

Send us your questions

As always, expect a few late twists and turns when it comes to the final days of the summer transfer window. 

There are however plenty of players whose futures remain up in the air and could be set for a late move.

Manchester City winger Jack Grealish is of interest to former clubs Everton and Aston Villa, with the Toffees confident of re-signing Grealish on loan for a second season in a row.

A potential swap deal could also see Tottenham striker Richarlison return to Everton, with Toffees playmaker Iliman Ndiaye going the other way.

Atletico Madrid striker Julian Alvarez is of interest to Arsenal and Barcelona and could leave the Spanish capital prior to the window closing. 

Liverpool winger Cody Gakpo has been a target for both Spurs and City, while the Reds could also add in that position in the form of wingers Bradley Barcola and Ismaila Sarr.

Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernandez has also been linked with a reunion with former boss Enzo Maresca at City. Xabi Alonso's side are set to be looking at Lamine Camara to bolster their own midfield.

While the deadline closes at 23:00 BST, deals can actually be completed shortly after that deadline.

In order to do so, clubs must submit a deal sheet to the Premier League prior to 21:00 BST, allowing them an extra two hours after the deadline, with the Premier League's blessing, to complete a late move.

Once the window is closed, clubs then have to submit a squad list to the Premier League of players who will represent them.

While there is no official deadline that is made public, it is understood the deadline is about a week after the window closes.

Clubs cannot name squads of more than 25 players aged over 21. On top of that, the maximum amount of senior players allowed that are not classed as locally trained is 17.
