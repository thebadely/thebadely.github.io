Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor are among the actors thought to be taking part in final auditions to play James Bond, according to reports.

Screen tests are said to be taking place this weekend, possibly as early as Friday, according to the Hollywood Reporter, external, with between three and five actors understood to have reached the last stages of the process.

The finalists are said to be taking part in auditions with three components - stunts, camera presence and dialogue. 

Deadline reported the screen tests, external had been due take place in early October, but had been delayed by scheduling conflicts, while Variety said Turner and Lowden, external "appear to be in the mix" to replace Daniel Craig.

British actors Aaron Taylor-Johnson and James Norton were previously connected to the role, while Irish actor Paul Mescal's name had also been thrown into the mix. 

Other actors now appear to be in the frame, but reports on the current casting situation vary.

Variety suggested Slow Horses star Lowden may now be edging ahead, while Masters of the Air star Turner was speculated about earlier this year, with some fans suggesting his wife Dua Lipa could sing the Bond theme song. 

Sources told Deadline that at least four final screen test offers have been made, and while unconfirmed, "three of the four expected to test are Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor".

O'Connor is best known for playing Prince Charles in The Crown, and for starring in 2025's Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery, with Craig. 

Jack O'Connell had been thought to be in the mix, but sources said this may no longer be the case, while Euphoria and Wuthering Heights Star Jacob Elordi is not thought to be testing.

Other stars who were previously linked with the role include The King's Man star Harris Dickinson, along with SAS: Rogue Heroes' Jack Barton. 

Deadline said: "Even with the tests wrapping next week, there is no timeline for when a final decision will be made."
