Here’s what you need to know: James Botham and Reuben Morgan-Williams were part of the Wales squad that beat New Zealand

Wales flanker James Botham has been recalled by head coach Steve Tandy as one of five changes for the Nations Championship finale against Japan at Allianz Stadium on Saturday.

In the only backline alteration for the game in Twickenham, Ospreys scrum-half Reuben Morgan-Williams will make his first Wales start, with Tomos Williams named among the replacements.

Exeter lock Dafydd Jenkins will captain the side for the first time in more than two years with regular skipper Dewi Lake and flanker Jac Morgan also dropping to the bench.

Prop Rhys Carre and hooker Ryan Elias make their first starts of the November campaign, while lock Adam Beard returns at the expense of Ben Carter.

Wales have named an experienced bench that includes 294 caps.

Ospreys number nine Morgan-Williams is preparing for his first start in a position that has been dominated by Saracens scrum-half Williams during the Tandy era.

Williams has started 14 out of the 15 internationals, including the previous 11, with opportunities in short supply for others. But Williams came off early in the second half against Australia with a groin problem.

Carre, named Welsh rugby writers player of the 2025-26 season, is given his first start this autumn, with Nicky Smith dropping out of the squad.

Saracens loose-head Carre missed the first month of the season because of a hamstring injury and his only appearance of the autumn Tests so far was off the bench against Japan.

Elias, a replacement in the three previous November internationals, is handed his first Test start since the 2024 Six Nations match against France. 

Montpellier lock Beard returns after missing the loss to Australia after suffering a shoulder injury against New Zealand.

Scarlets wing Ellis Mee is given his first involvement in the campaign after being named as a replacement.

Tandy has settled on his backline with full-back Blair Murray, wing Louis Rees-Zammit, centres Eddie James and Max Llewellyn and fly-half Sam Costelow starting all four matches in the November campaign as well as prop Archie Griffin and Jenkins.

Uncapped quartet Che Hope, Macs Page, Rhys Barratt and Aneurin Owen are among the players who will not be given game time, alongside Liam Belcher, Tom Rogers and Seb Davies, while Keiron Assiratti and Jarrod Evans have been late call-ups.

Wales have won three and lost three of their six Nations Championship matches to finish fifth in the northern hemisphere group, a position matched by Japan in the southern hemisphere section.
