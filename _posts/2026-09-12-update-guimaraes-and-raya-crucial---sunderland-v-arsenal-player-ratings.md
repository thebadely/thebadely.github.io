It has been recently reported that Bruno Guimaraes scored his first goal for Arsenal

Bruno Guimaraes' thunderbolt - 121 seconds after David Raya saved a penalty - helped a sub-par Arsenal maintain their 100% start to their Premier League title defence with a 2-0 win against Sunderland.

Here's how I rated the players and you can see how you scored them below.

Arsenal's brilliance at both ends helps overcome Sunderland

What do BBC Sport's player ratings mean?

Robin Roefs: Poor distribution - two goal-kicks went straight out of play. Hardly troubled in the first half but made a fine save to tip over Kai Havertz's acrobatic effort. 6

Thomas Meunier: Offered a threat on second balls from set pieces but couldn't covert a couple of half chances. Tracked back to help defensively. 6

Nordi Mukiele: Kept Christos Tzolis quiet and provided a good outlet on break, but didn't pick out the right passes. Weak shot when right in front of goal at 1-0 down. Forced Raya into saves with two late chances but should have scored the second. 6

Dan Ballard: Defensively solid as ever and coped well with Arsenal's press. Should have scored from Xhaka free-kick, but won a penalty. 7

Reinildo Mandava: Largely contained Bukayo Saka, though was baited into fouling him and getting booked. Conceded late penalty and sent off. 4

Nilson Angulo: Lively on the wing and diligent in tracking back, but he should have run at Ben White and Ezri Konsa more. 6

Noah Sadiki: Showed willingness to get forward but also track back, but final ball lacked quality. 6
