It has been recently reported that Sione Tuipulotu, Ben White and Jack Dempsey have returned to their clubs

Captain Sione Tuipulotu, scrum-half Ben White and number eight Jack Dempsey have all been ruled out of Scotland's final Nations Championship group game at home to Japan on Saturday.

Glasgow Warriors centre Tuipulotu was a late withdrawal from Sunday's win over Australia with a knee injury.

Toulon's White picked up a knee injury during the game at Murrayfield, with Toshiba Brave Lupus' Dempsey injuring a foot.

Also returning to their clubs through injury after sitting out that game are Saracens back Fergus Burke and Glasgow locks Alex Craig and Scott Cummings, while Edinburgh prop Ollie Blyth-Lafferty has been released from the squad.

Edinburgh forwards Grant Gilchrist and Magnus Bradbury and Leicester Tigers lock Cam Henderson have, meanwhile, been added to Gregor Townsend's squad.

Ali Price trained with the squad before the Australia game and the Montpellier scrum-half retains his position.

Uncapped Josh McKay qualifies for Scotland on residency grounds this week and the back, who joined Glasgow in 2021, could make his debut against Japan at Murrayfield.

Scotland sit fourth in the Northern Hemisphere table after five matches and the weekend's set of fixtures will determine who their opponents will be when the Nations Championship Finals take place at Allianz Stadium.
