"I know Rob very, very well. I mean, I've sat next to him as he's had a colonic."

That was Romesh Ranganathan, reminding us - in case we were in any doubt - of his close friendship with Rob Beckett in the opening episode of The Celebrity Traitors.

His words sum up the key difference between this series and the regular civilian version. In Celebrity Traitors, the players aren't just on-screen competitors - many actually know each other outside the show.

Lots have worked together, and some are even friends. And those previous connections can have a huge impact on the show's dynamics.

It's also partly why it is quite so delightful to watch - as friendships unravel, and former co-stars turn on each other.

We've been unpicking the six degrees of separation. Here are some of the connections we've found - if you spot any more, we'd love to know. Get in touch with your thoughts in the comments section below.

The dynamic between Rob Beckett and Romesh Ranganathan is one of the most enjoyable to watch play out this year.

As well as being great friends in real life, they have also worked together repeatedly, including in their travel show, Rob & Romesh Vs.

They've also frequently made joint appearances on panel shows such as Mock the Week.

But their friendship could count for nothing in the castle, Ranganathan said ahead of the show.

"Rob is one of my best friends, and we've worked together for a decade now, but I still won't be able to trust it," he said.

"I mean, he's just as easily going to be on my side as he is going to twist the knife in my back given the opportunity."
