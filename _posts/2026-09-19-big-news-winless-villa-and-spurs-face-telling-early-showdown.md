More than half a billion spent between them, for just three points and one goal. Tottenham and Aston Villa are in need of Premier League inspiration.

It is not a surprise that a summer of change at both clubs has failed to bring immediate results - but they need to ignite their season.

They meet at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Saturday as the past two Europa League winners but sitting in the bottom four of the early Premier League table.

Only Villa, with 3.39, have a worse xG in the Premier League so far this season than Spurs' 3.45. Alysson's header in the 2-1 defeat by Forest was their first goal in the Premier League this term, with Spurs still searching for theirs. 

Both are still worse than bottom club Coventry, who are pointless and goalless, but have an xG of 4.21.

Villa have had the lowest number of shots in the Premier League with 33, compared to Tottenham's 51, while their six on target is also the worst in the top flight followed by Spurs' 12. 

Only Hull have had fewer touches in the box (47) than Villa's 62.

Yet Villa's defence must still be wary of struggling Spurs despite the hosts' lack of goals.

Matty Cash is available after recovering from a muscle problem but fellow defenders Pau Torres and Ian Maatsen are out for a match in which Villa can expect to face pressure. Only Manchester United, Arsenal, Brighton and Liverpool have made more passes in the final third than Tottenham (617), who have the highest successful pass completion rate, at 81.4%.

Villa's tally of 32 crosses, the lowest in the league, is half as many as Tottenham have managed.

It has been 407 minutes since Spurs last scored in the Premier League. They have failed to score in four successive Premier League matches - the first time that has happened since September 2006.

Their stalemate with Everton was their first goalless draw at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in the Premier League after 140 games. 

But despite both sides' struggle for goals, history does not point to a 0-0 draw. None of the past 22 Premier League meetings between Spurs (15 wins) and Aston Villa (seven) have been drawn.

As Villa rebuild, they might hope a match against opponents struggling for wins might be the chance to kick-start their season. But they cannot afford to be lulled into a false sense of security.
