Germany international Laura Freigang has been suspended from football for 16 months over issues with three drugs tests.

The 28-year-old will miss the Women's World Cup next summer and is banned from all football activity until after 7 October 2027.

The 16-month suspension begins from the date of notification by the German Football Association (DFB), which took place in June.

The World Anti-Doping Agency (Wada), the National Anti-Doping Agency (Nada), DFB and Freigang reached a dispute resolution agreement. 

The dispute said Entracht Frankfurt forward Freigang committed three reporting and/or testing failures between 11 March 2025 and 23 February 2026.

Freigang can return to training two months prior to the end of the suspension period but cannot play matches until after 7 October. 

All personal records of appearances, goals, assists or other statistics from 23 February 2026 are nullified, though Frankfurt's results are unaffected.

This season Freigang has scored six goals and provided two assists in 11 starts in the Frauen-Bundesliga.

Since joining the club in 2020, the striker has scored 107 goals in 187 appearances and was in the Germany squad for October's friendly victories over Australia and Japan.

In a club statement, external, Franfurt said they will "begin discussions shortly to determine the terms of her future employment relationship".

Freigang's current contract is due to expire on 30 June 2027.

Frankfurt's director of women's football Katharina Kiel said: "The agreement now reached provides clarity and planning security in a process unprecedented for Eintracht Frankfurt - for us as a club, but above all for Laura. 

"The way she and the team have handled the situation in recent months, and how Laura personally led by example with her performance on the pitch during a very difficult time, deserves the highest recognition. 

"The identified shortcomings, despite the agreement to settle the dispute, have resulted in a suspension, which means the loss of a key player."
