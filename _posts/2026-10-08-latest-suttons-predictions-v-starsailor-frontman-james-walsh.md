Experts revealed that All eyes will be on Anfield on Sunday to see how Premier League leaders Manchester City react after being found guilty of breaking the majority of the 115 financial rules they have been accused of breaching.

"It's a mammoth game," said BBC Sport football expert Chris Sutton. 

"Everyone is looking at this game to see if City's players appear deflated and how much it will affect them. 

"We don't know what the implications are for this season, either. They are top of the league at the moment but will they get a massive points deduction that changes that?"

For week six, he takes on Starsailor frontman James Walsh, who supports Liverpool.

Starsailor have released a 25th anniversary edition of their debut album, Love is Here, this week and are playing it in full, with a string ensemble, on their UK and European tour this month.

Starsailor's debut album, Love Is Here, has sold more than 1m copies worldwide since it reached number two in the UK album charts in 2001, and has amassed more than 56m streams on Spotify

When Love is Here was released, Liverpool had just completed an unprecedented treble of FA Cup, League Cup and Uefa Cup.

They are not holders of any trophies at the moment, but James is optimistic about winning some silverware under new manager Andoni Iraola.

"You have to be realistic whenever a new manager comes in, especially with the big signings we made last year, Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz, not hitting the ground running," he told BBC Sport.

"I think just give him a chance to implement his way of playing football and give Isak and Wirtz a chance to prove themselves this season.

"I don't see us winning the title but I'd be more than happy with the FA Cup or even the Carabao Cup, and having a good run in Europe.

"It's a bit greedy but of course you do like to see your team win something every season. Three more trophies like in 2001 would be pretty good!"

Chris Sutton and James Walsh were speaking to BBC Sport's Chris Bevan.

The AI predictions were generated using Microsoft Copilot Chat - we simply asked the tool to 'predict this weekend's Premier League scores'.

Emirates Stadium, 12:30 BST

Brighton were brilliant when they beat Arsenal before the international break - the Seagulls really harried and hassled the champions and went toe-to-toe with them.

I don't think Leeds will go about things the same way, but they will still provide a stern test.

Daniel Farke's side are really solid but also carry a big threat too, and they are a very different proposition to the side Arsenal thumped 5-0 at Emirates Stadium at the start of last season.

So, this game will tell us a lot about where Arsenal are and how they have recovered from that Brighton defeat.

I'd still back the Gunners to find a way to win, especially because they are at home, but it will be a lot closer than last time. Leeds don't concede goals as freely any more.

Sutton's prediction: 2-0

James' prediction: Arsenal have just had a bit of a wobble and you wonder if it was just an anomaly, because Brighton have had quite a few scalps, or if it is going to be a slightly more difficult season for them this year. 

I guess we will start to find out here but I've been impressed by how resilient Leeds have been, to stay unbeaten, so I have made this my outlier prediction to give them some credit for their start. They will probably get thumped now but someone has to give them a chance. 1-1

Despite the difference in their form, I am going to stick with Villa here, even though the Bees have been seriously impressive so far. 

Johan Manzambi is fit again and seems to have found his best form while playing for Switzerland. He could make the difference here too.

I know I keep predicting the Tractor Boys to lose but it's not a case of me just doing them over every week - I actually hope they stay up.

This is a tough game for them, however, because while Fulham are winless so far, they have deserved more than they have got.

Everything tells me that Carrick's side should probably take the three points because Tottenham are so flaky at the moment, but I am just not sure about United either.

Anfield, 16:30

I've heard people suggest that City's players will be frazzled because of what is going on with the club off the pitch, but I don't see it that way.

I suspect that, along with the City fans, there will be this siege mentality now where they think 'everyone hates us, everyone wants us to lose, everyone wants us to be relegated' - even if I don't think that is actually the case, because there are actually a lot of balanced football fans out there.

City are likely to get a hostile reception at Anfield but I think they will be able to handle it.

Liverpool are still a work in progress under Andoni Iraola too. They have not convinced me so far when I've watched them this season.

Based on all of that, I am going for City here. I can just picture Erling Haaland scoring their winner, and celebrating with a big grin on his face.

Sutton's prediction: 1-2

James on what should happen after City were found guilty of the majority of the 115 charges brought against them by the Premier League: I am not sure we should start handing out titles and cups to everyone who lost out to City during the period in question, including Liverpool. You can't really take away those memories and emotional moments from the City players and fans, and just hand them to another team, but maybe on the official record those wins should be chalked off, or at least have an asterisk next to them.

It does also mean there is a very strange feeling around this fixture and the whole season too. City and Liverpool have had lots of huge games against each other in recent years when the title has been on the line but this time we don't know what will happen to City and if this result will even matter in the end.

James' prediction: This is a strange game for Liverpool because if we win then the narrative is going to be that we have beaten the cheats. If we lose, then people are just going to say well City have had this massive and unfair advantage that has played out over all these years, not just that specific period of their charges.

So, it is going to be a hard one for all 22 players to get their heads around the backdrop and compete against each other - but I still think Liverpool will win. 3-1
