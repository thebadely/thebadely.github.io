It has been recently reported that Boxing great Manny Pacquiao (left) is leaning towards a fight in early 2027 rather than facing Joe Cordina

Joe Cordina's hopes of facing all-time great Manny Pacquiao on the undercard of December's Tyson Fury versus Anthony Joshua showdown appear to be over.

The 34-year-old former IBF super-featherweight champion wanted to face Pacquiao, 47, on home soil at the Principality Stadium on 11 December.

The Cardiff undercard is not yet complete, but so far features only heavyweight clashes as support to the all-British affair between Fury and Joshua.

Deontay Wilder vs Jai Opetaia, Da'Mazion Vanhouter vs Michael Essomba and Martin Bakole vs Solomon Adebayo are the other bouts confirmed so far.

Cordina's promoter Eddie Hearn - who has been critical of the announced undercard - says he has "lined up a big fight for Cordina in January".

Reports indicate that Pacquiao, a 12-time world champion across eight different weights, may also have settled on facing an opponent in early 2027 instead, with an official announcement expected soon.

While Cordina had agreed terms to face Pacquiao, the Filipino legend - who has not fought since July 2025 - has not reciprocated.

Hearn previously admitted that he was unsure whether the final details could be agreed.

But the promoter has been adamant in his belief that a Welsh fighter must feature on what is one of the most highly anticipated boxing cards in recent years.

"Not having a two-time world champion from Wales on the undercard is crazy," Hearn told IFL TV.

Abdullah Mason, Shakur Stevenson and Keyshawn Davis are all fighters that Hearn has name-checked as potential opponents for Cordina if the Pacquiao showdown could not be finalised.

Turki Alalshikh, the co-founder of Zuffa Boxing and a member of the Saudi Royal Court, has confirmed that the 11 December card is not yet finalised.
