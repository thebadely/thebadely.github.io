Scotland head coach Gregor Townsend has made 11 changes to his starting XV for Sunday's Nations Championship closer against Argentina at Twickenham (13:10 GMT).

Only record try-scoring wing Darcy Graham, centre Rory Hutchinson, scrum-half Jamie Dobie and lock Jonny Gray retain their places from last weekend's comprehensive victory against Japan, with Finn Russell back at fly-half and flanker Rory Darge returning from injury and captaining the side.

Blair Kinghorn, Kyle Steyn, Huw Jones and Darcy Graham return to the back line.

Rory Sutherland, Ewan Ashman and D'arcy Rae make up a new front row, Gregor Brown joins Gray in the second row and Josh Bayliss and Magnus Bradbury complete the back row with Darge.

"There's an opportunity with number eights out of our squad with injury," Townsend said. 

"Magnus played well in the summer off the bench and he's trained well. He's come [into the squad] in good condition. I'm sure he knows it's a big opportunity for him ahead of the Six Nations.

"We feel this [centre] combination is similar to Sione [Tuipulotu] and Huw [Jones] in terms of the ball-player and the running lines of Huw outside. 

"Rory hasn't had a huge amount of opportunities to play at 12 with Sione playing so many games, so it's great for him and for us to see how he goes there."

Townsend also revealed he considered playing replacement hooker Seb Stephen at number eight for this game.

"We spoke to Glasgow a few weeks ago," Townsend explained. "He came off the bench in the back row in Glasgow's last game. 

"We don't think it's right this weekend, but given the quality of our three hookers, Seb probably fits that profile more than the other two. His big strength is his ball carrying. He's obviously a very good hooker, but when we're looking at potential options for the back row, he fits that profile."

The Scots and the Pumas are competing to finish seventh in the tournament.

Townsend's Scotland have won four and lost twice so far, while Felipe Contepomi's Argentina have won twice and lost on four occasions.

Contepomi makes three personnel changes with Rodrigo Isgro taking over from Bautista Delguy on the wing and Faustino Sanchez Valarolo coming in at inside centre for Justo Piccardo, who makes the bench.

Juan Martin Scelzo moves from seven to eight with Joaquin Moro coming in at openside and Facundo Isa dropping out.

"They have changed how they've defended over the last few games," Townsend added. 

"They are much more aggressive with their line speed, hitting hard and looking to put bodies into contact. With that, there are threats and opportunities.

"They have always had a really good forward pack and they've got talented, skilful backs. We're expecting a top performance from them."

Scotland: Blair Kinghorn, Kyle Steyn, Huw Jones, Rory Hutchinson, Darcy Graham, Finn Russell, Jamie Dobie; Rory Sutherland, Ewan Ashman, D'arcy Rae, Gregor Brown, Jonny Gray, Josh Bayliss, Rory Darge (capt), Magnus Bradbury.

Replacements: Seb Stephen, Nathan McBeth, Zander Fagerson, Max Williamson, Liam McConnell, Ollie Leatherbarrow, George Horne, Tom Jordan.

Argentina: Geronimo Prisciantelli, Rodrigo Isgro, Lucio Cinti, Faustino Sanchez Valarolo, Ignacio Mendy, Tomas Albornoz, Gonzalo Garcia; Rodrigo Martinez, Julian Montoya (capt), Francisco Moreno, Guido Petti, Pedro Rubiolo, Juan Martin Gonzalez, Joaquin Moro, Juan Martin Scelzo.

Replacements: Facundo Bosch, Joel Sclavi, Tomas Rapetti, Efrain Elias, Santiago Grondona, Agustin Moyano, Justo Piccardo, Tobias Wade.
