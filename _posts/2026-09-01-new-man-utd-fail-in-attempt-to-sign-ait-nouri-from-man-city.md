Manchester United failed in a shock deadline day move to sign Manchester City full-back Rayan Ait-Nouri on loan and also missed out on Leicester City's teenage midfielder Louis Page.

United have been trying to address a perceived weakness in Michael Carrick's defence for the latter part of the transfer window.

Having opted against pursuing deals for Barcelona's Alejandro Balde and Racing Santander's Jorge Salinas, and the transfer deadline less than 12 hours away, United decided to check if a deal was possible for Algeria's Ait-Nouri.

United sources say they were not interested in a permanent deal for Ait-Nouri and were told the only acceptable outcome for City would be a loan with an obligation to buy, which they rejected.

City sources said Ait-Nouri was staying with them.

Although Ait-Nouri has been on the bench for all three Manchester City games so far, he is yet to make an appearance and is third on the list of left-backs available to Enzo Maresca, behind Josko Gvardiol and Nico O'Reilly.

In more recent times, Owen Hargreaves and, famously, Carlos Tevez joined City after their United contracts expired. City unveiled a memorable 'Welcome to Manchester' poster to mark Tevez's arrival.

With Ait-Nouri staying where he is, 19-year-old Harry Amass will not be leaving.

Amass spent most of pre-season as back-up left-back to Luke Shaw.

However, he has not been chosen in Carrick's squad for either Premier League game so far and it was expected he would take up one of the numerous offers to sign him on loan.

However, United's failure to sign an experienced left-back means Amass will stay, even though he will probably be Carrick's third choice given Noussair Mazraoui replaced Shaw during Sunday's 5-2 win against Ipswich.

In addition to missing out on Ait-Nouri, United also failed in their attempts to sign highly-rated midfielder Page from League One side Leicester.

United's interest in Page was first reported by BBC Sport in August.

However, the first sign of a problem was Page being named in Leicester's starting line-up for their League One fixture against Plymouth on Tuesday.

It was then confirmed by sources the deal will not be concluded and Page will remain a Leicester player.
