It has been recently reported that Harry Kane scored twice on his 125th appearance for England - but who else impressed?

Harry Kane scored twice as England cruised to a comfortable 3-0 win over the Czech Republic in the Nations League at Wembley on Tuesday.

Here's how I rated the players - and you can see how you scored them below.

What do BBC Sport's player ratings mean?

Jordan Pickford: Will return to Everton knowing his place is now under threat from James Trafford. Some shaky moments. 6

Trent Alexander-Arnold: Given another opportunity to impress and took it. Ability on the ball perfect for deep-lying opponents like the Czech Republic. 7

Jarell Quansah: Deployed in his favoured centre-back role and looked comfortable. Slowly becoming a fixture in the squad. 6

Marc Guehi: But for a couple of lapses, cruised through the game. Started all four of England's matches during the extended international break. 6

Lewis Hall: Now in a fight with Nico O'Reilly to be England's first-choice left-back. A successful international period for the Newcastle defender. 7

Elliot Anderson: Goes from strength to strength at international level. Brilliant pass to unlock the Czech Republic for England's second. 6

Myles Lewis-Skelly: Can be happy with his output on his return to the international scene. Will be unlucky not to be in next squad on this evidence. 7

Eberechi Eze: Improved after an underwhelming first-half but did not do enough to cause Thomas Tuchel a selection headache after being handed a rare start. 6

Jude Bellingham: Did not hit the heights of his captivating display in Croatia but still oozed class whenever in possession. 7

Morgan Rogers: Showed his versatility by starting out on the right and looked comfortable in the role. Provided a real threat. 7

Harry Kane: Historic night for the England captain becoming the country's joint-highest appearance-maker alongside Peter Shilton with 125 caps. Goes without saying he scored... twice. 8
