A video showing a US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agent pointing his gun at a woman has been viewed by millions after she shared the moment on Instagram.

Carolina Molina says she was out delivering business cards in Falls Church, Virginia, when she saw ICE agents, who then surrounded her car.

The US Department of Homeland Security said she "attempted to harm officers by weaponising her vehicle". Molina denies this.

BBC Verify assesses the dashcam footage shared by Molina. Jake Horton reports.
