It has been recently reported that After more than 10 years, Amelia Dimoldenberg announced Chicken Shop Date was shutting up shop

When someone says "we need to talk", it often means one thing - it's time to break up. 

After Amelia Dimoldenberg announced on Thursday her hit YouTube series Chicken Shop Date would be turning off the fryers after one last series, some fans were begging for a second chance, while others agreed it was time to move on. 

Over more than 10 years and 100 dates, Dimoldenberg's awkward flirting, deadpan silences and willingness to overshare has made her channel an unmissable stop for any celeb, single or not. 

Before one last slurp on an orange soda, BBC Newsbeat looks back at some of the talk show's most iconic moments. 

Daniel Kaluuya later went on to win an Oscar and was one of Dimoldenberg's breakthrough guests

Chicken Shop Date grew out of a youth club project and interviews Dimoldenberg conducted with south London grime artists as a teenager for The Cut. 

She's spoken about how hard she had to work in the early days to persuade guests to come on the channel. 

Nearly five years into her series, in 2020, she bagged her first big name outside the rap scene: Oscar-nominated actor Daniel Kaluuya. 

During the date, Dimoldenberg admitted she would turn around when fans shouted "chicken" at her in the street.

Her interview with the Get Out star pulled in five million views - a fraction of the audience she'd go on to attract, but evidence her hard work was clearly starting to pay off.

It laid the foundations for even bigger names to join Dimoldenberg, with Sir Paul McCartney, Lando Norris and Shania Twain among those placing orders.  

"Did you just call me Phoebe?" became one of the show's most viral moments 

Forgetting your date's name can be a guarantee that there won't be a second one. But in Dimoldenberg's case it created one of Chicken Shop Date's most memorable moments.

During a 2023 date, she discussed marriage proposals with Jennifer Lawrence.

When Dimoldenberg said she wouldn't know how it felt to be engaged, the Hunger Games star looked the host in the eye, and began:

"Phoebe."

"Did you just call me Phoebe?" Dimoldenberg responded.

"Yeah."

"Amelia."

The pair burst out laughing, but it wasn't the only time her date was caught thinking about someone else.  

Pop star Sabrina Carpenter, who appeared on Chicken Shop Date in 2024, didn't forget the host's name, but did confuse the show with Hot Ones - the YouTube series where celebrities eat progressively spicy chicken wings.

In a sign of her own rising stardom, Dimoldenberg took on the chilli challenge in the same year.

Fans were convinced by the on-screen chemistry between Dimoldenberg and Andrew Garfield 

The spice in this series was reserved for the guests - some more than others.

After a couple of red carpet run-ins where there seemed to be palpable chemistry, actor Andrew Garfield's appearance on Dimoldenberg's show set the internet alight with speculation of genuine romance. 

When he appeared on Chicken Shop Date, Garfield conceded it had been "vibey" between them for a while, although for some viewers the encounter didn't live up to expectations.

The following year, Dimoldenberg insisted the pair were just friends. 

Garfield wasn't the only guest to seem to really hit it off with the host. Singer Billie Eilish was especially flirty, giggling after complimenting Dimoldenberg's body. 

And after rapper Aitch came on the channel, rumours circulated it had kindled a real-life relationship, although neither ever confirmed this. 

Dimoldenberg has collaborated with documentary maker Theroux multiple times since their date

It was the clash of two interviewing titans - the master of the strategic pause and the queen of the awkward silence.

But when Louis Theroux met Dimoldenberg the big takeaway wasn't a new, personal revelation. It was a viral rap.

During the pair's 2022 date, the Chicken Shop Date host reminded the documentary maker of the time he spat some bars on his BBC series Weird Weekends, when his quest to become "the first white, middle-class gangsta rapper" took him to New Orleans in the US.

Despite the 20-year gap, Theroux was able to recall an impressive amount of Jiggle Jiggle, his rap about his love of red wine and the struggles facing the very tall owner of a compact car.

Music producers Duke & Jones added autotune to a TikTok clip of the moment, turning it into a viral sensation that's had almost 84 million views since it was uploaded. 

Theroux eventually teamed up with the duo to record a new version of the song.

"When I was 17, I thought that I might meet the love of my life on a date in a chicken shop," she wrote in her farewell statement. "Since then, I've spent my entire twenties sat across from the most incredible people, people I'd always dreamt of meeting. 

"All of them charmed me (some more than others), and I definitely charmed them."

With one final series in the pipeline, there's still hope for some rekindled connections.
