New Exeter head coach Oli Bishop feels his side will be title contenders this season in Premiership Women's Rugby.

Bishop was promoted to lead the Chiefs over the summer after Steve Salvin left to become forwards coach with Wales.

The former Ealing and Wasps coach has spent 13 years coaching in the top flight and takes over an Exeter side that he helped finish third last season before a late 40-38 loss at eventual champions Saracens in the play-off semi-finals.

"The girls are in a good place," he told BBC South West.

"We're not changing huge amounts of things, our DNA is our DNA.

"We've looked to bring in an extra coach, this is the first time Chiefs have had three full-time coaches and I think that's going to make a huge difference to what we can do and the detail we can go to.

"So I would say that we're going to be contenders, but I'm also going to say I think this is probably the most competitive season that we're going to see out there this year."

Oli Bishop took over as Exeter head coach over the summer

Exeter have had to wait an extra week to begin their campaign in the nine-team PWR.  

They start the season with a tough trip to a Bristol side that ran in eight tries in a 48-21 win at Harlequins last week and have brought players including England star Ellie Kildunne and Wales flanker Bethan Lewis.  

Exeter have kept most of the squad that got them to the final four in June as they prepare to return to league action after the autumn's WXV matches.

"A big thing for us is continuity and cohesion," Bishop added.

"We're really happy with the squad and staff that we have and that's why we kept as many as we could at the end of the season.

"So we'll be looking to build, we'll be looking to try and do things slightly differently, but again keep our DNA and keep our identity how we want that to be.

"Me and Steve worked a lot last year on the culture and identity of this place and how we would change things from the previous way it was being run.

"I was 100% on board with that, I've made a couple of tweaks here and there, but they're small changes.

"I'm wanting the girls to go out on the weekend and be brave, take the opportunities that are there and be able to express themselves with a clear understanding of how our DNA influences the way that we play."
