According to new developments, Four men have been found guilty of sexually abusing a woman whose husband drugged and secretly advertised her for sex on the internet. 

Philip Young, 50, already pleaded guilty to multiple charges of drugging and sexually abusing his wife, Joanne Young, over a 13-year period at their home in Swindon, Wiltshire.

At Winchester Crown Court earlier, Norman Macksoni, 48, Richard Wilkins, 62, Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, and Mohammed Hassan, 38, were found guilty by a jury of sexual offences.

Wilkins and Macksoni were both found guilty of one count of rape.

Connor Sanderson-Doyle was found guilty of one count of sexual assault by touching and one count of sexual assault by penetration.

Hassan was found guilty of one count of sexual assault by touching.
