The comedian and author Bob Mortimer returns to his hometown to discuss his new novel 'Michael', in front of a full house at Middlesbrough Town Hall.

The book is based on the events of his childhood, and he shows BBC Media and Arts correspondent David Sillito the areas of Middlesbrough which most bring back memories.

He reflects on the difficulties his mother had raising him and his three brothers, after his father died at a young age.

Batting away suggestions he's a national treasure, he jokes that he's a "specialist interest".
