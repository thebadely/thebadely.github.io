Dragons are in advanced talks with head coach Filo Tiatia about a contract extension.

The former All Blacks forward joined the Rodney Parade club in the summer of 2024 as defence coach.

He was promoted as interim boss after the departure of Dai Flanagan the following November and was appointed on a permanent basis in February 2025.

Tiatia's deal expires at the end of the 2026-27 campaign and Dragons are keen to retain his services.

"We'd love to lock Filo in and back him for a long-term future at the Dragons," said chief executive Rhys Blumberg.

"He has definitely got an appetite to stay and we have an appetite to keep him. We will work through that over the next month or so to get something done."
