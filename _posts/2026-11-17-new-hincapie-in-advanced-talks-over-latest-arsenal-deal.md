Arsenal are in advanced talks with defender Piero Hincapie over a new contract.

Hincapie, 24, joined the Gunners last summer from Bayer Leverkusen, initially on loan, with the Gunners activating an option to make the move permanent at the end of the season.

The Ecuador international has established himself as a key member of Mikel Arteta's squad, making 47 appearances in all competitions.

The versatile defender's existing deal still has over four years left to run - but he is now set to sign a 12-month extension on improved terms.

Elsewhere, the club intend to open talks with left-back Riccardo Calafiori, 24, over renewing his deal.

The Italy international has become a crucial part of Arteta's plans since his arrival from Bologna in 2024.

His current deal expires in the summer of 2029, but it is expected talks over an extension will begin in the not too distant future.

Arsenal are on a contract renewal drive, with the club set to confirm new deals for Jurrien Timber, Declan Rice and goalkeeper David Raya.
