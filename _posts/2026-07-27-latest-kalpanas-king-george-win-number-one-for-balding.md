British trainer Andrew Balding labelled his King George VI & Queen Elizabeth stakes race victory with Kalpana at Royal Ascot as "number one" of the 2,000 career winners he has registered.

After finishing second 12 months prior, Kalpana held off a surge by last year's winner Calandagan to win by one and a half lengths on Saturday.

The Kingsclere trainer was in no doubt where the win ranked in his illustrious 25 years of training.

"This is probably number one and It is an emotional win for lots of reasons," Balding told BBC Radio Berkshire.

"She [Kalpana] is a high-class filly and deserved her moment to win a race like this, it's a big deal as she's been with us a long time and everyone is so fond of her.

"We actually thought the field this year was stronger than last year, so I'd have probably settled for third."

The filly beat off the best from around the world over a mile and a half with the win coming six months after the death of Balding's father, legendary trainer Ian Balding.
