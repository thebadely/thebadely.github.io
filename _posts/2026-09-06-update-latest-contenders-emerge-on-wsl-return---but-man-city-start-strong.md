Chelsea drop points against Aston Villa for the first time in WSL

New season, same flaw - Chelsea title hopes already under scrutiny

Manchester City did what Chelsea could not as Sonia Bompastor's side were held to a frustrating 1-1 draw by Aston Villa at Stamford Bridge. 

Despite 57 touches in the opposition box, 23 shots inside the area, an effort that hit the crossbar and a disallowed goal for offside, Chelsea could not break Villa down.

Their lack of ruthlessness was a consistent characteristic last season as Chelsea, who had won the previous six WSL titles, fell to third in the table.

Their opening display did little to ease concerns it would be a similar story this season, while City showed they remain a serious force to overcome.

Elsewhere, the bookies' favourites to win the league, Arsenal, did not find life easy at Brighton for their opening game. 

Arturo Ruiz, 29, took charge of his first match for Albion - a side many think can challenge the WSL's giants - and they made life tough for the Gunners.

A moment of quality from 19-year-old Lisa Baum on her Arsenal debut won the points in a 1-0 victory and, while unconvincing, it remains early days.

Last season's Champions League quarter-finalists Manchester United did not get off to a good start under manager Eva Olid as they were beaten by a surprisingly fluid display from opponents London City Lionesses on Friday.

The team of superstars, boosted by new signings such as two-time Ballon d'Or winner Alexia Putellas and Spain defender Mapi Leon, lived up to the hype on the opening night to pick up a well-earned 2-1 win.

They had 60% possession and 11 shots - not bad for a team who have barely got to know each other during a high-turnover summer. 

While they clearly have bags of talent, there was scepticism at how quickly London City would gel, but on Friday night's showing they could be one of this season's challengers for a Women's Champions League qualification spot.

Another contender will be Tottenham.

Martin Ho's side laid strong foundations last season in finishing fifth and they have started this campaign strongly with a 3-1 win over West Ham.

Debutant Kirsty Hanson, who they snatched from Aston Villa after she finished as their top-scorer in 2025-26, hit the ground running with two goals in London.

Perhaps the surprise package of the weekend was Liverpool, who have been busy in the transfer window and looked very strong against newly-promoted Charlton.

Tougher challenges will come their way but Gareth Taylor's side oozed confidence and new signing Vivien Endemann was a standout performer in a 4-0 drubbing.

It took them until January last season to pick up a victory as they battled at the bottom of the table - so they have already laid a marker. 

"It feels very, very good. We had a clean sheet, scored four goals and won the first match," goalscorer Endemann told Sky Sports.

"Last season, it was really tough, so now to start with a win is very important for us. We want to keep going."

Meanwhile, Crystal Palace's 1-0 victory over Everton proved there is hope for the promoted sides in the expanded 14-team league, despite those losses for Birmingham and Charlton.

Jo Potter's Palace edged in front in the first half thanks to Lexi Lloyd-Smith's finish and held off Everton, whose only shot on target came in stoppage time.

"No words can explain. I'm buzzing. To get to do this in front of my friends and family, it was an unbelievable feeling," said Lloyd-Smith.

"We knew it was going to be hard, we knew we were going into these games as underdogs. It's so good that in the first game we show we're here to put out a statement. 

"I feel like we've got a lot to give."
