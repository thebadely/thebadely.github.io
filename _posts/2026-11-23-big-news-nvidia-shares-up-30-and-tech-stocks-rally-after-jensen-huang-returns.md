According to new developments, Nvidia shares have opened up 30% after the tech firm reinstated Jensen Huang as its chief executive with immediate effect.

His shock reinstatement comes less than two weeks after Huang resigned after admitting an affair with a woman who was alleged to be a Chinese spy, a period in which shares in the chipmaker have also fallen significantly.

The jump in Nvidia shares - which lifted its market value by about $1 trillion -  has also lifted stock markets, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq up 6.5% in early trading.

Tech stocks more broadly have also rallied following large falls on Friday, with much of the rally starting in pre-market trading before Huang's return was announced.

Microsoft and Amazon shares were both up nearly 4% in early morning trading, while other major tech firms including Apple saw more modest gains, up less than 2%.

Other chipmakers, however, experienced large increases. Micron Technology jumped 19%, Intel shares climbed 14% and AMD was up nearly 7%.

Nvidia is a key firm at the core of the artificial intelligence (AI) boom, with the chips it makes central to the industry.

Kathleen Brooks, research director at brokerage firm XTB, said Huang's return showed Huang was the "godfather" of AI, and "has a talismanic influence over the sector".

"Barely two weeks ago this man was ousted from the company that he founded due to serious misconduct and potential national security issues," she said.

"Even though Huang did not hold special voting shares on the board, Nvidia's directors cannot see a future for the company without him."

Nvidia's board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate the firm's co-founder following "a series of discussions and meetings" with him, the company said.

While shares in Nvidia bounced on Monday, it comes after a fall of nearly 14% on Friday alone, following Reuters reports - unconfirmed by the BBC - that the company was being investigated by the US Securities and Exchange Commission over its accounting practices.
