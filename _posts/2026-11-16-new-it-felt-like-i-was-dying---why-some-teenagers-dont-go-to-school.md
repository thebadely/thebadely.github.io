Thousands of school pupils in England miss more than half the academic year, which experts say puts them more at risk of becoming Neet - not in employment, education or training.

A government-commissioned review into how to solve the problem of nearly one million young people being Neet is due to be published on Tuesday.

Two teenagers who experience school anxiety have spoken to the BBC about what led them to stop attending classes.

Mali, 18, is autistic and stopped going to school three years ago after experiencing panic attacks. She said leaving her bedroom would make her legs go numb.

"It felt like I was dying," she told the BBC.

Thirteen-year-old Eva, who is also autistic, stopped going to school after struggling with the transition to secondary education.

"It's like a physical barrier that you can't cross and you can't move," she said.
